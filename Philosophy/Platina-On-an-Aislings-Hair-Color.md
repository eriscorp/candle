# On an Aisling's Hair Color

_by Platina in **Dark Ages**_

_(an indepth look at how hair color and personality are connected)_

One day whilst walking among the flowers of Shinewood, I came upon a fae who
greeted me most kindly. Upon noticing my hair, reddened in color by a most
recent dyeing, she keenly observed, though I didn't know how, that I was quite
an adventurous person and quite passionate in all that I did. This being true,
I asked her what magic she had used to divine such things about me. She merely
laughed and said "Twas no magic, but the choice you made in the coloring of
your hair revealed your heart to me." Intrigued, I asked her to explain more,
which she did happily

I learned that the fae believed that a person's hair color could reveal what
they were really like--their personality, attitudes, beliefs. However, not all
Aislings are sparked with a color of hair that matches the person they would
come to be. Since hair dyes had been introduced into Temuair, Aislings could
color their hair however they liked. It would be wise, though, the fae
explained, if Aislings chose a color that best suited them, or the magical
creatures of the land and those Aislings and Mundanes learned in the ways of
color, might mistake their true nature. The fae explained to me what the color
of one's hair expressed to those who held this knowledge. She then asked if I
would share this with other Aislings, so they could best choose the color for
their hair that suited them, so that their true nature could be properly
expressed.

This is the information the fae told me about hair colors. First, many colors
can be grouped under one category, such as the color "strawberry" and the
color "auburn" are both classified as "reds" (or "ruadh" in the fae tongue).
Therefore, they both have the same fundamental characteristics, but the
differences in the darkness or lightness of the color can also add meaning.

## The Reds (_ruadh_)

_(from left to right: Strawberry, Auburn)_

Red is a color that is associated with passion--not just in the romantic
sense, but in any area--battle, politics, justice, etcetera. It is a good
color choice if you are full of energy and strive to accomplish whatever you
set out to do. Those with red hair are also seen as being "fiery", meaning
they might have a bit of a temper or attitude towards others. If you indeed
are quite vocal or boisterous, strawberry would be the best choice as it is a
darker red that stands out, like fire against the night sky. If you feel you a
little more subdued, auburn would be a good choice as the color is more toned
down.

## The Blues (_gorrym_)

_(from left to right: Cyan, Blueberry, Night, Dark Blue, Royal Blue,
Turquoise, Sky Blue, Ocean Blue)_

Blue is a color that invokes calmness, peace and release from boundaries and
restrictions. If you are someone who is calm and rational, who wants to keep
peace among your fellow Aislings, then blue is a good color for your hair.
Lighter blues are best for people who feel like a wanderer at heart, who want
to follow their dreams wherever wind or water may take them. Darker blues are
more for those who feel most calm and serene when they are by themselves.
However, a darker blue may also reveal that you carry some sadness deep in
your heart.

## The Yellows (_buidhe_)

_(from left to right: Gold, Platinum, Dirty Blonde)_

Yellow is a color that reminds us of the sun and all the brightness and warmth
it brings. If you are someone who is cheerful and energetic, always feeling
happy and ready to take on the world, yellow is a good hair color choice. If
you are very talkative or playful, a lighter yellow color choice is best. If
you are more reserved, choose a darker shade.

## The Greens (_ghlass_)

_(from left to right: Mustard Seed, Evergreen, Light Green, Lime Green, Jade
Green)_

Green is a color that reminds us of nature. Those who believe in the beauty of
nature and all that which dwells in nature, green is a good hair color choice.
Deeper shades of green invoke the sense that a person believes in harmony and
peace, a desire for beauty and health. Lighter shades of green are best for
those who also feel the need to be noticed by others and in contact with many
people.

## The Browns (_dhone_)

_(from left to right: Brown, Tan, Light Brown)_

Brown is a color that invokes simplicity, yet strength. Those who put family
and friends above all would do well to choose brown as their hair color.
Darker shades are best for those who feel happiest at home, doing things they
know well with the people they know best. Lighter shades are best for those
who are more sociable with people outside of their own circle.

## The Purples (_purpaidh_)

_(from left to right: Lavender, Violet, Purple)_

Purple is a color that reminds of us magic and regality. Those who feel deep
connections to the mysteries of magic should choose purple for their hair,
especially lighter shades that invoke a sense of mysticism. For those who
think highly of themselves, or strive to live a rich and full life, are best
in choosing a darker shade.

## The Pinks (_bane-yiarg_)

_(from left to right: Pink, Rose Pink, Blush)_

Pink is a color that invokes the sense of cuteness, innocence, sweetness.
Darker shades of pink tend to include a tendancy towards mischeviousness or
playfullness, while lighter shades are for those who feel or act young at
heart.

## The Oranges (_oranje_)

_(from left to right: Copper, Orange, Dark Orange)_

Orange is a color of warmth, celebration and enjoyment of life. Those who
cherish life and all the things in it, especially the pleasures of life,
should choose orange as their hair color. A darker shade is for those who are
more fiercly independent while a lighter shade indicates a person is happier
with someone nearby.

## Other Colors (_dubh, glas, kione, argid_)

_(from left to right: Black, Grey, White, Silver)_

Black is a powerful hair color for one to choose. It can mean many
things--from being someone who is a loner, to someone who is deeply involved
with magic, even to someone who wants to be enveloped by the darkness. It is a
good choice if you are someone who believes deeply in your own abilities and
talents. However, a word of caution iff you choose this color, your actions
will be carefully watched to see what lies beneath the color.

White is a hair color that speaks of both innocence and wisdom, someone who
has learned much but is untouched but the harsh truths of the world. People
who are pure in heart and kind to those around them should choose this color.

The colors of grey and silver for the hair express that one is cool and
reserved. It gives the indicitation that one is quite refined and oftend
reserved in nature. Grey is a better choice for those who keep to themselves
or feel the need to always be formal. Silver is for those who are less subdued
in these respects.

_Written by Lady Platina Valkari, from the words of a kind fae in Shinewood._

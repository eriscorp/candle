# Untitled

_by Serendepity in **Dark Ages**_

So you found your spark! Great job. I imagine you are ready to find a guide to
show guide you down your predestined path. But what after that, you may ask.
Well, it is time to get your armors and spells/skills. In order to do this you
need money right? I imagine you would much rather have the pride from doing it
yourself rather than walking around begging others, only to be confronted with
rejection. Well, if you were wise then, you should have some money from
killing those floppies. With this, get either your first spell or a weapon.

Now it is time to venture into the depths of the crypts! Not only can you gain
experience here, you can also find many creature drops that sell for a fair
amount of coins. This is the way to getting any future items or spells you may
need. There are a number of things that you can sell to Dar, the mileth
wizard, for a fair price. Spider's eyes are probably the most common and
easiest thing to collect and sell. There are tons of them laying around if you
ever need money quick. You dont even need to kill the spiders! Centipede's
Glands are also another common and good selling item. In addition Dar also has
need of these items for himself and will award you 5 thousand exp for getting
them for him.

Here are a few prices for Mileth Crypt items:

- Rotten apple-25 sold at Cian the alchemist
- Rotten Grapes-50 sold at Cian
- Mold-50 Sold at Cian
- Rotten Tomatoe-100 Sold at Cian
- Spider's eye- 500 gold sold to Dar(just 10 eyes and you have 5k)
- Centipedes gland-1,100 Sold to Dar
- Bat's Wings-1,250 Sold at Dar

Here are some prices on Enchanted garden items:

- Raw Wax-50 gold Sold to Devlin-the white witch of Mileth
- Viper Gland-137 gold Sold to Cian the alchemist, right next to Dar
- Mantis eye-1,500 sold to Cian
- Bee's Sting-400 gold Sold to Dar

As a sidenote- Rouges can sell things like wolfs skin, wolfs teeth, goblin
skulls,Hobgoblin skulls, and a few other items at the black market in
Rucession

This should get you on your way! As you become stronger, and you start
venturing deeper into the crypts or East Woodlands, you will see piles of
money left behind after the kill. When hunting in groups, pooling is the
considerate thing to do. This way everyone gets an equal share of the money.
Because they also will need money for thier spells and items!

Now that you are equipped with this knowledge and a desire to become a Master,
get out there and enjoy!!

# Srad

_by Ignious in **Dark Ages**_

I feel you in my heart,  

the greatest burning fire.  
From me you shall not part,  
I fear to lose desire.  

Thou fury shant I test,  
for alone I could not best.  

Your name is glory clad,  
known to us as Srad.  

You keep us all so warm,  
within the frosty night.  
We keep you out of storm,  
lest you shed no light.  

For your pain there be no cure,  
though you may be power pure.  
And only time will tell,  
if you ever shall ever be well.  

But if you should meet a stronger foe,  
deliver it will that fatal blow.  
Come out on top for certain it shall,  
mocking repeats of your conqueror, Sal.  

Your colours are deep,  
and with life you leap.  
You keep me so hearty,  
and bring light to a party.  

You throw out your heat,  

where I keep warm my feet.  
And with your great flame,  
you cook up my game.  

You toss up a blaze,  
in your powerful craze.  
And all you shall burn,  
without simplest concern.  

You guide me on torch,  
and the wood you shall scorch.  
And as I explore through floors of Nobis,  

dissappear shadows from your sweet warm kiss.  

As you draw to your close,  
you do not ever impose.  
Without a big flash,  
you burn to an ash.  

_Ignious Dra'lyenn_  
_Poeta de Incendia_

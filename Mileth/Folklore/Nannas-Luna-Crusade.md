# Luna Crusade

_by Nannas in **Dark Ages**_

Born in this Deoch as it falls apart,  
Let us show the World Our Reason for no departure. We as children of the night dancing in the rain Of sorrows and no light in the Deepest of Nights For death does not scare us, For we are Immortal  
, we have taken flight. We Of The Luna Crusade march into the Night Sky  
With our hands held high to the temuair gods blessing our steps we take unde the Gliocan enchanted Moon of it's beauty.  
Hear our Cry in the Darkest of Nights For you shall see us as we Pray, Hear Us as we charge into battle  
, Hear our whispers under the Moons graceful light. For the Crowns upon our heads made of the moonlight that blesses us and guides us threw  
We of The Luna Crusade rise with the Pride of Ancients gone before us.  
We Shall not run from Any Challenge  
For we would fight until Our Dying Breath  
, Only to be Brought back to Life by the Moonlit water of the oren fountains.  
We March Forth with Our Flags held high in the Night sky  
Like the Armies of Old, we march forth From Land to Land  
, From the Snowy Peaks of Mount Giragan, to  
The Deep Forest of Shinewood,  
From the Deep cold Walls of Chaos To the Plains of Asilon,  
From the Ancient Veltain Mines to the Undiscovered Lost Ruins.  
For Our march is the march of the brave lead by light so magical and bright.  
We shall continue to Pursue The love needed in temuair with The Moonlight behind us,  
We Of The Luna Crusade Shall Show No Mercy Upon Our Enemies and embrace our loved ones.  
Let us Go forth and Show our Enemies Why We Never Die.  
Even if we are Doomed to fall Our Flag shall stand tall.  
For only when we are Forgotten shall we die,  
May The Light of the Moon Bless us in this Unforgiving night.  
A forgotten family  
Born In This World As It begains to fall apart.  
We Kneel before the Altar of the Night's Sky  
And Take up the Mantle for The Pride of the Crusade.  
Let us show the World Our Reason for Living, and Our Heart's desires. So  
Children of the Night Dancing on with spirits shining bright join us. May The Light of the Moon Bless us in this Unforgiving night  
Until we are forgotten,  
For We Are The Crusade of the Moon, we Are The Luna Crusade.

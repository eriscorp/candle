# A Harsh Farewell

_by Elyon in **Dark Ages**_

I sometimes wonder if you even care  
When you are leaving all of us behind  
It wasn't 'til now which is when you shared  
Did you believe that we all would not mind?  

Perhaps you had no clue until right now  
But does not allow us feeling fear  
THe rest of us are clearly wondering how  
There'd be no malice if you'd hold us dear  

And what is this? You're gone, to leave us all?  
We never said goodbye, you left no trace..  
You wanted space and now you've got no wall  
I don't think I can't notice your own face  

Regardless, I will always think of you  
Forget me not, for I'll remember too.  

***

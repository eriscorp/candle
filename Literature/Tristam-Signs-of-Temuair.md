# Signs of Temuair

_by Tristam in **Dark Ages**_

This is not the way a kingdom is built, this is how it is destroyed  
the way we laugh, the way we play, a ploy for girls and boys.  
A plague, a plague, a plague, a curse, the page, the book, a day.  
We paid amounts of disrespect, to whom it is at bey.

* * *

Carefully the doors are closed, and windows shut to seal.  
The gates of which we cannot see, but somehow always feel.

* * *

Do you feel the ignorance, and bliss between us all?  
between us all a single word exchanged when darkness calls.

* * *

Fancy words they are I say, and something isn't right.  
The negligence of gluttony has always been our fight.

* * *

Constantly chasing the chapters of chadul, but Danaan doesn't wake.  
Await we do the terror's day, and fear until it breaks.

* * *

I held my heart in my own hand, and watched it bleed for moons  
something else must happen here, something must resume.

* * *

The vague old stench of goblins blood, stained so deep it burns  
but will we all now recognize, until all aislings learn?

* * *

Peace be with those who haven't fault, or claim they weren't there  
they cannot be judged just for the past, as if we even care.

* * *

So carry on, ofcourse I must, a portrait made of me  
I look in books, and see myself, as if they were the sea.

* * *

This is not the way a kingdom is built, this is how it is destroyed  
The parchment scrolls and sands of time skip beating hearts of joy.  
A kiss, a kiss, a kiss, a charm, the bridge, the fire, a bond.  
we have memories of darker days, of which we aren't fond.

* * *

Young hearts do not ache long, and soon all wounds are healed  
the scars we bear of innocence, are in a sense a shield.

* * *

The path will take it's course I fear, no bread crumbs left to find  
We mark our ways and leave a scent of parody behind.

* * *

Complete the quest, and earn your mark, demerit of the truth  
but who is who to tell me why demerit is of use.

* * *

Slumber, deep, sleep can be turned into a cell, a cage.  
Awake you will from dreams you earned, and yell into a rage.

* * *

Common sense, and common law abide it and be strong  
remember without guidelines, aisling, you will not live long.

* * *

The cold wind blows, I take a breath, and blow it out my nose  
I feel the forest beckoning, awakening the rose.

* * *

Arise, the fall appears to come, and summer is the past  
but days and nights are always there, and nothing ever lasts.

* * *

So laugh again, and play the game of life we mussn't win  
before you quit, and take your leave, learn first how to begin.

* * *

This is not the way a kingdom is built, this is how it is destroyed  
The children search for mysteries, their parents say avoid.  
A sign, a sign, a sign, the light, the patience to pretend  
now we begin a legacy, by starting at the end.

* * *

Concentrate and take the vow, take your place, or take a bow  
for everything and everyone is wrong.

* * *

A constant rate, and pace of clouds, chase your fate, or face it now  
may this be said, we've been here all along.

_Tristam Hallowfield_

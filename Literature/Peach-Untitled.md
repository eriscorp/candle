# Untitled

_by Peach in **Dark Ages**_

> _"Good things come from the  
> transformation of strange things.  
> It is a mystery of alchemy."_  
> \- _Cian, Alchemist of Mileth_

***

Some aislings are careful, they hold a strong skepticism  
of phenomenal colors being held in vials and flasks like liqueur,  
they do not understand the intrigue of what those liquids hold in their prism.  
Vivid golden mixed slowly with a pulsing sanguine in a condenser.  
Withered verdants extracted from milky blues in an apparatus.  
Bleached whites and bursting violets glow alone on a glass lattice.  
Cian, you are faithful to your experiments, a trade you claim that is both  
life consuming and engrossing. You say it isn't a heartless trade  
but much of your life has been spent with the mold and fermenting growth.  
Your equipment is your family and your supplies are about to fade.  
You ask aislings for a bit of that and a little bit of this, a whole rotten lot-  
fruits and vegetables barely recognizable; you say the mice cause some awful rot.  
Only the young aislings venture to your shop anymore; for your blue powders or three potions.  
Do you wish for new concoctions to be brewed by your hands?  
I can do the fieldwork; you just sit tight and wait for me while I cross over the oceans  
on a ship to Medenia. For your love of alchemy and knowledge, I will explore those far away lands.  
I will search through the towns of Asilon, Noam and Hwarone and learn all I can  
and report back to you with a new alchemic plan!  

***

Cian, have you heard of tonics before?  
They come from Noam, from a dangerous and distant  
ant colony rising from the ground called Andor.  
While hunting, I found a blue one on an ant  
and took it for inspiration to show to you.  
Do you wonder how these insects brew?  
I said it before, your knowledge of alchemy only stretches  
as far as your homeland Mileth- I could sense your frustration  
of lack of new learning and hopefully, my friend, if your interest catches,  
you too can conjure your own tonic creation!  
I hypothesize, in Andor, the boros help collect mold and spores  
and bring them to inner ant workings on lower floors.  
Fascinated, I prayed to Fiosachd to stay hidden  
and followed the tunnels and searched to learn ant alchemy.  
As I spied deep within their musty indigo stained den;  
I saw an ant guard rush with a fresh tonic in grasp by me,  
abruptly I looked to the hole where he exited from  
and I ran to it, peered in and I heard a soft hum.  
Pinholes in the colony walls let in yellow light; ants were mixing vats of lucid bright colors.  
Boros dropped off bushels of spores, ants were grinding up dusts with rock tools and a stirrer.  
Smaller ants were polishing blown glass bottles to shine like mirrors.  
The ants were melodically humming in clicks to each other,  
I inched closer to the bubbling incandescent work of alchemy  
and realized the ant I stood closest to was staring right at me!  
I gasped and began to run- Fiosachd's luck did not last me long.  
The ants clicked loudly at my presence, hide had run and I barely noticed!  
Readily in hand, I unfolded a scroll and read the words to use a Mileth song,  
I collapsed on the grass to process what I had just seen- I paused to focus,  
I scribbled on the back of that Mileth scroll to report to you my findings.  
May I add, I suggest your faith be divine next time you plan to research while hiding!  

_An original poem by Peach_

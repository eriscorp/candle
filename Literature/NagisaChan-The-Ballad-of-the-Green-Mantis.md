# The Ballad of the Green Mantis

_by NagisaChan in **Dark Ages**_

My dearest nobles  
I beg forgiveness for bringing you troubles  
I ask you to judge my ballad,  
Which I hope is better than a random word salad,  
About an evil beast that we don't want to see in doubles!

In the depths of the Shinewood, in the lands of Temuair  
Lives an evil mantis whom we all despise  
With his big bug eyes  
In the hearts of Aislings he causes despair  
He haunts the woods and everybody knows him

Mantis! Mantis! Green Mantis  
He will blind you with dall  
Mantis! Mantis! Green Mantis  
The meanest little mantis of them all

Now mantises are an evil kind you know  
They will attack you if you move too slow  
They don't like travelers away from home  
They just want to eat you if you don't leave them alone

But one day an Aisling dared to go  
Attempt to kill Lady Phoenix, the most evil foe  
To try and become a Grand Master  
With the help of their friends, a basher and a caster

Mantis! Mantis! Green Mantis  
He will blind you with dall  
Mantis! Mantis! Green Mantis  
The meanest little mantis of them all

Against the Aislings he fought!  
A lot of chaos he wrought!!  
He plagued their minds with Dark Seal!!!  
They feared for their lives, this time for real!!!!  
He put them to sleep!!  
Trapped in the forest, their bodies he wanted to keep!!!  
He annihilated them with his Mad Soul!!!

Mantis! Mantis! Green Mantis  
The meanest little mantis of them all

Now he's back deep into the woods  
That mean little green turned the Aislings into foods  
The Shinewood is once again quiet  
Now that the mantis has satisfied his diet

Mantis! Mantis! Green Mantis  
He will blind you with dall  
Mantis! Mantis! Green Mantis  
The meanest little mantis of them all

`((This work was inspired by "The Ballad of Bilbo Baggins, by Leonard Nimoy https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QuQbus0xfhk It probably does not fit the original melody exactly anymore, but you can try having the tune playing in your head as you read it (:))`

# Feasgar's Promise

_by SOLiN in **Dark Ages**_

A long forgotten city rests  
on silver plumes that haunt the Sky  
and stolen glimpses can be seen  
when coloured arks betray the eye

while diamond streets reflect the heat  
the silver alleys trap the cold  
this place that once was full of life  
plays host to shades and ghosts of old

yet in this tortured city soul  
there lies a golden castle wall  
where once there reigned a mighty king  
who took too much, and lost it all

still, legend tells he will return  
to make the city gleam and glow  
with seed of thought and light of love  
a brand new castle, ours to grow

***

# Godly Impressions

_by Galena in **Dark Ages**_

_Regardless of which deity we worship all eight of them influence our lives in their unique way, leaving their imprints in us and moulding us into the Aislings we are._

**Glioca**  
Hands touching gently.  
Deep wounds heal.  
Loving forgiveness.

**Deoch**  
Flames dancing in a hearth.  
Rejuvenating warmth.  
Inspiration.

**Cail**  
Sharpened senses.  
Deep, peaceful breath.  
Nature wispers quietly.

**Gramail**  
Infinite order.  
Time beyond time.  
Eternal wheel turning.

**Fioschad**  
Abundant riches,  
Uncovered secrets;  
The soul is content.

**Luathas**  
Lessons of experience,  
knowledge begets wisdom.  
Safe haven.

**Ceannladir**  
Strenght with strenght collides.  
Bloodshed, tears.  
Waging war.

**Sgrios**  
Life cut short.  
Greedy fingers  
extinguish the flames.

( Written by Galena of Dark Ages)

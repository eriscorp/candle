# Lost Temuairan Love

_by Cynix in **Dark Ages**_

I felt the fire slowly burning away.  
The newly cold embers spreading between us.  
Painting a wall so hard to over come.  
Painting a line I couldn't seam to cross.  
Dividing us into pieces.  
Slowly, silently, and painfully you were slipping away.  
The never ending tears were falling;  
falling quickly upon my cheeks.  
Reaching out with all that I had for one small grasp of sanity.  
Searching my entire self for a decent emotion to bring me back to reality.  
To realize what my life would soon become.... I would soon be lost.  
Holding on as tightly as I could to dying flames......  
How could I let myself slip away?  
Everything I've wished and hoped for have been found  
in these now small grains I hold so delicately in my hands.

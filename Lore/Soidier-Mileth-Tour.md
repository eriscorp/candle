# Mileth Tour

_by Soidier in **Dark Ages**_

Mileth is a city of many sites. In our program, we will show your around town
and help you familiarize yourself with Mileth.

First, we will meet in Riona's Inn. The official Inn of Mileth. Here, Ye'
shall find a soft bed and a pleasant dream.

Though she houses the only inn in all of Mileth, Riona is gracious enough to
offer free rooms, and to sponsor the _Temuair Tour Groups_.

Our second spot to visit, is the Altar of Mileth. All of the Teumairan gods
have been known to appear here to the faithful. Tis located in front of the
Mileth inn, recognizable by the woman dressed in the ancient robes of Aosda.
Before her is a basin, which the offerings are placed.

After making an offering, walk west to a rather large building. Here, we find
Torrance, a armorer, smith, and jeweler. He sells some of the finest
equipment of Mileth. and for a fee, he will also repair ye' wares. In
addition to selling fine equipment, he also purchases gems in any shape or
size.

After looking at some of Torrance's goods, ye' can walk north and visit
Daithi, the rogue master. He teaches some skills and secrets to warriors and
monks, and also a variety of traps to rogues. Daithi also sells lock picks for
4,000 pieces of gold. Theses picks are vital to opening the treasure chests in
the crypts. "Offering talents for the dexterous. Arts for everyone"

North across bridge ye' will see two buildings. To the east is Dar's, And
further north, is Cian. Let's visit Dar first.

Dar, is the Dark Wizard of Mileth. A master of the Arts of the Arcane. He
offers much knowledge to young wizards and priests. He also offers knowledge
of mentoring and has many books on the topic as well. Dar not only teaches
secrets and skills to aislings, but he also allows wizards to research the
arcane arts.Another thing aislings can do is offer Dar dark things, which he
analysis in his own experiments. He often searches for a Spider's Eyes, a
Spider's Silk, a Bat's Wings, or a Scorpion's Glands. All these items can be
found in the cursed crypt. He will also sell parchment scrolls for 500 pieces
of gold. Scrolls are necessary for wizards to make arcane scrolls.

After taking a look around this wizard's home, let's visit Cian, the dabbler
in Alchemistries. this alchemist searches for rotten foods found throughout
the crypt. The Alchemist also sells a pair of coral earrings for 8000 pieces
of gold, giving 2 surges of intelligence. Cian will also take viper glands
found from the eastern woodland for 137 pieces of gold, and mantis's eyes for
1,500 pieces of gold.

After ye' visit these two mundane ye' can take a walk to the the Temple of
Choosing. If ye' haven't chosen a path yet, find a guide to teach you the arts
of your desired path (Monk, Warrior, Priest, Wizard, or Rogue). If ye' are at
your 11th insight, ye' can also begin guiding students yourself. But before
guiding an aisling into your path, be sure they know what's up ahead of them.

If ye' are looking for a fight. Venture South of the Temple of Choosing, and
past the old fountain. The Crypt of Mileth is 33 floors of vile creatures.
Beware of darkness in here, lest you have light in your heart. Within these
crypts lurk rats, centipedes, bats, spiders, and other dark creatures. Many
Aislings venture into this dangerous place to gain experience, and to collect
valuable dark items. It is recognizable by the vile odor coming from it.

West of this vile building lies Devlin, the Fae Priestess. The priestess
offers many valuable secrets and skills to Priests. She also offers much
insight of lover's glade and of the woods. Fae purchases many items found from
the Eastern woodlands.

In the next room over, ye' will run into Keallach, the master of weapons. This
strong and robust mundane teaches many tactics of combat to any Aisling. He
also offers many skills and secrets to Warriors and Monks of fighting
techniques.

Leaving the warrior master, walk west until you see a large gleaming building.
Here ye' find the Mileth church, dedicated to Glicoa, Mileth's Lady of Light,
and Also Deoch, the bring of light. Inside, the Priest Naomhan offers much
information of the altar. Frequently, ye' will here joyful sounds and
cheering, usually indicating a marriage.

West of the Church is Colm, the avian deliver of Mileth, whom guarantees any
package flown to anywhere in Temuair within minutes.

Northeast of the Colm is Callough. He sells many fine clothes to young
Aislings. He will also lend out his tools to aspiring tailors.

East of Colm is the famous Mileth Bank. Here, one can deposit their
possessions as well as gold for safe keeping. Inside a bank ye' will also find
the market board, where business deals are constantly being offered. Also, in
the bank, ye' can offer your labor to another Aisling. Rogues here can higgle
for products and change the hairstyle of other Aislings.

This concludes our tour of Mileth.

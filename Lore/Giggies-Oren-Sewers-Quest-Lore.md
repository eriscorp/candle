# Oren Sewers Quest Lore

_by Giggies in **Dark Ages**_

## The Beginning

There is an unusual little girl who lives on Oren island, and as a child she
heard a story from her grandfather, people know as Baldo. He once told her of
an old king, a fountain, and some swashbuckling pirates. This was how it went.

There once was a king named Bruce. He lived in Oren many deochs ago as a great
king. He did his duties, day in, day out, listening to the townspeople and
controlling political affairs. On a day when he went about his routine, there
came pirates whose ship washed ashore to the beaches of Lynith. They came in
search of a fountain, one that was kept in the great king's castle. It was
said that if one drank from this fountain once every four deochs, it would
enable one to live eternally.

This fountain was a very important monument, and if the wrong kind of person
or peoples drank from it, then it would help those people to prolong the
torture that they befall the land with. The pirates had invaded Oren in search
of this, for they were getting old and weary, and drinking this would give
them better life to continue on with their treacherous deeds of the land. They
interrogated the king many times, asking where this fountain was, but he
resisted them and never answered or gave away where it was at. The pirates
refused to believe that the fountain was not there, and they stayed on Oren
island, waiting for him to tell them, and they preyed on the women and
destroyed people's properties.

One day, he decided it was enough, that he did not like how his people were
being treated, so he had the fountain moved deep into the sewers of Oren, and
then he told the pirates exactly where he had had it. They looked at where it
used to be, and the area showed that the fountain had indeed been there. To
save his people the troubles, he fled the land, moving all the way to Loures
in hopes that the pirates would follow him, in pursuit of the fountain's
ability to make them live on.

Although most of the pirates did in fact leave, some pirates stayed on the
island. This is why there are still pirates that roam there today, and why if
you wish to live an extended life, you must travel deep into the dangerous
sewers of Oren.

The little girl was told this story by her grandfather, he was one of the few
people on the king's council that knew where he placed it. He told the little
girl so that the story would not be lost forever. If you should happen to come
across the girl, ask her about these sewers and this seeming fountain of
youth.

## Your Group

For a good group to do the Oren sewer quest you will need to have a rogue,
priest and a wizard. But Im not saying you -have- to have those classes in
order to do the quest I just reccommened those classes. Go in a big group if
you would like just make sure everyone has reach the insight requirement,
insight fourty-one or higher.

## The Quest

The first thing you and your group are going to need your pirate clothing. Go
to Lynith Beach, and take a left at the entrance.

Once you get into the next room go straight up until you find a raft on the
shores of lynith beach.

Once you get on the raft, walk up to the nose of it and you will get a pop-up.

Click 'yes' on the pop-up and you will be transported to a pirate ship. Go
into either door, it doesn't matter which one you choose they all lead to the
same place. (Also, make sure you are wearing your Pirate clothing so you dont
get thrown into the brig!)

When you enter the pirate ship go down the stairs and into the second room
located on the right side of the ship.

Go inside and walk away from the door going right and you will run inot a
pirate with a yellow shirt, talk to him.

Click 'The Aislings killed the captain!'

After you finish talking to the pirate, walk out of that room back into the
hall and go to the second to last room on the left side at the bottom.

Once you enter the room, walk from the door to the right side of the dresser.

Once you have recived the key to the captains quarters, leave the room and go
back up the hall and enter the room under the staircase.

You will be taken to the lower second deck of the Pirate ship. Walk to the
left door and go in and you will then be taken to the pirate navagation room.
There is a door on the up right hand side. Go into that door.

Walk up straight ahead to the desk and stand infront of the chair on the left
side. Read the map there.

After you read the map, use a song or exit and go to the entrance of Lynith
beach. Go into the right entrance.

# All You Need to Know About the Pentagram Quest

_by Starza in **Dark Ages**_

_The spirits are calling you…_

> _"The Library was especially cold. A dusty volume has just arrived. A volume
> excativated in House Macrabe by an Aisling explorer, Bentic. It related the
> history of five previous occupants, the scarlet beetle, the kopfloser reiter,
> the Lich, the Dark Cleric, and the Nagetier. One adventurer read in this tome
> of the ritual they have preformed. It was a gruesome ritual. Yet, more
> gruesome when he realized some two hundred years passed between the account
> of a man's life and the account of the ritual. He understood the ritual
> could be repeated. With a sick sense of dread, yet an interminable curiosity
> he sought out companions that would be needed to complete the ritual: A
> monk, a wizard, a priest, a rogue, and himself, a warrior. Not any, but
> worldly wise adventurers of at least three score insights. When he found the
> other four, he made them swear to secrecy. Then he told them the tale and of

> agreement to a pact. They made a pact of five, one of each of the Aisling
> classes."_  
> \- Rucesion Mundane Baltasar

## The Legend Begins

Ah, so you want to do the Pentagram Quest? This guide is to help you with the
common questions and problems that plague Aislings today. Many days, I've seen
Aislings do the Pentagram quest, but fail, due to a lack of knowledge. This
guide is by NO means an answer to the quest itself, but to give you some
advice when doing it. This guild also includes the armor possible to get
there.

## Starting out

As Baltasar stated, five Aislings of each class must be gathered for this
quest. They must have obtained their 60th insight at least. The following is a
range. Each Aisling needs to be in the range of insight, otherwise, you can't
do it.

60th-69th insight  
70th-79th insight  
80th-89th insight  
90th-98th insight  
99th insight

You should all prepare for this quest. For lower insights, you might want to
stay together at all times. That's the best way to do it. Make lots of
_beothaich deums_, and get the protection spell, _Aite_. You're going to need
them. Also, a Dark Belt will help you here, because you will be going to House
Macrabe and Cthonic Remains. You will also need full labor to do this quest.
Make sure you have 2 pieces of zombie flesh.

The first place to go is the Loures library. The wizard of your group will
start the quest. Next to the mundane Liberian is a shelf where the pact is
kept. You will all be asked to sign the pact. Sign it if you are truly
dedicated to doing this quest, otherwise leave. Once you have signed the pact,
head to House Macrabe.

## The Star

Head to the left in yard 1. You will come across a big open grave leading to
Study 1. When you enter, you should head up to the brown open box. You will
end up in a room. The room will have a star shape on the floor, skulls being
the five corners. I will show you where to stand on the start formation.

The wizard then drops two pieces of zombie flesh two steps next to him where
it will be in the star. You will be engulfed by purple bubbles. Everyone will
move two spaces clockwise. You should all now be in the following position.

Call on the spirit. What class you are will depend on what spirit answers you.
The list below tells you what spirit goes with what class.

Monk - Nagetier  
Priest – Dark Cleric  
Rogue - Kopfloser Reiter  
Warrior – Scarlet Beetle  
Wizard – Lich

You will now receive a clue on where to go. This is totally random. I can give
you a little help however. When you find this clue, another spirit will give
you another clue. You will see the spirit. Match this spirit up with the list
above. That is where that Aisling has to go. For example, let's say you are a
priest and you call up the spirit and you see the Nagetier. The clue you get
goes to your monk companion. Go to the exact spot and don't take any short
cuts. You will have about 1-2 days to do this `((It's about two in-real life
hours))`.

## Important Tips

The big mistake people make is rushing through to finish the ritual. This will
most likely lead to failure. NEVER rush. If you have no patience, then you
shouldn't sign the pact. The ritual is about patience and teamwork. You all
must work together and have the patience to succeed.

Another mistake I have noticed is that Aislings don't work together. This
ritual was put together so that one depends on each other. That mistake can
cost you the whole quest. Stick together and don't let anyone go alone into
the Cthonic Remains, especially low insight people.

Always be prepared. Bring extra _Beothaich deums_, bring some brown potions,
bring some arcane scrolls, etc. You're better off being over prepared than ill
prepared.

If you can get it, have older Aislings help you. Believe me, kind masters and
99th insight Aislings will be willing to help you. They can guide you in the
Cthonic Remains if you are lost. It doesn't hurt to call on help if you need
it.

If someone dies, DO NOT PANIC. Don't get mad, upset, or feel like quitting.
Keep your cool and help that teammate. Getting upset will do you no good; it
will only make others become upset.

## All the Clues come together

So you FINALLY got all the clues? Good! Now, once again, stand on the skulls
in the first position to begin with. The wizard should drop two pieces of
zombie flesh again. Darkness will engulf you for just a few seconds.

Out comes a brown monster! You must defeat this monster. Don't worry; he's not
as hard as you might think. With good teamwork, he will fall easily.

Now, stand on the skulls again. The wizard should drop the zombie flesh again.
You will then receive your armor. Congratulations!

## Some other nifty things to know

The Pentagram Quest is probably the hardest quest yet. But it's fun! Take a
look below at the armor you can receive!

Monk  
Priest  
Rogue  
Warrior  
Wizard

Currently, you do not receive a legend mark for this quest. But I think the
armor is well worth the effort.

Remember to work together, have patience, and most importantly, HAVE FUN!

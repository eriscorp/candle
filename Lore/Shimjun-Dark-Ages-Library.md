## Class Guides

### Monk Guide

#### Basic Pre-Master Information

Monks are one of the basher paths of Temuair. Monks are generally weaker than
warriors in raw power, due to having less assails, but monks deal more power
through skills than a warrior. Monks do well hunting in most hunting grounds
and are exceptional in East Woods, West Woods and especially Karlopos Island
since they have the spell 'Ao Puinsein.' Monks can also learn a spell 'Draco
Stance' or 'Dion' to give them temporary invulnerability. Monks have some
special features for only monks, the first is the animal forms. Monks have the
option of choosing a special 'form' that comes with 3 unique skills/spells.
Draco, and White Bat are the most commonly used forms, and Kelberoth is a
great form for those with higher HP, and sub-paths. Monks also have the
Sapphire Stream Monastery which provides them with many skills and spells. To
learn the spells in the Monastary you must teach other Aislings their 'Dugons'
which to some is a great unique monk ability, and to others is a hassle of
getting their required 'Hubaes' or students. For monks the most difficult time
to overcome is the Insights 80-99.

#### Sapphire Stream Monastary

The Sapphire Stream Monastary is a special area where monks (Sabonim) can
teach other monks (Hubae) to get access to a various amount of spells and
skills. The skills and spells learned at the Monastary greatly help the Monk
path. Most Aisling subbing will not get Black Dugon, but only Red Dugon as it
has the last important skills. Wraith's Touch is not a very powerful spell..
but Wolf Fang Fist (wff) is very popular. Because every monk needs to get
20-32 Hubae students, there are quite a limited amount in the game and most
often you will have to buy your hubae or be very patient to get free ones
which is one major downside of monks.

#### Herbalism

Herbalism is a very handy skill you can learn early on as a monk. It not only
creates potions that heal, and cure poison, but creating them earns you
experience. There are many kinds of herb's you can produce. To create these
herbs you are required to have a cloth glove equipped, also some herbs need
either wax or wine. You can get wax from bee's in the eastern woodlands, and
wine is readily available at all taverns, rogues can higgle wine also. Head
out to the enchanted garden with your wine, wax and equipped cloth glove to
begin herbalism. Please note that a day's labor is needed to create a potion.
It's recommend that you take an assistant who also know's herbalism to assist
you, as it requires less labor and you have more of a chance of success. Walk
around the plants in the garden. Once you locate the herb you desire, start
the process. Every so often, you will attain a legend mark stating you are a
herbalist *Herbalism (1)* the higher your level, you get more chance of
success and a chance of getting more potion out of a bottle of wine.

Hydele Deum: This potion heals the user to full health. The plants are large
and vibrant red, they are very hard to miss. Stem into the bush, pluck a few
leaves and start mixing it in with the wine.

Personica Deum: This potion cure's an aisling of poison. This potion can be
used by Aisling willing to do the "Mother's Love" quest. Personaca is a small
purple leafed plant.

Betony Deum: Its only visible attribute is removing nightmare's from one's
dream's. Help the mother's in the taverns with this vanilla potion. You could
earn yourself a purple flower which is quite the rare artifact. (mother's love
quest). The bright white leaves on this plant make it stand out.

Fifleaf Deum: A potion that revives a small amount of health, Fifleaf is not
sought after, so it is rarely grown. Higglers in Mileth grow them, you can
find them growing outside their door's.

Hemloch Deum: This potion will bring you very close to death. Warriors require
this potion for several things, to start the Carnun Quest or to practice the
skill 'Crasher'. The hemloch weed grows near lake soaked rocks. Its main
source is undine.

Ancusa Ceir: This herb is used mainly to cure burns of the mother in the
Mother's Love quest. Ancusa can be found along banks of river's mainly in peit
and undine. Make sure you carry a piece of raw wax with you to mix it with the
Ancusa. You will then Create Speckled wax to apply to the burns. Speckled wax
can also be used as a belt to provide Fire Defense.

#### Monk Master

Once you have gotten to Insight 99, you are required to make a choice to
either sub and be a dual-path aisling or master as a pure path. Everyone can
master, but whatever the 2nd class you choose is, will be your mastering
class. For example a monk who gets to 99 then masters would be a 'Pure Monk,'
a monk the gets to 99 then subs to Warrior would be a 'Monk/Warrior' and would
learn the warrior master skills once they are ready to master. If monk if your
ending class when you are ready to master here are the skills, spells and
armors you will be able to acquire, as well as your mastering requirements.
Monk Master Requirements: 1) You must first meet the HP/MP Requirements to
master. Monks have require all stats to be 15 or greater without items and
have 6850HP and 4000MP. If your stats are not all at 15+ you are required to
have 8850HP and 4500MP which will make your stats very expensive to buy. 2)
Next you must have the required skills or spells in order to master. Monks
have 3 options for mastering these. 1st option is have Brown Dugon and learn
Wise Touch. 2nd option is to max Puinneag Beatha and Beag Nochd. 3rd option is
to max Claw Fist and Wise Touch. 3)Next thing you must do in order to master
is craft the Sand Glass at the Monastery. To craft it you must have a Cauldron
and Gold Sand. You can either buy your master set or hunt them in Cthonic
Remains 55. 4) Lastly you must have 200000000 exp, and be ready to sacrifice
it to master. Pure Master vs Sub Master: Subs simply get the skills/spells of
2 classes. Pure Monks can learn the skill Whirlwind, which is a HP based 4-way
attack. The attack is not very powerful compared to the other HP based attacks
in the game. Pure class masters also gain better Grand Master Armors and
Weapons which you can see below. Also note that once you become a Druid you
lose your master and grand master gear. Pure Druids get a bonus of 5000MP when

#### Druid

Monks 2nd tier class is Druid. To become a druid you must have 250mil exp,
500hp and 500mp ready to be sacrificed. You must also have completed all 4
Creants. When you become a druid you then have the option to hunt ability
points (AP) to gain Abilities. At new abilities you can get new armors, skills
and spells. When you become a druid you also lose all of your master
armors/weapons and grand master armors/weapons. If you are a Pure Monk, you
will receive a bonus of 5000Mp when you go druid. Druids also have the unique
feature of choosing a new form, these forms however cannot be changed once you
choose them so choose wisely. Druids can choose become either a Feral Druid,
Komodas Druid or Kurura Druid. When you go druid however, you lose a lot of
weapon power due to the loss of the grand master weapons.

### Priest Guide

#### Basic Pre-Master Information

Priests are supportive casters and healers, they also have the ability to
curse creatures and make them easier to kill. Priests are the only class that
can advance in the religions. All classes can join a religion, but only as a
worshipper or an acolyte. Priests can become a priest of 'god/goddess' and if
they have won an award they can advance further into the clergy. Once a priest
becomes Insight 19 they can wield a staff, the best staff for a priest is the
Holy Diana which takes 2 lines off of all spells. Priests are generally needed
at all levels so it should be easy to get hunts no matter what insight you
are. As you are leveling up it is suggested to put most of your stats into
wisdom, especially earlier on, this will give you more mana and help you get
the most important skills in your hunts mor cradh, and ard cradh, many groups
will choose a priest with the better curse when they are hunting. Always use
+stat items to learn spells, you don't want to add any str or dex before
master.

#### Beothaich

Beothaich or 'red potions' are very important when hunting. If you are
ungrouped and your HP hit 0, you die instantly. If you are in a group, and
your HP hits 0, you 'skull' and are your group has the chance to save you by
walking up to you, facing you, and using a red potion. Priests can make
beothaich with the spell 'Beothaichdeum' To use it you must have cherries and
grapes with you. 1 batch of beothaich takes 2 Grapes and 1 Cherry. When
successful you will make Beothaich deum, when unsuccessful you may sometimes
rot the fruits or make wine. Priests can gather grapes and cherries in Suomi,
or have a rogue higgle them from any banker. You can hold up to 30 red potions
at one time. Or you can hold 30 "unidentified red potions" and 30 "identified
beothaich deum's" leaving you with 60, however, when you log off, you will
only have 30 left.

#### Religion

Religion is something that all aislings can take part in regardless of class.
However only Priests can advance deep into the Religion's Clergy. First all
aislings start off as a 'Worshipper' allowing you to pray to your chosen
Deity. After showing your faith, you may become a 'Acolyte' gaining the
ability to summon creatures of your chosen Deity. Anyone can become an
Acolyte. Now only Priests can advance to the position of 'Priest of XXXX
Religion' allowing them to initiate other aislings into the religion, invite
fragiles into masses, cast mass, and remove aislings that are disturbing the
temple. If you show yourself worthy by winning a contest held at the Mileth
College you can advance further into the religion as a Cleric, Minister or
High Priest(ess). For more information about the different religions of
Temuair, see the Religion Section.

#### Altar Worshipper

Being a Priest also allows you to become an Altar Worshipper and receive gifts
and experience from the Gods. The Altar is located in Mileth right near the
Inn. When you become an Altar Worshipper you receive a legend mark "Mileth
Altar Worshipper (X)" and you will also gain 50000-100000 exp and perhaps a
consecrated ring. To gain the mark you must drop items into the altar, the
Gods expect items with at least 500 Coins of value to gain favorable
recognition. The easiest way is to just visit a hunting area and pick up all
of the junk, and sacrifice it at the altar. Each item also has a chance of
giving you up to 1000 exp. A great way for young priests to gain some exp and
rewards. For additional information about the Altar you can visit the Mundane
located in the Mileth Church.

#### Herbalism

Herbalism is a useful skill you can learn as a priest. It not only creates
potions that heal, and cure poison, but creating them earns you experience.
There are many kinds of herb's you can produce. To create these herbs you are
required to have a cloth glove equipped, also some herbs need either wax or
wine. You can get wax from bee's in the eastern woodlands, and wine is readily
available at all taverns, rogues can higgle wine also. Head out to the
enchanted garden with your wine, wax and equipped cloth glove to begin
herbalism. Please note that a day's labor is needed to create a potion. It's
recommend that you take an assistant who also knows herbalism to assist you,
as it requires less labor and you have more of a chance of success. Walk
around the plants in the garden. Once you locate the herb you desire, start
the process. Every so often, you will attain a legend mark stating you are a
herbalist *Herbalism (1)* the higher your level, you get more chance of
success and a chance of getting more potion out of a bottle of wine.

Hydele Deum: This potion heals the user to full health. The plants are large
and vibrant red, they are very hard to miss. Stem into the bush, pluck a few
leaves and start mixing it in with the wine.

Personica Deum: This potion cure's an aisling of poison. This potion can be
used by Aisling willing to do the "Mother's Love" quest. Personaca is a small
purple leafed plant.

Betony Deum: Its only visible attribute is removing nightmare's from one's
dream's. Help the mother's in the taverns with this vanilla potion. You could
earn yourself a purple flower which is quite the rare artifact. (mother's love
quest). The bright white leaves on this plant make it stand out.

Fifleaf Deum: A potion that revives a small amount of health, Fifleaf is not
sought after, so it is rarely grown. Higglers in Mileth grow them, you can
find them growing outside their door's.

Hemloch Deum: This potion will bring you very close to death. Warriors require
this potion for several things, to start the Carnun Quest or to practice the
skill 'Crasher'. The hemloch weed grows near lake soaked rocks. Its main
source is undine.

Ancusa Ceir: This herb is used mainly to cure burns of the mother in the
Mother's Love quest. Ancusa can be found along banks of river's mainly in peit
and undine. Make sure you carry a piece of raw wax with you to mix it with the
Ancusa. You will then create speckled wax to apply to the burns. Speckled wax
can also be used as a belt to provide Fire Defense.

#### Bard

Priests 2nd tier class is Bard. To become a bard you must have 250mil exp,
500hp and 500mp ready to be sacrificed. You must also have completed all 4
Creants. When you become a bard you then have the option to hunt ability
points (AP) to gain Abilities. At new abilities you can get new armors, skills
and spells. When you become a bard you also lose all of your master
armors/weapons and grand master armors/weapons. If you are a Pure Bard, you
will be able to learn Mesmerize. When you go bard however, you lost a lot of
geared MP due to the loss of your Grand Master gear, and pure bards lose a
very nice staff.

### Rogue Guide

#### Basic Pre-Master Information

Rogues are a very versatile path in Temuair. Rogues jobs tend to change a lot
as they advance in their insights. At low insights a rogue's main hunting
method is by using their traps. Once they can tell elements and use Great
Blind Snare they can join in on hunts as a blinder, and can tell elements for
the bashers. Once they master, and then become Archers their jobs in a hunt
will be different again. Rogues are the only class that have access to the
Black Market, a special bank and shop for Iron Gauntlets, Jade Rings, Spinel
Rings, Emerald Rings, Scrolls and other gear. Rouges can also smith gems,
daggers, and soori's as well as tailoring armors.

#### Smithing

Smithed Blades and Soori's can be one of the greatest assets to a fighter or
rogue who wants to hunt light. With a lighter blade you can hold greater
armor, also smithed weapons increase the damage that is done. Rogues can smith
two types of weapons, 1 Handed Blades, and Soori's/Daggers. There are four
grades of smithing, the first is a 'good' smith, next being 'fine,' then
'grand' and the best being a 'great' smith. For each degree a blade gets it
will be harder to use (higher insight requirement, deal more damage, increase
the value, and be lighter to wield. You also gain small amounts of experience
points by smithing weapons. The more blades and sooris that you smith the
better you will become, When you smith a good weapon you get 1 point, fine, 2
points etc. Once you achieve 50 points from blade smithing you earn the legend
mark 'Blade Smith (1)' and it can go to 'Blade Smith (10)' Having an assistant
increases you chances of making a better weapon. There is also always a chance
of not improving the weapon and even breaking it. This brings us to the other
type of smithing. Rogues also have the ability to smith gems, and are the only
class that can do so. Smithing gems mostly have the purpose of getting spells,
many priest and wizard spells require Uncut or Finished gems. Some other uses
for gems are making Insight 97 Armor (Need 25 Finished Talos), Selling smithed
gems, Giving Finished gems to the mundanes in Pravat for experience. Gem Smith
can only go to Gem Smith (10) as well, when you smith with a partner, gems
never break. It will take a few times to smith a gem to finished, 1 time for
uncut (without a partner). All types of smithing require and use labor.

#### Tailoring

Tailoring is a way to improve one's armor. Tailoring increases the AC, and
lowers the weight of the armor. The armors that can be tailored are Insight 1,
11, 41, 71, 97 and Warrior's 26, 56 and 86 armors. Pentagram Quest, Nightmare
Quest, Master, Grand Master and Medenian armors cannot be tailored or dyed.
Tailor legends can go very high, but it takes insane amounts of time to
improve them, Tailor (5) is a fairly easy task, and after that it get
increasingly difficult. The highest I have ever seen is somewhere between
13-15. Tailoring armors with an another tailor will highly increase your
chances of making a better armor. Tailoring legends are needed to craft some

#### Archer

Rogues 2nd tier class is Archer. To become an archer you must have 250mil exp,
500hp and 500mp ready to be sacrificed. You must also have completed all 4
Creants. When you become an archer you then have the option to hunt ability
points (AP) to gain Abilities. At new abilities you can get new armors, skills
and spells. When you become an archer you also lose all of your master
armors/weapons and grand master armors/weapons. If you are a Pure Archer, you
will be able to learn a spell called Blinding Bolt, which is simply quite
useless in most cases, it it a 4 line, 7 second blinding spell, and it does
not work on aislings. Archers most important spell is Star Arrow, which is a
'meall' light attack spell, which means it hits a target and all surrounding
it by 2 spaces in all directions, and as a plus it takes low mp, and has 0
cast lines. Rogues get much stronger as Archers, but once again their job in a
hunt will change. When you go archer however, you lose a lot of weapon power,
the archer weapons are much weaker then the grand master weapons, and you will
probably end up using a Throw Axe to get in your stab skills.

### Warrior Guide

#### Basic Pre-Master Information

Warriors are one of the basher paths of Temuair. Warriors are generally
stronger than monks in raw power, due to having more assails, but monks deal
more power through skills than a warrior. Warriors do well hunting in most
hunting grounds and are good grouped with a priest and/or wizard. Warriors get
the most armors in the game, they are they only path that gets armor at
Insight 26, 56 and 86, also warriors get many shields, and they ability to
wield Two-Handed Swords. Warriors have a special fighting area for warriors
only called 'Pit Fights' where warriors can challenge others around their
insight. Pure warriors have many special skills that can be learned at the
Warrior Training Grounds. For warriors the most difficult time to overcome is
the Insights 80-99. When building your warrior you will most likely want to
add your 25-35 con early on to give you the best HP as you level up.

#### Pit Fights

Warriors can challenge other warriors to a Pit Fight, a battle of honor on the
beaches of Abel. In the building on Abel Beach the challenger must say "Honor
Field (Insight Level)" to challenge your opponent, and you and your opponent
must be within 3 levels of each other. The winner gains a small amount of
experience and a legend mark 'Won 1 Pit Fight(s)' Once you have won 5 Pit
Fights you can no longer challenge anyone, but if someone challenged you, you
can still battle. There is no penalty of death for dying in the pit fight
arena, once the winner talks to the mundane and claims their prize the loser
will be removed from the Sgrios pit.

#### 2 Handed Sword Smithing

Smithed Blades can be one of the greatest assets to a fighter who wants to
hunt light. With a lighter blade you can hold greater armor, also smithed
weapons increase the damage that is done. Warriors can smith twohanded weapons
weapons only. There are four grades of smithing, the first is a 'good' smith,
next being 'fine,' then 'grand' and the best being a 'great' smith. For each
degree a blade gets it will be harder to use (higher insight requirement, deal
more damage, increase the value, and be lighter to wield. You also gain small
amounts of experience points by smithing weapons. The more blades and soori's
that you smith the better you will become, When you smith a good weapon you
get 1 point, fine, 2 points etc. Once you achieve 50 points from blade\e
smithing you earn the legend mark 'Blade Smith (1)' and it can go to 'Blade
Smith (10)' Having an assistant increases you chances of making a better
weapon. There is also always a chance of not improving the weapon and even
breaking it.

#### Gladiator

Warriors 2nd tier class is Gladiator. To become a gladiator you must have
250mil exp, 500hp and 500mp ready to be sacrificed. You must also have
completed all 4 Creants. When you become a gladiator you then have the option
to hunt ability points (AP) to gain Abilities. At new abilities you can get
new armors, skills and spells. When you become a gladiator you also lose all
of your master armors/weapons and grand master armors/weapons. If you are a
Pure Gladiator, you will be able to learn a spell called Aegis Sphere, which
gives the user a -17AC spell that cannot be stacked with Armachd, but it can
not be ao sithed either and lasts 40mins. Gladiators gain a couple nice damage
skills with pretty low aethers making them quite useful in the hunting field.
When you go gladiator however, you lose a lot of weapon power, the gladiator
weapons are much weaker then the grand master weapons.

#### Elemental Information

In Temuair there are 6 elements. First I will talk about the first basic 4
elements, which are the elements of the spell learned by wizards; Fire, Water,
Earth, Lightning or Srad, Sal, Creag and Athar. The way the elements work is
this. Srad > Athar > Creag > Sal > Srad. So when the rogue or monk usually
that you are with reads the element of the creatures defense, you will have to
use the opposite to deal the most damage. There are also 2 other elements that
creatures may have, the element of Light and Dark. The only element that will
harm a dark defense is Light, and vise versa, only Dark will harm Light.

| Defense | Use |
|---|---|
| Fire | Sal |
| Water | Creag |
| Earth | Athar |
| Lightning | Srad |

#### Gathering Fiors

Fiors are used with 'leisaich' spells to refill some of a wizards MP. To
refill your mana, you must have a Nadurra Necklace and a 'Leisaich' Spell. If
you had for instance 'Ard Leisaich Srad' you would need to have a 'Fior Srad'
in your inventory. Fiors can be purchased at the Black Market, but can also be
gathered by a wizard with a leisaich spell. The locations to gather fiors are
as follows: Fior Athar can be gathered near trees. Fior Creag can be gathered
by the rocks near Mileth Crypts. Fior Sal can be found near any well. Fior
Srad can be found by any torch. Gathering fiors will eventually give you the
legend mark "Elementalist (1)"

#### Summoner

Wizards 2nd tier class is Summoner. To become a summoner you must have 250mil
exp, 500hp and 500mp ready to be sacrificed. You must also have completed all
4 Creants. When you become a summoner you then have the option to hunt ability
points (AP) to gain Abilities. At new abilities you can get new armors, skills
and spells. When you become a summoner you also lose all of your master
armors/weapons and grand master armors/weapons. If you are a Pure Summoner,
you will get a spell called Lyliac Plant which will transfer the wizards MP
directly to another aisling. Summoners have the ability to learn more powerful
elemental spells called summons, some nice graphics also tag along to the
wizard summons. When you go summoner however, you lost a lot of geared MP due
to the loss of your Grand Master gear.

### Druid Guide

#### Druid

To become a druid you must first be a Master Monk with all 4 Grand Master
Quests completed. When you become a druid you will lose 250mil exp, 500hp and
500mp. When you become a druid you then have the option to hunt ability points
(AP) to gain Abilities. At new abilities you can get new armors, skills and
spells. When you become a druid you also lose all of your master
armors/weapons and grand master armors/weapons. If you are a Pure Monk, you
will receive a bonus of 5000Mp when you go druid. Druids also have the unique
feature of choosing a new form, these forms however cannot be changed once you
choose them so choose wisely. Druids can choose become either a Feral Druid,
Komodas Druid or Kurura Druid. When you go druid however, you lose a lot of
weapon power due to the loss of the grand master weapons.

All Medenian Spells/Skills with numbers will be replaced when the next
spell/skill of the sequence is learned. Ex: When 'Double Rake 2' is learned it
will replace 'Double Rake 1'. When you meet the Ability requirement to learn a
skill or spell , make sure you have the required items in your inventory, and
experience saved, then to learn the skill or spell, use 'Learning Spell'
anywhere spells can be cast and type the name of the spell/skill you are ready
to learn. If you meet all requirements the items and exp will be removed and
you will receive the skill/spell.

### Cthonic Disciple Of Dawn

Walkthrough - Insight 80+

#### STEP 1: PREPERATION

A: For this quest you will need to have a Sgrios Prayer Necklace, make sure
you bring one with you. These can be difficult to aquire, Sgrians tend to keep
their prayer necklaces to themselves, so you may need to buy one off an
aisling who has acquired one.

B: Talk to Gregory in the Temple of Choosing, located in Mileth, where you
choose your class.

C: He will tell you that there are only certain days you can do this quest,
which is what makes it so difficult. The days are the day of Sgrios, the day
after Sgrios, or the day of Luathas.. (4th, 8th, 9th, 12th, 16th, 17th, 20th,
24th, 25th) To check if you are on the correct day use the spell nis (or
relog) and check the current 'sun' that will tell you the day. If it is not
one of those days, you must wait until its one of those days to complete the
quest. THE DISCIPLE OF DAWN IS DONE ON THE DAY OF LUATHAS (4th, 12th, 20th)

#### STEP 2: Mileth Crypt

A: The first place you must go is Mileth Crypt 22-2, once there stand at this
location with your Sgrios Prayer Necklace on.

For a map to Mileth Crypt 22-2 Click Here (maps not yet completed)

B: Once you get the popup go through it and pray to Sgrios, and you will get
ported to the Cthonic Disciple room. Make sure you read this part, it tells
you where to go next.

#### STEP 3: Dubhaimh Castle

A: The next place you will be told to go for the Disciple of Dawn quest is
Dubhaimh Castle. If you do not get told to go here you have another version of
the quest which gives the same reward but a different legend mark. Dubhaimh is
located past Rucesion. You want to go in the furthest right building and head
to room 6-2 and the 8th pillar.

For a map to Dubhaimh Castle 6-2 Click Here (maps not yet completed)

#### STEP 4: Back to Mileth Crypt

A: Now you must head back to Mileth Crypt 22-2 to the same spot, once again
when you pray to Sgrios you will be sent back to the Cthonic Disciple room,
this time however you receive your reward.

B: Talk to Thycan then Brilann in the Nobis tavern.

buy a shovel for 1500 coins while you're there.

General Hints: When going through the ruins to do the quest the White
Skeltons, the Fire Beetalics and the Ruidhtear's cast Light Blast which is a
light damaging spell that hits its target and anything nearby as well. Click
Here for the Oren Ruins beastiary. When taveling the group should wear Light
Belts, if bashing a blind creature wear a Dark Belt to stay protected. This is
an area where Fas Nadur is more of an option because there are both light and
dark attacks to worry about.

section.

### Lady Phoenix Creant

Walkthrough

#### STEP 1: PREPERATION

A: Before you start this quest here is a list of items you will need, and
where you can get them. By gathering some of the items before hand you will
save yourself a lot of time. Each person will need... Cherry (1) - Suomi
Farms, Higgle Grape (1) - Suomi Farms, Higgle Mantis's Eye (3) - East
Woodlands 1-5, Shinewood Forest 2-9 (Mantis) Papaya (1) - Shinewood Forest
9-11, 13 (Wasps) Green Grapes (1) - Shinewood Forest 9-11, 13 (Vipers)
Tangerines (2) - Shinewood Forest 9-11, 13 (Forest Centipede) Rambutans (1) -
Shinewood Forest 14-35 (Shinewood Mantis) Passion Flower (1) - Shinewood
Forest 19-35 (Gold Beetlalic) Tentacle (1) - Pirate Ship, Lynith Sea

General Hints: Shinewood Forest is a newbie hunting ground until rooms 23+
Shinewood Mantis's in 23+ are all Elemental Attack and Dark Defense and cast
Light Blast spells so you are best off with a Light Belt. Gold Beetlalic's in
29+ also cast Light Blast and are Fire Attack and Light Defense. To increase
the speed of this quest your group can go to the summon spots for the Ant and
Mantis first, then make the potions to save 2 trips back to Shinewood 17.

#### STEP 2: The Dragon Scale Sword

A: First you need to talk to Theradus, the old beggar in Mileth Tavern, fyou
must have your Tentacle on you and he will tell you of the Dragon Scale Sword

For a Map to Shinewood Forest 8 Click Here (maps not completed)

B: Summon the Dragon in Shinewood Forest 8 by walking in its eye (use tab
map). After killing it he will drop a Dragon Scale Sword

Hints: The Dragon is very easy, The Dragon Scale Sword sub-quest can be done
at Insight 20. Use your map when walking around in Shinewood Forest 8, the map
is shaped like a Dragon's Mouth, and you summon on the Eye.

#### STEP 3: The Giant Ant

A: Talk to "?" in Shinewood Forest 17 in a small house.

For a Map to Shinewood Forest 17 Click Here (maps not completed)

B: Head to Shinewood 34 to see the Giant Ant, you need to walk into the Ants
body (use tab map) until you receive a popup that says you see the Giant Ant.

For a Map to Shinewood Forest 34 Click Here (maps not completed)

C: Go back to Shinewood 17 and say "Giant Ant" in the house. You will need to
make a Sweet Potion with your Cherry, Grape, Papaya, Green Grape and Rambutan
by saying "I have the fruits"

D: Go back to Shinewood 34 to summon the Giant Ant in the same spot where you
saw it. Kill the Giant Ant to receive the Ant Head.

Hints: Just bash the crap out of this summon and casters keep your bashers
healed, this summon is fairly simple but it does cast Asgall Faileas and
Armachd on itself, and it will use Crasher (Sucks HP and MP) and Beag Suain on
anyone adjacent to it. (So casters stay back). Bashers wear Light Belt's it
attacks with Holy.

#### STEP 4: The Red Mantis

A: Head to Shinewood 27 to see the Red Mantis, you need to walk into the
Mantis's body (use tab map) until you receive a popup that says you see the
Red Mantis.

For a Map to Shinewood Forest 27 Click Here (maps not completed)

B: Go back to Shinewood 17 and say "Red Mantis" in the house. You will need to
make a Mantis Passion Potion with your Passion Flower, Mantis Eyes and
Tangerines by saying "I have the Mantis stuff"

C: Go back to Shinewood 27 to summon the Red Mantis in the same spot where you
saw it. Kill the Red Mantis to receive the Mantis Claw.

Hints: Just bash the crap out of this summon and casters keep your bashers
healed, this summon is fairly simple but it does cast Asgall Faileas and
Armachd on itself, and it will use Crasher (Sucks HP and MP) and Beag Suain on
anyone adjacent to it. (So casters stay back). Bashers wear Light Belt's it
attacks with Holy.

#### STEP 5: The Hydra

A: Go to Shinewood Forest 31 to the Altar to summon the Hydra. To summon the
Hydra Equip your Dragon Scale Sword, click on the altar and choose "Attack
Altar" Your sword will disappear and you will have your next summon to kill.

For a Map to Shinewood Forest 31 Click Here (maps not completed)

B: Kill the Hydra, the first 2 heads will drop some gold, the last head will
give you the Lady Phoenix Claw

Hints: Lay a Suain trap for the each Hydra head. Make sure to curse each head.
Bashers wear Light Belt's it attacks with Holy. Hydra attacks with Ard Srad
Gar and Ard Dhubh Gar when not suained.

#### STEP 6: Completing the Summoning Set

A: Go to the house in Shinewood 30 with all of your items and say "Creant" out
loud.

For a Map to Shinewood Forest 30 Click Here (maps not completed)

B: Say "I have the Head" and you will lose your Ant Head and gain a Lady
Phoenix Ring

C: Say "I have the Claw" and you will lose your Mantis Claw and gain a Lady
Phoenix Feather

#### STEP 7: The Lady Phoenix Creant

A: Go to Shinewood 35 at the giant platform to summon the Lady Phoenix Creant.

For a Map to Shinewood Forest 35 Click Here (maps not completed)

B: Kill Lady Phoenix and receive a Lady Phoenix Helm.

C: Walk back up to the altar to finish the quest and receive your reward.

Hints: Lay lots of sleep traps and keep Lady Phoenix suained. When no suained
she will cast Ard Srad Gar and Ard Dubh Gar on you, she will also Dion making

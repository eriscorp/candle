# The Races of Temuair

_by Sakura in **Dark Ages**_

_Dedicated to the Seekers of Knowledge_

_Greetings...fellow seekers of knowledge,_

_Ever since I can remember, I have been fascinated by aspects in life often
looked over or ignored by others. I realize we, the Aislings...the Mundanes,
are not the only ones who dwell within this world and that we share our realm
with a variety of creatures. To fully understand these other beings and their
actions is probably quite impossible, as we do not even fully understand
ourselves. However, by gaining more knowledge about these different races and
the way they interact with one another, we can perhaps hope to attain a better
understanding of our own place in Temuair. Knowledge is a powerful key...which
can be used in several different ways._

_For several moons, I have performed careful observation and research of some
of the various groups and tribes of Temuair. I have spoken with the shopkeepers
and masters in various villages and have made many a trip to the grand library
at Loures seeking the most accurate information. I have decided to compile a
summary of my findings, in order to assist those seeking to expand their own
knowledge and maybe in the process, cast away some predjudices formed against
other races. I cannot say that everything in these writings are completely
true, as some are simply opinions, and not facts, formed by others. I have
tried, however, to stay as close to truth as I possibly can. This is especially
hard when dealing with those races there is little known information about. I
can also say that these are possibly only a few of the many races that dwell
within the world Temuair...there appear to be more, hinted at from my readings
at Loures, whether known to us or unknown, but these five I have detailed here
continue to be the most common besides our own. I hope this document will serve
it's purpose and that you will gain some insight from my writings below..._

## Goblins

A word of caution to the wise, avoid the goblins at all costs. I have dealt
with these 'warriors' on more than one occasion and this is the best advice I
can give to those thinking they can stand up to their hordes. They are a
malevolent and volatile race, preferring to fight with unbalanced tactics
against those who dare cross their paths. They always fight in groups of two or
more, with their blunt, wooden clubs ready to smash any friend or foe that
appears bothersome to them. Be cautious when you see one ahead of you, for
there are surely more about, ready to aid their brother when he yells for them.
A bit of good fortune to the weary traveler perhaps is that goblins prefer to
stay deep within the woods past Mileth and have only been known to stray
outside their safehaven in cases upon those who have done them wrong. A small
band now dwells in the Pravat Caves for this reason, in open defiance against
the Grimloks who have lived there for years. It strikes me as strange however
that they prefer living with their natural enemies, the Grimlok, instead of in
their secluded houses in the woods. This could be due to their warlike
instincts to be in the midst of conflict and to avoid the peace other races
hold so dear. They love to stir up conflict and to involve any who are foolish
enough to want to help.

Goblins live in a society where the most powerful and strongest is the one who
rules the tribe. They base their ranks upon strength only, for this is what is
most important to them and is what constitutes a good leader. Goblins are short
in stature and lack agility and intelligence, but make up for these weaknesses
by possessing an amazing brute strength. I pity the person who is on the wrong
end of a goblin's fierce swing. Goblins possess some magic, but prefer to use
their weapons and numbers to ward off their enemies. Goblins have never been
known to be 'kind' to any race, only tolerating the kobolds, with whom they
live, because of an ages old pact dating far back into ancient history. This is
not a race to be trusted by any means and they should be watched with a
cautious eye.

## Kobolds

"A bunch of tricksters...that's all they are. Nothing more...their love for
gold is their only motivation." When asking the mundanes about the kobolds who
live inside the Mileth woodlands, this was the only reply I could gather. They
seemed rather disinterested and unimpressed with the small, wolf-like tribe
whose main concern is the collecting of large amounts of gold and other
valuables. Upon my own encounters with this race, they seem to be ones to be a
bit more cautious around then the mundanes would have had me believe. They use
much of the same group tactics as the goblins do, most likely due to their
close proximity with one another for so many years. So, when you see a lone
kobold wandering about on the path, I advise you to be careful about engaging
them in battle. They use more deceitful tactics then that of their neighbors.
When one of their own is attacked, they will not assist unless the battle
weighs heavily in their favor. If so, they proceed to come out of hiding in
full force and relentlessly pursue those who wish to do them harm. They do not
possess brute strength, but rather, an unmatched agility and swift hand-to-hand
combat abilities. Because of their fast movements, one may fail to see the
others closing in before it is too late.

Kobolds are a greedy and petty race, basing their importance and status on who
has obtained the most wealth and riches. They are most often referred to as
thieves and not warriors, for they do not have the great weapons or armor the
goblins possess. Their only weapon is a small pointed piece of flint, crudely
fashioned out of the raw materials found around their dwellings. They wear no
protective gear, more prone to run if they get into trouble. They have never
been seen outside the woodlands and most likely never will. They are a more
withdrawn group than that of the goblins and only attack the unsuspecting who
happen to come across their homes. A small group has be rumored to live near
the Suomi vineyards, but upon asking the cherry farmer in Suomi, he
responded,*"There 'ave never been any pests, such as those kobolds you speak
of, around this region...and I dinnae expect t'see any soon."* Upon asking
others around the village the same question, I must conclude that the rumor was
false. This is the way of the kobolds though; they prefer to stay in small
groups in one area, staking out their prey there.

## Dwarves

A fearless and stubborn bunch, dwarves live deep within hidden and god-forsaken
places. They prefer the cool climate of these dark caves and would rather not
be bothered by the chaos that infests the surface world. Craftsmen by trade,
dwarves spend their time deep within the crypts of Abel, fashioning, refining,
and perfecting their weapons and armor. The climate here is favorable for their
trade for dwarven crafted weapons and armaments are among the finest and most
durable in the land. However, the dwarves hold their secrets close and share
their knowledge with no one. They have no written history of how they make such
grand weapons. It is rather passed on from generation to generation by word of
mouth. They are anti-social, even among their own group to an extent, and live
by quite primitive means, most likely the result of their seclusion from the
upper world. When they are not immersed in their work, they are among the
finest brewers of ale in Temuair and will often sit and enjoy their brew quite
heavily without any worries of the world above.

Dwarves have no set order of importance or rank, rather choosing to concern
themselves with perfecting their crafts then with a system of set politics.
They are distrustful of all races and to any who ventures into their realm, be
warned. They believe that those who venture that deep are only there for one
reason: to steal their secrets of weapon crafting. This is their most prized
secret and they would guard this with their lives. And they do! They ruthlessly
fend off any who make the trek down deep within the crypts. Dwarves have master
weapons and armor, drawing strength from their strong, muscular bodies. They
are slow to move, but attack with a force rivaled only by that of the
wood-goblins' mighty swing. Their weapon of choice is the ax, a symbol of
dwarven pride as they are the only ones who have been able to master the
abililty to wield this powerful weapon.

## Grimloks

Primarily miners and builders, Grimloks hone their innate skills inside the
damp caves of Pravat. They work all day and night, implementing new tunnels and
passageways through which they can better transport gems and stones back to
their base. They are hard workers, adapting quickly to their plot in life. I
myself have seen young Grimloks not more than a few Deochs old tolling
throughout the caves, working just as hard as their elders. They mine the
precious gems of beryl, coral, and ruby, which they polish and sell at fair
prices to afford the costs of new tools and clothing.

The Grimloks believe fiercely in the will of their Queen and openly defy anyone
who contradicts it. To them, the Queen's word is law and they follow it without
question. They do not openingly enjoy conflict, but because of the recent
inhabitance of their ancient enemies, the goblins, inside the caves, they must
be on guard against attack at all times. They are not strong fighters and can
only cast basic magical spells, but they have great defensive tactics, which
make them just as formidable an opponent as the goblins. They rely greatly on
each other in battle, with the more combat trained grimloks protecting those
who only know the art of mining. They use their tools as weapons, such as picks
and hammers, and can quickly escape from battle, by hiding in one of the many
tunnels they themselves have built. The caves itself is an intricate maze that
only they know the complete way through. Trying to map the inside of Pravat is
quite an impossible task to bestow upon any one person, but the Grimloks can
quickly move about the cave without hinderance. They built it in this fashion
as a natural defense against those who would dare do any harm against their
Queen, as any who enter the caves can quickly become lost or worse yet...face
to face with one of the many malevolent creatures who dwell in Pravat,
including the violent and relentless Draco. The Queen's room is at the heart of
Pravat, which is guarded by several Grimlok warriors at all times. The Queen
herself is a formidable figure, wielding a large iron rod. She will speak with
any who comes to see her, as long as they swear allegiance to her own cause. If
not, she quickly banishes them from the caves and orders all her warriors to
attack them if they are foolish enough to return.

## Faeries

The Faerie....the keepers of magic since ancient times. For this subject, I
thought it best to talk with Devlin, the fae priestess in Mileth. Many had told
me she was quite knowledgeable on the subject and would prove to be the best
source of information on this tiny race of creatures. Upon entering her study,
I looked about for her and found her making various magical potions in the back
of her room. She seemed quite willing and happy to speak with me when I asked
her and we sat down at a small research desk and begin discussing these
ethereal beings. "The faerie, or fae, have been around since the beginnings of
time...they were the ones who have preserved the art of magic throughout the
ages. They are magnificent creatures, always ready and willing to bring their
aid where ever it was needed. They are the true children of the Light, pure of
heart and without any malice...It saddens me that they are all but extinct now,
as they are kind and harmless creatures, only wishing to help others with their
magicks. The faerie have been in danger of extinction ever since the beginning
of this new age, due to the rapid changes occuring throughout Temuair." I
hastily reminded her of the ones who dwell in the Enchanted Garden and she
looked at me quite wistfully, before continuing. "Yes, you are correct. That is
the only place they dwell now...with safety...now only appearing to the kind at
heart. You certainly must have seen them...they recognize the priest's
compassion for life and will only appear to those like you. The others
paths...they see as destroyers, perhaps a threat to their existance and are
fearful of any who carry a blade. I seldom get out to see them any more though,
I am so busy now...perhaps...I hope they have not forgotten me." She then
turned away from me and looked longingly out the window, while I scribbled down
the information she had shared with me.

Thinking about my past encounters with the dark faerie deep in the woods, where
the small arial beings carried small, rune enchanted blades and cast dangerous
elemental spells at any passerby, I could not help but wonder at what had
happened to make this faerie so radically different from their kind hearted kin
who dwelled within the glades. Hesitantly, I motioned to Devlin that I wished
to ask her something and upon revealing my question to her, her look changed
quite rapidly and her eyes became clouded with both fear and shame. She thought
a long while, paused, and finally replied to my query, while I sat there in a
state of bewilderment. "The dark faerie....yes, I know of them; those dark
faerie that dwell deep in the forests...may they realize their own mistake.
They are the fae who believed in their own survival and wished not to go into
hiding as the others did. They moved deep within the woodlands and have become
dark and twisted over the eons...feeding only upon the hatred from being made
to live in such a harsh place without any contact from the outside world. They
are the dark, unspeakable cousins...the garden faerie fear them as they have
almost completely abadoned their age-old beliefs. I'm sorry...I wish not to
speak of them either...I just hope you never have the misfortune of meeting
them..." And with these words of caution, she quickly got up and continued her
previous work of making herbal potions, leaving me quite mystified. I then
realized the fae are a race with many mysteries, perhaps not to be fully
understood by any aisling or mundane alike.

_Sakura, Priestess of Deoch_

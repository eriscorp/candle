# Keeping a Pet in Temuair

_by Neacal in **Dark Ages**_

_~ From Neacal Tolfjord to the aislings of Temuair ~_

_The sparks were fading, aislings did not shine as bright as they used to.
Temuair was in dire need of something to make aisling eyes sparkle once again,
to make them dream. The countless days of fighting monsters had gotten to the
sparks, they had become dull. Then, as a divine intervention by Danaan,
something happened that brought the life back to the sparks. Something that
made them radiate from the inside with sheer joy._

_It was during the 36th deoch, not long after the discovery of the bonding
between aislings and faeries in Shinewood. One day word went around Temuair
that something new, something amazing had entered Temuair. From out of nowhere
Areini had arrived with a shipment of new pets for aislings, real huggable
pets! All aislings were ecstatic, and rushed in great numbers to Tagor, where
the new pet shop was rumored to be. But when they got there, they were faced
with another problem, which one to pick? So many adorable pets, just wishing to
be brought home._

Greetings fellow aisling. I have encountered many aislings looking for help on
raising a pet, so why not share my knowledge and help them along the way. This
lore is intended to teach young aislings how to obtain and take care of a pet,
not to mention aiding them in deciding which one will suit them the best.
However, the final decision is entirely yours to make.

I will consider the pets in two different groups. Personal pets and Novelty
pets. Which type you want is purely depends on your personal taste. Most
aislings would only be interested in the novelty pets, but for the sparkly
aisling, a personal pet might prove to be a much more enriching relationship.

## Personal pets

Personal pets are just that, your personal companion along your travels and
hardships in Temuair. Nothing warms an aisling heart and enhance a spark more
than knowing you are never alone.

In order to obtain a personal pet like this, you have to do the following.
First you find a pet without an owner. Usually those can be found around the
various hunting areas in Temuair and are for the most part free, good thing!
Now once you have found the pet, your first task is to dedicate room in your
pack for it. Pets are not items, so you cannot keep moving them to a new spot
whenever you feel like it. They need to be kept in an orderly manner. After
your pet is settled down, you can start the initiation process. If you have
ever had any kind of imaginary friend as a young mundane, you will know that
the most important thing is to name your pet. The naming process is where your
spark is bonded with the pet. Once you can call it by name, it will always be
there to talk if you need it. However, one of several drawbacks with imaginary
pets is that not all will talk to you. Some of these pets are simply not
intelligent enough to talk. So you have to settle for a one way communication
stream.

Now the biggest problem with this kind of pets is also one of the bigger
blessings. If you should be so unfortunate and have a close encounter with a
monster, that ends up sending you to Sgrios, your pet will not follow you.
Instead most of them will stay behind and guard the items you did not bring
with you. Now, if you had a ferocious pet killer cherry, that would be a good
thing, since no one would dare to steal your items. However, since most
imaginary pets are not overly active, more often than not you will find them
simply discarded by the thief. Or even worse, stolen! So take care of your
imaginary pet and do not forget to return and save your pet from a nasty fate
if you happen to leave it behind unintentionally. Other than the occasional
retrieval of your pet, there is no upkeep with imaginary pets. They will eat,
or at least try to, any food you give them, but they will not complain if you
forget to feed them for extended periods of time.

## Novelty pets

These are what most aislings think of when they hear pets. Areini, the pet shop
owner in Tagor, sells a number of pets that are not only good companions, but
also a novelty you can use to show off to your friends. These pets are in many
ways different from the personal pets described above. Other aislings will be
able to see them, pet them and, if they are crazy enough, talk to them. Not all
aislings name their novelty pets, since many consider them just novelties and
nothing else. Just like only very few warriors name their swords, and those
that do, can be considered to keep swords as pets too. However, once again,
naming your pet is essential to obtain a good relationship with your pet, and
not just consider it an item like any other. One good thing about the novelty
pets is that they are not only a good companion and something you can show off,
they are also extremely loyal. They will follow you through anything, and when
you lose everything else, your pet will still be there to keep you company and
comfort you. Granted, this will leave your belongings unguarded if you happen
to die. But nothing is preventing you from keeping both an imaginary pet and a
novelty pet at the same time. That way you will not only have someone guarding
your belongings, you will also have someone to keep you company while walking
back to retrieve them.

Now, for the price. Of course loyalty never comes cheap. In this case Areini
have to specially breed the pets to teach them the right amount of aisling
dependencies right from birth. This is a costly process, so she will ask of you
to make a donation if you wish to adopt a pet. Also, the demand of pets is
large indeed. So she rarely have time to leave the shop with all those pets to
feed and groom. Because of that, she will ask aislings adopting pets to provide
enough supplies to breed the next generation.

Of course these supplies vary depending on what pet you wish to adopt. I have
compiled a list so you can save some time and have it ready when you go visit
Areini. However, it might be a bit risky to collect all of it for one pet. You
never know which one will catch your heart when you go to adopt one. Each pet
is unique, so I will let Areini do her sales speech when you get there.
However, the supplies she ask for is always the same so I will list those.

### Pet Dog

_The pet dog is an average pet, requireing little attention and just a walk
every now and then. As of right now, all of Areini's breeding stock is pure
white. Maybe they are all descendants of BioMagus's puppy, but how Areini
managed to get another for breeding purposes is still unknown. If you are an
aisling who does not wish too much responsibility, a dog would be a wise
choice._

To adopt a pet dog you need to supply Areini with a piece of beef, chicken, a
baguette, an apple, a vegetable and a jar of royal honey. As well as 5 million
coins to cover unexpected expenses. Most of the supplies can be gotten from the
Mileth tavern and bakery, so they should not be a problem. However the honey is
a bit more tricky. I would suggest asking for godly help on this. Royal honey
comes from the rarest of queen bees, and only the all knowing would know where
to find those. You should go to the Cail temple in Undine and ask for help. If
Cail is not answering you, you can ask a Cail follower to help you. However, do
not just beg, be nice and offer them a trade for something they need.

### Pet Mongrel

_Mongrels are a lot like dogs, they are slightly smaller and a bit more greyish
in color. However, unlike the dogs, mongrels are rather aggressive. So it takes
an aisling with a firm temper to keep one in check. If you are soft and really
hate having your sleep interrupted by barking, a mongrel is not for you._

To adopt a pet mongrel, you only need to donate two green grapes, as well as 5
million gold coins. This is one of the easier things to get. The grapes can be
found in Shinewood, from the 9th area through to the 13th. Kill off a few bugs
there and you should get them. Just watch out for the green wasps. If you are a
young inexperienced aisling, those might turn out to be quite a problem to deal
with.

### Pet Blob

_Blobs are amazingly easy to take care of. In fact, they pretty much take care
of themselves. However, since they pretty much devour anything in their path
you have to be careful where you put it. You do not want a pet blob if you are
messy and do not keep order in your pack, since one day you might return from a
hunt, only to find that your blob slobbed all over the loot making it
worthless._

This is one of the more expensive pets. You will need to donate 10 million
coins to adopt one. In addition to that, you will need to provide Areini with
two slugs of Goo, as she use that to farm the pet Blobs. The Goo can be found
in Abel Dungeon. You might be able to salvage some from the corpse of blobs
there. Again, this is rather dangerous and you need to be a certain insight to
even enter the dungeon.

### Pet Bunny

![bunny](images/neacal_pet_bunny.webp)

_Pet bunnies are soft, very soft. They are hard to keep still but in general
just likes to jump around in the grass. However, you do not want to let two of
them stay together for too long. I suggest taking Areini's warning seriously,
or you might just end up with a lot of bunnies to take care of._

This is the most prized pet sold in the shop. It will cost you 25 million, yes
quite a lot, in donation. As well as Tangerines and a Rambutan. The two fruits
can be found in Shinewood. The tangerines same place as the green grapes and
the rambutan in the deepest areas. The rambutans are found near very dangerous
monsters, and I would advice you to ask a master if you can have one. They are
not uncommon, so it is likely they can give you one.

### Pet Cat

_Cats are solitary pets, they mind their own business and require very little
attention. They are happy if they are left alone to catch some rats every now
and then.However, cats are said to have a influence on the dark arts. So if you
are tilting slightly to the dark side, maybe you should adopt a cat._

In order to adopt a cat, you will have to donate 10 million coins. You will
also have to provide Areini with 10 spider silks. She uses those to make balls
of silk for the kitties to play with. The silks can be found near spiders in
the Mileth crypt, from 3rd level and down, or in the studies in House Macabre
where they are found near green widows.

### Pet Floppies

_Floppies require a lot of attention, and as the only pet available with
different looks for the genders. Floppies are very social creatures. They like
spending time with other floppies, so if you get one, you should make sure it
has regular contact with other floppies. A floppy would be a perfect pet for
the inactive chatty aislings, since they like nothing more than getting to
waddle around while you are chatting._

These come in both genders. Slightly different in color. Both will require a
donation of 15 million coins to adopt. The female floppies like to eat roses,
so you will have to bring Areini a basket of those for a female. The males like
cornucopia, so you have to bring that instead. The roses can be bought in the
Suomi tavern and the cornucopia bouquet can be bought in the Piet inn.

### Pet Ducklings

_While the other pets are all just one, ducklings are more than that! Its a
whole bunch of them. Where they come from is a mystery though, no one has ever
seen a duck in Temuair. That means noone knows what happens to those that are
no longer ducklings, so take good care of them if you adopt some. They like
steady aislings, because running in line behind an overly active aisling is not
only confusing, but also impossible at time. If you get ducklings, try to walk
in straight lines._

These are another expensive pet. You need to donate 25 million to adopt a
batch. But remember you get more than one! You also need to provide Areini with
a large feather, which she will use to make comfortable nests for the ducklings
to hatch in. The feathers can be found around Churubs near Oren and Nobis.
Again, these are dangerous, so it might be good to go in a group, or ask
masters for help if you are a young aisling.

Apart from the pets sold in the pet shop in Tagor, there are two other ways to
obtain a pet. There is a penguin and a faerie. To get these you pay a different
price than just supplies.

### Pet Faerie

_Faeries, being bonded to your spark are different from other pets. They mingle
with your aisling energies and create a special glow around you. The way to
take care of a faerie pet is to keep your spark shining. This are the pet for
the healthy spark._

Faeries are elusive creatures. You will not pick them as a pet, they will pick
you as owner. In order to get considered by them, you need to go to the 11th
area of Shinewood. Make sure you do not kill any faeries on the way, obviously
that would ruin your chance of winning their trust. Once you are there and
talking to the mother fae. She might tell you that a fae have spotted your
spark and wish to bond with you. If you are lucky, you can accept the offer and
a faerie will become your pet. However, the bonding between your spark and the
faerie does not go unnoticed. It will reduce your stamina and mental capacity
slightly `((reduce both hp and mp by 250))`. This might not seem like much for
a master, but for a younger aisling, it will be a lot. Up to you to decide if
you wish to pay the price.

### Pet Penguin

_The penguin is in its own element when its cold. They like the cold weather of
the mountains, but a cool breeze at the sea would do them well too. However,
they do not like too warm places, so this is not the pet for you if you like
venturing down the steamy pits of Chaos._

If you happen to find an egg without an owner, it might be able to hatch into a
pet penguin. Mind you however, not all eggs are penguin eggs.. Word goes around
that Raylan, a mundane boy often found in Mt. Giragan knows where to find them.
However, he will ask for a favor before giving you one. How to complete this
favor is up to you, not something I will cover in this lore. However, once you
have obtained a penguin egg `((they can be identified as "Pet Egg"))` you can
have Areini hatch it for you. It will take one moon to hatch after which you
can return to pick up your pet.

That concludes the novelty pet list. One thing worth noticing is that you can
only ever have one novelty pet at a time. If you regret the pet you picked, you
can return it to the pet shop and someone else can adopt it. However, Areini
has to make a living too, so you will have to pay the same price for a new pet
as if it was your first. Likewise, another aisling who wish to adopt your pet,
will have to pay as well.

As already stated, there is no limitations on what you can adopt as your pet.
Just remember to differentiate between the novelty pets and the personal pets.
The pets are always there for you, so their title of "Aisling's best friend" is
well earned. Take care of your pet and it will take care of you.As to where
Areini found the pets, or where she is from. I dont think we will ever find
out. I just know that walking around with a new pet sure make an aisling's eyes
shine brighter. I will be saying my prayers to Danaan, thanking her for leading
Areini to Temuair at the time she did.

Take care of yourself, and no less of your pet. Enjoy your adventures in the
lands of Temuair. Until next time I have knowledge to share, that is all for
now. And dont forget, pets might be good companions, but nothing beats a true
aisling friend.

_Neacal Tolfjord_

Aisling's best friend, a pet Neacal!

_~Wizard, summoner and keeper of less known knowledge_

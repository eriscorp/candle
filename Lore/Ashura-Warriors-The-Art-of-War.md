# Warriors: The Art of War

_by Ashura in **Dark Ages**_

![Warrior](images/ashura_warrior_warrior.webp)

## Basic Guide

### The Warrior Class

The warrior class is the most popular class in Tamuair.  The number of
warriors online at anytime usually doubles that of any other classes.
Relying almost exclusively on sheer strength and attack ability, they are
power-incarnate.  Warriors are able to carry super heavy armors and weapons,
and their ability to deal fatal damage in melee combat is second to none.
Hence, warriors play a vital role in a hunting party.  Morever, the warrior
class is the only class that could effectively hunt without a priest in the
party (by relying instead on magical potions).

The warrior class also enjoys a larger selection of armors and weapons.
Armors range from leather clothings to heavy platemails, and weapons range
from tiny daggers to venerable two-handed swords.  By equipping magical
items such as rings and necklaces, warriors can enhance their ability in a
variety of ways.

### Warrior Skills

Skills are the key to mastering the warrior class, and any aspiring warriors
must have good commands of their skills in order to prevail in the land of
Temuair.  Most skills require money and specific items as a token to learn.

#### Fighting skills

**Assail/Assault/Clobber/Wallop:** These are basic attack skills that can be
activated by pressing \<space bar> key.

**Rescue:** A vital skill in combat performed by facing the enemy and double
click the skill icon.  This will draw the enemy's attention on you instead
of your friends.

**Wind Blade:** One of the favorite attacks among warriors.  The force of Wind
Blade can be unleashed up to 3 tiles away from the target, and can deliver a
great deal of damage to the target.  Requires Assail of 20.

**Ao Beag Suain:** Anti-paralyze skill, allowing warriors to neutralize
paralyze attack.  Requires Rescue of 60.

**Beag Suain:** Paralyze attack is perhaps the best skill any warrior can
have.  It allows a warrior to paralyze the target for several seconds during
which the target becomes very vulnerable to any attack attempts.  Requires
Rescue of 70.

**Crasher:** The most devastating attack skill.  However, it can only be
unleashed when a warrior's health is near zero.  Hence, this attack could
either kill or get you killed.

**Two-Handed Attack:** This skill allows a warrior to wield 2-handed swords.

#### Accesory Skills

**Mend Weapon:** Allows a warrior to repair his/her own weapons.

**Tailoring:** Allows a warrior to repair his/her own armors.

**Melee Lore:** Allows a warrior to identify melee weapons.

**Armor Lore:** Allows a warrior to identify armors.

### Warrior Secrets (require mana to use)

**Taunt/Spit/Insult:** Allows a warrior to draw an enemy's attention from far
away.

**Various Lectures:** Allows a warrior to bring up windows of lectures about
lore, armors, weapons, etc. without having to visit the NPCs.

## Advanced Guide

### Know Yourself

#### 1. Know your stats.

**STR:** governs the weight you can carry and DMG.  Warriors need very high
strength.  
**INT:** governs MANA for wizard class.  
**WIS:** governs MANA for priest class.  
**CON:** governs the HP you receive when you gain an insight.  
**DEX:** governs TO HIT and ability to avoid being hit.

#### 2. Know your latency.

When you hunt, it's very important to check your latency first.  It's not a
good idea to go into a rough area when you're having bad lag.

#### 3. Know your equipments.

_Swords:_

Qualities range from **Normal** to **Good** to **Fine** to **Grand** and to
**Great**.  Weights remain the same but the amount of DMG increases with
quality of a sword.  Normal swords can be upgraded to higher quality by
skilled rogues.

Each sword does different amount of damage against small (1x1 tile size) and
large creatures.  When a sword is lored or identified the information will
be shown.  
S = damage against small creatures  
L= damage against large creatures

_Armors/Shields/Items:_

**ABUNDANCE** increases DMG by 1.  
**BLESSED** increases TO HIT by 5.  
**CAIL** increases CON.  
**CEANNLADIR** increases STR (*doesn't alter how much you can carry).  
**DEOCH** increases regeneration by 2x.  
**FIOSACHD** increases DEX.  
**GLIOCA** increases WIS.  
**GRAMAIL** increases magic resistance by 10%.  
**LUATHAS** increases INT.  
**SGRIOS** high durability.

In general, use the best weapon you can wield without compromising AC too
much.  The faster you can down a creature, the fewer chance it can do
additional damage to your party.

When you equip an armor, the action window (small panel with orange texts)
will display its stats.  
Armor = AC  
R = Regeneration rate  
S = Spell/magic resistance

### Know Your Enemy

Any seasoned warriors will readily tell you that knowing one's enemy is of
paramount.  Some monsters have the ability to cast devastating spells,
others don't but can hit very hard.  Learn the types of monsters that
inhabit the area you will venture into and equip yourself accordingly to
maximize the chance of survival.  In general, it's a good idea to equip some
GRAMAIL items when hunt in an area that contain spellcasters.  However,
equipping ABUNDANCE and BLESSED items allows quicker kill.

### Know Your Party

Hunting with the people you know always enhances safety of the entire
party.  Each individual has his/her own playing styles.  Some priests or
wizards are slow at casting (due to lag or slow reflex) hence it's good idea
not to venture around too far or too fast when hunting with such players.
Some players tend to be panic and run around when surrounded by hordes of
monsters leading to a chaotic fight that could put an end to your party.
Hence, it's a good idea to keep them in line and lure monsters away from
them.  In general, always hunt with the people you trust and feel
comfortable with.

Also know what basic spells do, especially priest spells so you know what to
ask for during battle.

**AO CRADH** (anti-curse): lifts the curse spell.  
**AO PUISIN** (anti-poison): neutralizes poison.  
**ARMACH** (armor): lowers and enhances AC.  
**BEANNAICH** (blessed): increases TO HIT.  
**CRADH** (curse): increases your AC making you easier to hit and take more
damage.  
**FAS DEIRAS** (dmg): increases DMG.  
**IOC** (heal): regains HP.  
**NAOMH AITE** (santuary): decreases damage taken.

### Know Your Skills

Skills are very important to warriors, especially attack skills.  They are
also very hard to raise, so a lot of practice is required before you can
raise a skill.  Most attack skills are activated by simply pressing \<space
bar>.  However skills such as WIND BLADE, AO BEAG SUAIN, BEAG SUAIN require
you to manually activate the icons.  Since timing is one of the most crucial
aspects in combat, it's a good idea to "hotkey" such attack skills so you
can activate them by hitting numeric keys on the keyboard.  (Go to skills
window and drag/drop icons to the top rows, the left most icon will be
activated by pressing \<1>, the next \<2>, and so on).  Important attacks are
described below.

**WIND BLADE**: one of the best early attack skills you can have.  Allows you
to attack from 3 tiles away, and does a great amount of damage per hit.

**BEAG SUAIN**: the best warrior skills in game.  Allows you to paralyze an
enemy for a few second.  A paralyzed enemy cannot attack back, hence with 2
warriors taking turn paralyzing a battle could be won without a scratch.

**CRASHER**: the most devastating warrior attack skill.  However, it requires
that your HP become near zero before you can let out a crasher, hence not
very useful in general.

### Know Your Potions

**Beothaich Deum** (red): resurrects a party member when he/she is skulled.
This is the potion you can live without, and is required in every hunting
occasion.  Note that there's also a similar looking potion (**hemloch
deum**) that heals instead of resurrects, so always have potions identified.

**Hydele Deum** (brown): revitalizes your HP by bringing it to full.  Can be a
life saver at critical times.

**Personaca Deum** (light brown): neutralizes poison.

## Advanced Tactical Combat

### Hotkey is The Key:

Always hotkeys important skills and potions.  Timing is everything in
combat, and the faster you can execute skills or maneuvers the faster you
can kill.  In critical fights, you can resurrect your dying friends or
revitalize yourself very quickly by using hotkeys.

### Always Repair:

Always and always.  In order to prevent armor and weapon breakdown, repair
at the smith or by skilled warriors with high mending and tailoring skills
(it can save you a lot of money).

### Practice and Timing:

Practice everything you have got, and do it over and over.  Learn to time
your movement from one spot to the other by holding down movement keys
(timing will be dependent on your latency).  You'll find that in critical
fight, timing is everything.

### Crucial Items: always carry them!

**Arcane Scrolls:**  
For teleporting away when overwhelmed by hordes of enemies.  Scrolls can be
made by wizard, or by praying to your god (choose supplicate, then songs of
shrine).

**Beothaich Deum:** red potions  
For resurrecting your party member when they are facing death (skulled).
The potions can be made by skilled priests.

**Hydele Deum:** brown potions  
For healing yourself when the priest runs out of mana or has no time to heal
you.  The potions can be made by skilled monks or priests.

### Use Overlaying Map: \<tab> key

Very important when venturing into a new area.  Also very important when
combating large creatures (Ogres, Dracos, etc) by allowing you to pinpoint
where to stand and attack for maximum damage.  Hint: There are a few spots
you can stand next to large creatures and attack without getting hit.  This
is the key to defeating a Draco.

### Maximizing Attack:

This is done by approaching the enemy from (behind or side ways).  Attacking
from behind or sideways can deliver up to 2x amount of regular damage.  When
you have acquired paralyze attack, always move to the enemy's side or back
and attack after it is paralyzed.  Two warriors can take turn paralyzing one
target for easy kill.

### One at a Time:

Attack the same monster or one monster at a time.  This is crucial in group
hunt when you are up against several monsters.  Concentrating the attack on
one monster allows the quickest kill and the best chance of survival for the
entire party.  Learn how to run from other monsters while landing attacks on
the targeted monster periodically.

### Protect The Healer:

Always protect your priest.  This is usually done by hitting the creature
that is chasing your priest and use rescue skill on it, although sometimes
Taunt/Spit/Insult can work as well.

### Blocking and Trapping:

Blocking is very crucial in fight.  In general, a monster will continue
fixate on and chase after a selected player.  Other players can block the
monster's path to that player and continue to fight the rest of the pack
without having to worry about that particular monster.  That monster,
therefore, is considered trapped and hence almost harmless (although high
level ones may still cast offensive spells).  Always experiment around to
see how many different formations your party can do to block and trap
monsters.

### Door Tactics:

Door tactics can be thought of as advanced blocking.  This is the favorite
pratice among experienced warriors allowing them and a priest to take on
hordes of monsters at once.  One warrior would go into the monster infested
area first and stand on either side of the entrance.  This way he gets all
the baddies to concentrate on him, and his sides that are next to the wall
and the entrance are blocked (monsters cannot approach the entrance tile,
since it's the teleport tile to the next screen).  A few seconds later, a
priest would go in and stand next to his side, thus blocking attack from one
of his sides while the entrance is blocking attack from the other side.
With 2 sides blocked, the warrior only has his front to worry about since
his back is against the wall.  Hence, he only fights one monsters at a time
and monsters would not attack the priest since they are all focused on the
warrior. _(Note: with the current patch, if you stand next to door tile for
30 seconds you'll be automatically transported in/out so this tactic doesn't
work anymore)._

### Formations:

There are several interesting tactical formations you can perform during
during combat.  Some examples are illustrated by diagrams below:

X - yourself  
O - your friends  
1,2,3,... - creatures

#### 1. Circle and Attack

```
  X
O 1 O
  2
```

With this formation,  you can avoid creature 2 by walkinng in a  circle
around your friends while landing hits on creature 1 periodically.  Your
friends only need to concentrate on creature 1, and can help paralyzing
creature 2.

#### 2. Double Paralysis

```
1 O
X 2
```

This formation allows you and your friend to concentrate attack on creature
1, while either of you can take turn and paralyze creature 2.

### Large Creatures

Large creatures such as Ogre and Draco are creatures that are larger than
1x1 tile.  It is highly recommended that you turn on mapping (\<tab>) during
fight.  Why?  Large creatures appear larger than their actual tile size and
hence you could stand on a certain spot hitting them and not knowing that
you instead are hitting the air.  Knowledge of map representation is vital
in combat against large creatures.  Examples are shown below:

R - red dot on overlaying map  
~ - space occupied by creature

#### 1. Ogre (2x2 tile)

```
R ~ w
~ ~
w
```

#### 2. Draco (3x3 tile)

```
R ~ ~ w
~ ~ ~
~ ~ ~
w
```

You can anywhere facing these tiles (R and X) to attack.  However, large
creatures hit very hard and one hit will usually take out more than half
HP.  Hence, it's very important not to stand next to R (red dot on map
screen) since the creature can only hit whoever is next to R (though it can
still attack anywhere with spells).  Also note that you can land windblade
only if you line yourself up with R (windblade will not work with other
spots).   And since standing next to R is not recommended, there are only 2
spots left that you can stand fight and windblade the creature (marked by
w).

Certain creatures have smart AI (artificial intelligence) and will run upon
low HP.  This requires the entire team effort to block and prevent them from
running away.  Draco is a good example.  It has humongous amount of HP and
incredible regeneration rate.  A Draco will usually regain most of its HP if
allowed to run away for a few seconds.  Hence, the key to beating this type
of creature is to attack non-stop and prevent it from running away.

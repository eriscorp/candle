# Dragon Scale Sword Quest

_by Karasi in **Dark Ages**_

_Quests for Insights 20+_

Start this quest in the Mileth Tavern. In the east corner is an old beggar
named Theradus who will tell you about the Dragon Scale Sword, but only for a
price...

## Map of the Shinewoods

- SW ~ 8 Green Hydra. (drops dragon scale sword)
- SW ~ 11 Faerie ~ Gives spells and you might be granted a faerie pet! Just
  greet her with a nice "Hello".
- SW ~ 17 Herbal Shop. Qaeli the shop owner will help you with mixing the
  potions you need, just bring the ingredients required.
- SW ~ 34 Giant Ant.
- SW ~ 27 Red Mantis.
- SW ~ 30 Shinewood Home. Codus would like to know what you are seeking, say
  "Creants" to him.
- SW ~ 31 Three headed hydra is here by a stone shaped crypt.
- SW ~ 35 Lady Phoenix Creant.

Here is a map of the Shinewoods, the creatures cast mor cradh, ard cradh and

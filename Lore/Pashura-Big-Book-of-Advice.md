## Introduction

Welcome to the Big Book of Advice: An Aisling Guide to Etiqutte.

The purpose of this book is to teach proper conduct within the land of Dark
Ages.

Etiquette is something that is often forgotten. This guide will help you
understand what behavior is accepted and what is frowned upon.

Please don't forget to read the section on frequently asked questions. These
are questions asked by aislings with real concerns.

I hope you enjoy reading these pages as much as I enjoyed writing them.

-Pashura  
Loyal To Cail

## Chapter One

_Social Skills: The Harmony Of Cail_

### Overview

This section covers the basics of how to interact with others and conduct
yourself in public.

This is the etiquette of proper communication and appearance.

### General Interactions

Always remember: What you say can be heard.

Whenver you speak to an aisling for the first time, greet them. Do not ask them
a question without a proper greeting.

Make conversation! Nothing is more boring then only talking about game
mechanics. If you wish to only speak of game mechanics, please spare the ears
of those around you and take it to a private conversation.

Greet your guild when you awaken!

Always remember to be patient, many cannot type quickly, but that does not mean
what they have to say isn't as important as the fastest speaker!

When you speak, realize that these are "Dark Ages" the expressions of today are
not viable in the ancient land of Temuair.

### Aisling Services

Need items dedicated to a God? Need a gem polished or a fine armor tailored to
meet your favorite color?

These are just some of the services that aislings can provide. Here is the code
of conduct:

1. Greet the one you wish aid from.
2. Ask if they are able to provide the service you ask of them.
3. Offer something in return for the time spent helping you. Even if it's an
   offer of services in return.

4. Thank them.

Always remember that even if the aisling is able to provide the service you
request, does not mean they wish to run to your aid at that moment.

Be polite and patient.

### Guild Etiquette

Finding a guild: If you have to beg to be in a guild, then most likely you
don't belong in it.

Creating a guild: It takes a great amount of time to form a guild, patience is
required. When you need to support the formation of your guild, do not ask
those that are already in a guild for they cannot support yours.

Leaving a guild: Do not leave without telling at least one member of your
guild. You can easily pigeon mail someone if they are not awake.

Use common courtesy.

### Struck With Boredom?

You stand around, waiting for a hunt, waiting for your love, waiting for an
event. What do you do?

Here is what not to do:

Do not hit others. Do not block areas. Do not clutter areas with items.

What to do if you are bored:

Create a story. Get others interested. (Chances are they might be bored too.)
Learn more about the land. Teach a new student. Join politics. Ask strangers if
they need help.

### Speaking In Public

Oh the perils of speaking in public!

Avoid using abbreviations for words. Avoid using all upper case letters. Avoid
using "yell" unless what you need to say is an emergency. Avoid saying the same
thing over and over again.

Above all, remember if you are having an ((Out of Character)) conversation do
not continue it publicly. Using the brackets three (3) times is considered the
limit. You can always take it to a group conversation or with whispers.

Do not abused ((Out of Character)) ways of speech.

### Appearance

Armor makes the ordinary aisling extraordinary!

The armor/clothes you wear defines many things about you. It tells the ones
around you what insight you have achieved and your style!

Do not walk around without clothes on by choice. It is considered rude and
unacceptable.

Take pride in what you wear, keep your armor in excellent condition.

Wear appropriate armor for events and special occasions.

In case of death, always keep a spare set of armor/clothes in your vault. This
avoids any embarassment you might face and helps you to retrieve your items all
the faster.

## Chapter Two

_Learning: The Spark Of Deoch_

### Overview

This chapter will discuss the etiquette of learning.

There is much to learn and understand in the realm of Temuair.

You must strive to be eager and persistant to find out the truths that exist.

### Where To Learn

Learning is a challenge all of us face. You must not get discouraged.

To become a successful Aisling you must learn to read everything you can,
listen to everything that is said, and observe things around you.

Speak to the mundanes, speak to your mentor, speak to anyone that can provide
you with a reasonable answer.

Visit the library in Loures, spend days reading and learning the works of so
many that have contributed.

Ask questions! If you do not ask, you will not learn. Asking a silly question
when a youngster is much easier then to do something foolish at a higher
insight.

Be curious.

### Finding The Right Mentor

Your teacher, your guide and your mentor. Who should you choose?

Your mentor should be one with the same values as you. For example, if you are
a priest that believes in good and light, you will probably not wish to have a
priest of death and decay teach you.

Look at the legend of your mentor. You will see the experiences he/she has.

Get a recommendation from friends you have met. Your mentor is one you will
need to trust for advice and their knowledge.

If your mentor does not provide you with the answers you need, take on a new
mentor. Always remember to contact old/new with changes you are about to make.

### Becoming A Good Mentor

Experience, knowledge, charisma and availibility is what you need...

Experience - You must know your way around Temuair. Hands on experience.

Knowledge - You must understand Temuair. You must know the requirements or
techniques on getting new spells/skills or strategy.

Charisma - You must evoke the spark of your student. Find the sparkle in their
eyes and merge your energy with theirs.

Availibility - Before taking on a new student make sure you have the time to
spend with them. Show them the world. Open the world to them.

In other words, be there for your students.

### The Spark Of Learning

Upon awaking in the land of Temuair with your blouse or shirt on, you are given
the spark of learning.

While your mind swirls at your new surroundings, you will be able to absorb the
sights and sounds.

Wake up with an open mind. Feel the sights and sounds of the light and dark
around you.

Be curious, ask questions. Take your time, you have plenty of it.

## Chapter Three

_Hunting: The Strength Of Ceannlaidir_

### Overview

Hunting is an integral part of your journey in the land of Temuair.

You will want to hunt for companionship, experience and knowledge.

You will make some of your best friends and worst enemies while hunting.

Never feel obligated to hunt. Hunt at your own pace, insight only means
importance to those that don't have anything to fall back on.

### Finding A Good Group

A good group consists of reliable members that will be by your side in times of
adventure and still by your side when they are screaming "RUN!"

You will wish to find aislings around the same insight as you. Experience is
divided equally and you will not be hunting with someone who claims to know
everything.

The most common place to find ones looking for a hunt is in the Eastern
Woodlands.

Be polite, discuss where you want and/or can hunt and who you already are
grouped with.

If you don't trust the one you are about to group with, stay back and find a
group that makes you feel good.

### Knowing Your Limits

Warriors: Are they strong enough to do damage and the skill to protect you?

Priests: Do they have enough mana to heal you and the group through a series of
difficult combats? Are their healing spells high enough that you will survive?

Rogues: Are they experienced enough to know when to "hide" and brave enough to
stay behind to save members of your group when everyone else is fleeing?

Monks: Are their skills high enough so that they are able to provide enough
damage and survive an attack?

Wizards: Are they reliable to run away from attacking creatures while casting
spells?

Know your group, don't go into a dangerous situation, unless you want to take a
chance with your life.

### Interacting With Others

Communication is a very important part in any group.

Listen to those around you. If your Priest tells you; "No, I don't want to go
in there." then heed the warning.

If you are a Priest healing, then make certain you tell your group your mana
status before running out. This gives them the chance to decide if running is
an option or not.

Make sure everyone has red potions before entering into any dangerous zone!
Remember if you have red potions and your group doesn't, they can't aid you
when you are skulled!

Don't be afraid to ask for healing, but in the heat of battle if your priest is
struggling to keep healing everyone, let them heal. They will get around to
casting other enhancements on you.

### Items Found/Dropped

Treasure, but how do you share?

First, you will want to have one aisling to gather the items/coins, preferably
one that everyone trusts.

The coins are to be divided equally after the hunt is over.

As for items found or dropped by creatures, it is bad eitquette to just grab
things. Let the one who identifies the object pick it up and tell the group
what it is. From there it can be decided who is to get what. Usually after the
hunt is the best time.

Don't get greedy! Share with your group. Everyone deserves something for
risking themselves hunting.

### Equipment And Items

Here is an inventory for your hunt:

\-> Red potions (As many as you can carry.)  
\-> Brown potions (Hydele) (Always bring spares, just in case.)  
\-> Proper equipment (All properly mended.)  
\-> A good attitude (Boring is just boring.)  
\-> Prayer necklaces (When in doubt, pray.)  
\-> Scrolls (There is no place like home.)

You will want to coordinate with the other members of your group to make sure
everyone is as prepared as they should be.

## Chapter Four

_Greed: The Wealth Of Fiosachd_

### Overview

While what is in your pockets can buy you all in the world, it cannot buy you
friendship, loyalty or the sincerity of others.

Those nice little coins, you need them desperately, you need a new spell, to
mend your armor, to buy a gift for your significant other.

This chapter will discuss how to handle your funds and the proper way of going
about getting them.

### Gaining Wealth

As a young aisling, getting coins can be quite the challenge. You will need to
work hard to earn your way.

Seek your mentor. Your mentor can be a source of a loan until you can slip into
your boots.

After your 2nd circle you will find that coins can be had much more freely.

Hunting is primarily the best way to gain wealth. As your insights increase,
the more coins the beasts you slay will drop.

Through the wealth of Fiosachd and prayer to him, you can also obtain a healthy
sum of money.

If in doubt, find items and sell them! For example; wolf skins or furs do not
sell for that much but if you bring a sackful to the mundane, you will leave
with a heavy pocket!

### Spending Your Wealth

You leave a hunt with a pocket full of coins, what should you do?

I highly suggest saving some for a rainy day. Have some stored so that if an
emergency comes up you are able to purchase what you need.

Think ahead! If you are in the market for a new item, checkout current selling
prices. No one wants to overpay for something.

Be generous! Do you see someone that has just perished? Offer to buy them
something to help them. A little thought goes a long way!

### Dealing With Mundanes

Banking: No matter which bank you choose to store your goods in, you will pay a
hefty price. Always remember to ask yourself if the price of storing an item is
really worth it.

Pigeon Mail: Those strong, fast pigeons can be quite quick and reliable for
those that are able to pay their hefty fees. For all precious items, hand
deliver them please, pigeons are not known for their reliability.

Smithy Shops: You have the choice of mending all your items at once or each
item one by one. It might be more time consuming to mend each item on it's own,
but then you are given prices.

### Proper Etiquette

Begging is something frowned upon within the land of Temuair. Do not beg, it is
not accepted.

If a hunt goes bad for any number of reasons, always make sure the money is
divided even if it's at a later date.

Choose your investments wisely. Do not loan your hard earned money to those you
do not know well.

Using your coins to buy your friends an ale at the local pub is always
accepted.

## Chapter Five

_Religion: The Knowledge Of Luathas_

### Overview

This chapter will cover the basics of religion with regards to asking for
services and joining/leaving a fellowship.

Religion is a very large part of a character's essence. It is what they believe
in, and shows to the world your true nature.

### Joining A Fellowship

If you are a priest, you can initiate yourself, which is convenient, but please
make sure you contact one of the higher priests in that fellowship. This makes
sure you are one of the recognized priests and it is just proper.

Some religions are easier to join then others. No matter which you choose, make
sure you have spoken to one of the shrine assistants and know of the Gods and
the history.

To join a religion you will want to contact a Priest to initiate you. Be
patient and respectful at all times.

Don't demand or expect to join a fellowship.

Do not join a religion for the wrong reasons! It is difficult leaving a faith,
especially if you are a priest!

### Mass Etiquette

Mass is a special event where a priest can praise the God they serve with their
whole fellowship.

Mass takes time. Be patient! Do not disrupt! Show your respect at all times.
Offer your help.

Proper attire is important. Ask your faith's priest what is acceptable and what
isn't.

Be on time! This way the ceremony can be held in a timely fashion.

Be quiet! Take your conversations to whispers.

Do not cast spells (for outdoor mass)!

Remember mass takes much effort on the parts of the priests, do not ruin what
they are trying to create.

### Religion Goodies

Ah gifts from the Gods... Care to remove this scar?

Special entreats from Gods take faith. Some take much more faith then others.
Asking a priest to consecrate 10 items to their God is abusing them. So don't
do it.

The best time to ask for scar removal is after mass.

A Geas is a religious quest that differs per each religion. However it is
something you earn, not something that is owed to you.

Always be polite in asking for the favors from the Gods.

Faith is important to everyone, do not abuse those who have it.

### Finding A Priest For Your Religious Needs

Have a horrible scar that you need removed? Mayhaps an item that needs
consecrated?

Those are just a few of the many different things that can be done with the
help of faith.

Always be polite, never "expect" immediate service.

Remember to not always put all the burden on the priests. Most things can be
done by all of the congregation. With the exception of consecrations and
desecrations.

It takes time and faith for the favors of the church. Be polite and show your
dedication.

### Donations

Know the allies and enemies of your faith.

In order for your fellowship's priests to keep their faith high and provide
services to you, they must desecrate items of their enemy fellowships.

Remeber - Priests are usually rather weak in strength. Arrange with your priest
first, to see what he/she can hold.

Be considerate when asking for services. Do not be selfish, offer something
that provides faith rather then takes it.

Have spare objects from an enemy fellowship? You can leave the item in the
shrine for a priest to desecrate, or use a pigeon to send the item.

### Religious Hierarchy

Internally within any of the religions, there is a hierarchy. This is usually
determined by deochs in the fellowship and/or dedication and wisdom.

Respect the hierarchy. When looking in the events of Temuair, you will see a
list of religious mass times. Usually the priests that give mass have special
ranking within the fellowship.

These "mass" priests will be able to help you understand the sermons and/or
actions within that fellowship.

You'll want to get to know all the priests of your fellowship, young and old,
for I am quite certain you will need their assistance at some point.

### Leaving A Fellowship

There are many reasons to leave a fellowship. You have but a few choices on how
to depart from it.

Priests - Leaving a fellowship is very difficult, you must forsake your God and
wait a time before you can follow another. To do this you must find another
priest to take your place within your current fellowship.

Take the time to find someone to replace you. Once you do make sure it is known
who has replaced you.

Excommunication - Oh the easy way out. Easy for you that is. Difficult on the
one who does the work. In general do not go through the trouble of trying to
get excommunicated. You'll just make more enemies then you ever desired.

Choose a faith wisely, so that you can be honorable in your actions towards
your fellowship.

### Etiquette On Choosing A Faith

Ah down to the nitty gritty.

Which religion is the one to choose?

Read - Learn about the Gods, what they represent, what they are.

Talk - Speak to the ones that are already in the fellowship, attend mass.

Good Vs. Evil - Religion gives you the best excuse in the world to roleplay.
Whether good or evil, your religious beliefs play a big role in who you are.

Understand - Know what you are talking about. Don't claim that a religion is a
certain way without finding out for sure.

Be respectful at all times, voice your opinions, but at the proper time.

## Chapter Six

_Courtship: The Love Of Glioca_

### Overview

Glioca has given us the chance to fall in love.

Love has many complications, from finding the right mate to knowing when it is
time to end it.

Always remember this is roleplaying, you are falling in love with the character
in front of you, not the one behind the computer. You can never know for sure
how old the one you are playing with is, nor exactly what gender they really
are.

### Finding Your Love

Your love can be your hero(ess), your confidant, your soulmate, your hunting
partner or even your nemesis.

Love blooms in many different places. It usually strikes when you are not
looking.

Where to find your love? In your heart.

You won't find your love by asking anyone and everyone in the city of Mileth to
marry you.

You will find your love in someone you spend time with, take your time, go
slow, love is a beautiful ride that should be enjoyed by both of you.

### Dealing With Rejection

Rejection comes in many forms, each one of them painful enough to make your
tears turn into a river of heartache.

Rejection could bring anger. Keep calm. Take your anger out on creatures
instead of the one who rejected you, your guildmates or friends.

Keep strong - Your tears are not of shame. Your tears are of heartache.

Ponder - Think of what happened, the good times, the bad times and the reasons
for the final blow to your heart.

Smile - Smile in the face of this beast of sorrow, for the smile will bring
others to your side and before you know it your wounds will mend.

### Weddings / Fae Love

You are in love, you want to admit your love to the whole world.

This is a choice that both of you have to make. Talk it over, weddings and fae
love are both different, but are both considered a legal bonding.

Wedding - The "official" way of joining together. This is done in the Mileth
church and once the ceremony is over your legend will show "married to <>."

Fae Love - This is also an official way to join to your lover. However it is a
ritual of love, depending on the ceremony it can mean - love, friendship,
adoration. Once completed your legend will show "Loves <>."

I would suggest that if you plan on doing both the Fae and the Church wedding,
that you do one at a time, for they are each their own event. Fae love coming
first.

### Courtship

Your love is sparked, let the courtship begin.

First, make sure the one that you are spending time courting is actually
interested.

Ladies - Give the special male aisling in your life objects of affection. Males
usually like more useful items such as staves, armor, clothes and so on. An
occasional vase of flowers will definitely get his attention!

Gentlemen - Your special lady in your life wants attention, she was poetry and
flowers. She wants useful items and items that are beautiful. She wants to be
surprised and felt loved at every chance you able to.

Don't be predictable. Find your creativity to give your mate everything you
can.

### Divorce / Break-Up

So heartbreaking when a Fae love or marriage falls apart.

The break-up will be permanently on your legend until you find a new love.

Make Things Easier:

- Do not break-up without discussing it with your mate first.
- Do not break-up when your mate is not awake.
- If you have fallen in love with another do not show your love publicly until
  your current relationship has ended.
- Be certain you wish to end the relationship. Make sure you try to make it
  work. Seek counsel from your friends and your congregation.
- Never let anything that was said Out Of Character become public or your
  reasons for the break-up.

### Showing Affection

You are in love, you want to tell the world - but don't disgust them.

Romance is a beautiful thing, but do not attempt to make all of it public.

Blowing Hearts - Blowing hearts when you meet, when you part and when it is
merited is beautiful. Blowing hearts until you are out of breath and blue in
the face is not for the public's view.

Love And Hunting - Think of others around you. Mayhaps they haven't found a
love of their own? Do not ignore them because you are with yours. You are in a
group, be part of that group, be respectful to all.

### Is It Love Or Friendship?

Your heart races each time you see her/him. You feel your hands get moist and
your face get hot. Is it love?

- Be observant. If you are the only one blowing hearts, there might be a
  problem.
- Conversations are supposed to be with both contributing. Are you the only one
  talking?
- Are you being used? Watch for signs of being used. Do they only call upon you
  when they need something?
- Avoiding the subject of love and commitment. Are you the only one talking
  about a wedding?

Please remember to use your common sense. Love can make your eyes go blind.

### Abandonment

You awaken one morning, rub your eyes to see if your legend has changed...

Your love broke up with you. Tis a very sad day indeed.

Remember you cannot expect your love to wait for you for all eternity. If for
some reason you are no longer available or rarely awaken, your love will
experiance loneliness and possibly fall in love with another.

Never abandon someone without telling them. If they have left Temuair then that
is the only reason to not contact your love.

Remember to always try to talk things over before settling things with a
divorce or break-up.

Love is beautiful, love is blind.

## Chapter Seven

_Politics: The Power Of Gramail_

### Overview

For various reasons, becoming involved in politics can almost be a necessity.

Supporting the right candidate as well as being the right candidate is all very
important.

Use your votes wisely, who you put in office can also make your Asling life
very challenging.

Politics inevitably becomes a struggle of who has the most power and support.

### Taking The Big Step

Become a politician for the right reasons.

Have clear opinions and directions on where you think your city should be.

Speak with the current politicians, to see if they will prove to be an
opposition or an ally as to what you wish to accomplish.

Become very aware of what has happened in your city previously. This will avoid
serious attacks on your lack of knowledge.

Have your guard up all the time. The public is watching you at all times.

Remember, Aslings have a very good memory they will remember your conduct even
when you were a wee one.

Good luck.

### Gathering Support

Never Beg For Support.

Always be prepared to answer questions regarding the position you are seeking.

Make your views known in a manner that approve effective. Posting on a board
that you are running for a political office is accepted, posting it more then
once every moon is not.

Think ahead. Try to have some extra support in case of necessity.

Be polite, there is plent of support to be had, there is never any reason to be
rude or demanding.

Never use macros to gain your support. Show you are willing to go the distance
by being sincere and not lazy!

### Being In Office

There is one prime directive to remember when you are in office.

**You are always on duty.**

No matter where you are, no matter what you are doing, you are on duty.

The Aslings supported you, you will be there for them.

If for any reason you are unable to go to them, then find someone on duty that
can.

When bringing forth a punishment or a warning, do it publicly and idealy with
another representative of your city.

It is completely against any rules of conduct to bribe and/or accept bribery
while being in office.

### Power Struggles

Heed this warning:

**Be prepared for conflict.**

You will get into arguments. You will be spending a great amount of time
arguing and doing research on the aislings of your city.

Everyone has their own opinion on laws, punishment and general conduct.

Always keep calm, do not act in haste.

Keep to the facts, don't be afraid to state your opinion at anytime, but listen
to what your opponent is telling you.

### Wearing Your Uniform

Congratulations, you have made it in office. You are now the proud owner of
your political garment.

Your new garment is too precious to deposit in a vault and the mundanes will
take it from you when your office term has been completed.

Political garments are considered formal.

Wearing your political uniform for hunting is not appropriate.

Your uniform is deserving of respect, treat others with respect when wearing
it.

Never, ever wear your office uniform when you are not in your body. ((afk))

### Knowing When To Leave Politics

You find yourself standing near a board, all you are doing is complaining to
your companions, your fellow officers and your guildmates.

It is now time to take a break from being in office.

When an aisling is frustrated they can become very irrational. Take a break.

The last thing you want to happen is to have the mundanes remove you from
office.

Remember even if you are not in office, you are a citizen, and your voice still
counts.

### Ousting A Bad Seed

Do not attack an opponent or remove an aisling from office unless you have
tried every other option.

Politics can be a dirty struggle of power. Do not play that part. Make your
views clear and why you will attack one of the officers of your city.

Do not let this become personal. You should be removing someone for their
conduct or behavior. Not because you do not like them or have an argument.

Use your judgement wisely, for the one you attack might be the one to attack
you next.

## Chapter Eight

_Community Boards: The Chaos Of Sgrios_

### Overview

This Section deals with the your responsibilty towards public posting.

Expressing yourself is your right and a large part of life in Temuair. However,
many forget that posting over and over is more of a nuisance then it is an
expression.

Don't be fooled by your own reasons for expressing your views.

### Etiquette On Posting

Have a purpose.

Do not personally attack anyone on the community boards. Attack their opinion
or stance or their poetry, do not attack their being.

Do not reply twice to the same thread of discussion, be considerate to others
that are trying to read.

Does your post **need** to be on a public board? Or will sending a private pigeon
mail be more then adequate?

Be respectful at all times.

Use punctuation and carriage return. Help those reading your posts, let them
know when a sentence begins/ends and when a new subject begins.

### Where To Post

The community boards are divided by what topic is involved.

Merchant boards are for posting items for sale or wanted.

Political boards are for the discussion of laws and who is running for office.

Community boards are for discussions of events, a place to express your views
on situations that have happened, to express your creativity with poetry and/or
love and for your opinion.

College Boards are for the posting of classes and/or announcements.

Religious boards are to deal with only the fellowship's day to day activity. Do
not post about an event or anything else without the consent of one of the high
priests.

### Using Pigeon Mail

Why post something publicly directed at one individual when you can use a
pigeon to mail them privately?

Do not clutter the boards with a personal argument. Take that privately.

The boards are to discuss the issues at hand, not to attack the very character
of the aisling that has that view.

Be responsible for your posts. Think twice before sending it publicly.

There is more strength in fewer words that mean something, then many words that
have no meaning at all.

## Conclusion

Etiquette is a very serious topic in the land of Temuair.

Always remember to treat everyone with respect, even if you are playing an evil
character.

Keep things simple for those just learning. Don't confuse them with too much
information at once.

Always use your creativity and your originality.

Listen with an open mind, things are not always as they appear.

You are being watched.

-Pashura

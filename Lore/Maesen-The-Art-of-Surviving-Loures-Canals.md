# The Art of Surviving Loures Canals

_by Maesen in **Dark Ages**_

_A true masterpiece._

## Introduction

Deoch 55, Fall.. Fate drove us Aislings' to search every square inch of the
Loures Market Place, though ill-fated. Upon reaching Loures Shop 12, secured
tightly in the back of the Market Place.. friend and foe alike would discover
more. A terrifying discovery in-which many would journey forth and many more
would lose their lives.

9th Moon, 12th Day: Aislings' weren't ready for the horrors that lay deep
within' the confined walls of the sewers below. Bone-chilling curdles beckoned
from the stairs, that jutted up from the floor of the Shop, welcoming anyone
who was up for the challenge. Amidst the twisted pipe and fowl murky waters
lay a new world left in the hands of the bold and brave to conquer. A new
chapter written in the history of Temuair. But, the story has only just
begun..

## Chapter 1: How do I get there?

So you need to find your way to the Loures Canals? No problem. There are two
different ways to get there, which I will cover briefly.

The first and fastest way: Make your way to Pravat Cave. Once there exit to
the south. Once you have done this, make your way to Loures Castle. Heading
north, you will run into an extravagant fountain. Turn south. Heading
southwards through the path, you will reach the Loures Market Village.
Continue south until you reach a crossroads. Here you'll want to continue your
way west. You'll notice a large building with a fine sign out front. You're
there!

The second and slower way: Make your way to Pravat Cave. Once there again exit
to the south. Once you have done so, make your way to Loures. Not the castle!
This will bring you to Loures Castle Way. Head up past the Temple of Gramail,
up the path into Loures Entrance Yard. Once there, continue northward past the
gates into Loures Market Village. Once there, head westbound dodging all the
buildings. Continue northward up the main path until you find a building in
the northwest corner with a fine sign outside of it. You're there!

Now go inside. To your left, you'll see the entrance to Loures Canals. To your
right, you'll see a lovely banker mundane by the name Kasumk.

Now when you enter the canals, you have to be grouped or it won't let you in.
I'd suggest talking to Kasumk and persuading him to hold your valuables
because it's really tough down there!

Don't let those little rats fool you, they've got a bad disease filled bite.
The last thing you want to do is to go see Sgrios! You want to get lucky, not
lose stuff!

## Tricky Amorphous Blob Two and Mass Amorphous

When your group defeats the Skrull, your group will once again be ported back
into LC room 1, and like before, your group will have to enter the door
directly in front of you. Next, your group will have to kill five sewer rats
to enter the door. Before, your group entered the bottom door, this time, your
group will have to enter the side door. This door will bring you to LC room
14. Again, your group may skip this room and advance to room 15. **Notice,
your group will need to go to the top room as seen below**

Entering the top room, your group will be in LC room 20. Your group can skip
this room and go to the lower room, entering LC room 21.

When entering LC room 21, your group is before the Amorphous Blob Two. Your
group will need to kill 5 sewer blobs to advance into the boss room.

When your group enters this boss room, your group will have to walk around
because it is a very large room. Just like Skrull and Captain, sometimes it
will take a little bit for it to spawn. But, when it does, good luck!

This time, when your group defeats the second boss, it will port your group to
LC room 33.

You can enter the door and it will bring your group to LC room 34. Here, your
group can skip this room, and it brings your group to LC room 35. This is the
room before Mass Amorphous. Your group can not advance to Mass Amorphous until
your group has killed five smily blobs. When your group has made those kills,
you'll be ported to LC room 36. (Mass Amorphous)

When your group is in Mass Amorphous, decide quickly who is going to Ard
Cradh, who is going to Heal, who is going to Flower the bards, and who is
going to tank, if you have not done that already. Do not take Mass lightly,
this monster is very dangerous, and even the highest bashers in Temuair or
Medenia can be killed by it.

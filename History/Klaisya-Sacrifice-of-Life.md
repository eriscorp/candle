# Sacrifice of Life

_by Klaisya in **Dark Ages**_

## Íobairt Cara Dúinn Beatha

_I found a tattered scroll at the foot of the Mileth Crypt dragon one late
night back in Deoch 30-something. I curled up in a corner and carefully untied
the purple succubus hairs that held it rolled shut. As the parchment unrolled,
I could see spatters of what looked to be red ink…or possibly blood. Once I
read the entire scroll, I deemed it necessary to share the contents with all
of Temuair for the subject is a history we all share in some way. Young and
old…we all despise those dark, evil creatures that dwell in the darkness of
the crypts while feasting on the flesh of our dead…Not once have we ever
considered them one of our own. Now, however, you shall…the scroll read as
follows:_

In his dark chamber of the third realm, Chadul admired his work of darkness
and looked through his smoke-filled glass globe to watch as Grinneal fell. His
heart was young but had already turned to stone and frozen within his demonic
form. However cold he was, his desire to call another his own was as
overwhelming as the stench of death that hung in the air. He watched as young
and old fell into his grip and searched for the being, which he would enslave
as a lover. Day by day, more souls were taken by the dark presence when,
during the 2nd phase of the moon, a maiden with long, lavender hair and
porcelain skin was overtaken by the Dubhaim. As she sank deeper into the realm
of decay, he ordered his attendants retrieve her at once.

As the cloaked attendants carried the lifeless form to their master, a
hybrasyl locket slipped out from under the girl's armor. Chadul reached for
the locket and ripped it from her neck as to read the inscription. He held the
locket close to the fiery pile of burning flesh and read:

_"Succumba: Nay shall you allow yourself to succumb to anyone but Danaan."_

He laughed to himself and glanced at the girl.

_"Succumba…what a perfect name for a slave girl. She will succumb to my will
if she values all that is dear to her still existing in Grinneal."_

He motioned for the cloaked mutants to place the girl on a granite altar near
his throne. Her lush, lavender hair toppled over the edge and swept down the
side of the altar. Chadul swaggered over to her body and squeezed the sap from
a dark blossom onto the girl's bosom.

_"She shall awaken from he sleep soon enough now…the sap of the dark blossom
with harden her heart and turn her blood black…but her heart will pump evil
life into her limp figure," he said to the attendants as he walked back to his
throne._

He sat back in his chair and used the bone of a faerie to sharpen his long,
pointed nails. As the bone wore away, he continued to watch the girl until a
stir of emotion raged in her body. She jolted forward and gasped for air. The
force of her awakening knocked her off the altar onto the cold, dirty floor.
She tried to stand but her knees folded beneath her. She continued to try push
upward but the invisible chains of evil pinned her down. After realizing her
attempts would come to no avail, she looked up in to the darkness. Chadul
appear through the clouds of smoke and filth and displayed a goulish smile.
Succumba's eyes widened in terror and she let out a shriek but her voice was
swept away by a strange wind.

_"Who are you? Where am I? What am I doing here…?"_

_"You, my child, are in the third realm and I am its keeper. I am the keeper
of all that is dark and decaying. I am the anti-good…the prince of the
Dubhaim…I am Chadul," he replied in a deep, raspy voice. He continued, "You
are here because my servants have claimed your soul as my own…you are now
mine. Most of those who have a fate similar to yours end up in the pits of
despair, but you, my dear, will serve as my darkest treasure. You will become
my wife."_

His smile widened showing a second set of piercing teeth as he laughed to
himself. Succumba sank backwards into the altar and cried to herself but
regained her composure and glared at the dark figure now standing over her.

_"I refuse to aid you in the destruction of Grinneal. I refuse to succumb to
the evil ways of darkness…"_

She reached for her locket but could not find it. She looked about her and
checked the pocket of her armor, but found nothing. She looked up right as
Chadul began to twirl the hybrasyl chain around his long, bony fingers. He
whistled a tune and opened the small cross shaped locket…

_"'Nay shall you allow yourself to succumb to anyone but Danaan.' What a
sickening and ignorant statement. Do you and the others truly believe the
power of Danaan and her light can overpower me? Have you not seen the
destruction my servants have caused? Have you not witnessed with your own eyes
the fall of Grinneal?"_

He glared down at her with his coal black eyes. The shadows of fire danced
around him and the moans of the suffering were drowned out by his booming
voice…

_"I am the king of your home now, and you, my dear, shall be my queen of
darkness! What have you to say to that?"_

_"I will never help you hurt those I love…"_

He smiled and snapped his fingers. There was a flash of light and smoke around
Succumba and as it dissipated she looked down at herself. Her skin was ghostly
white and her hair had turned a more vibrant purple. Her clothes had ripped
away to show a black, form-fitting corset and boots. She tried to hold back
her tears and gently bit her lip but wrenched in pain immediately. She touched
her fingers to her bottom lip and realized she was bleeding…she now had sharp
fangs like that of her master. Her tears began to flow freely now but instead
of a stream of clear liquid, a stream of red ran down her hardened cheek.

_"What have you done to me? What am I?"_

_"You are now one of my servants…and you must look like one. Your heart has
been chilled and the blood has left most of your body. You are a ghoul like
all others here. There is no escape my dear…you cannot change your fate. Not
even your precious 'mother of light' can save your soul."_

The girl continued to sob to herself. She wiped her eyes and looked up just as
her treasured locket slipped through Chadul's fingers into a fiery pit. She
jumped out to catch it but was nabbed by a sgraith before she could catch her
necklace.

_"No! My mother gave me that!"_

_"You mean, this mother…?"_

Smoke erupted from his mouth and formed the figure of a stout little woman
working away in a bakery. She looked sad but determined to finish her work.

_"Leave my mother alone you wretched filth! She has done nothing to harm a
soul…"_

_"Name calling is not needed my princess…Now, if you continue to fight me, I
shall push your mother into a fate worse than death…"_

_"There is no such fate! My mother is protected by the kiss of Danaan!"_

_"Very well…"_

The smoky image became clouded by darkness and a gargoyle appeared in front of
the elder woman. She tried to fight it off but she failed. Chadul blew away
the smoke and the old woman appeared in front of Succumba.

_"Mother!…Mother?"_

_"She cannot hear you, but, as you are my lover, I will give you a last chance
to say goodbye to her…_

Succumba reached out to hug the woman but fell through the image of smoke…She
whimpered and tried to control her emotions…

_"I am sorry mother…May Danaan protect you. I love you…"_

The image faded away as another appeared. The image of Succumba's new form
lurked deep within the crypt.

_"Is that to be MY fate?"_

_"No, that is your mother's soul. She is now trapped in your body as you
caused her demise. She shall forever dwell in the depths of death and feast on
the flesh of the living!"_

_"You're a monster! What I wouldn't give to have my husband here. He would
slay you in one strike!"_

At hearing this, Chadul's eyes glowed with fire and he bore his sharp teeth.
He again blew a smoky image from his mouth. This time, a weapons smith was
beating a piece of mythril into the blade of a sword. Succumba's eyes widened
in fright…Just as she was about to break down into tears, a small child
skipped into the room and hugged the smith. Following the girl was a woman
about Succumba's age but with vibrant red hair.

Succumba let out a moan as she watched her husband Jacobin, sister Givinia,
and niece Kardinella interact for the last time. Just as with her mother, a
gargoyle stormed the scene…but this time, two fiend pupas followed him. Each
of her loved ones were overtaken by the Dubhaim as the scene again faded into
nothing. Succumba felt her a pain in her chest.

_"What have you done to them? Where have your evil servants taken my beloved
family?"_

_"They are along side your dear mother," he mocked. "They too shall serve in
your form as my servants of death. They will feast off of the flesh of those
who come to save them. They will curse the depths of the crypt for all
eternity…because of your own foolishness. You are the cause of this, not I!
What do you have to say for yourself?"_

_"I…I…"_

_"Go on? Does the bat have your tongue?"_

_"What must I do to break this curse you have placed upon them? I will do
anything! Just let my family rest in peace in the heart of Danaan!"_

_"You will do anything…?"_

His brow rose with devilish thought. He paced around the girl and looked about
her as if examining her every curve.

_"You are a beautiful fiend now. You shall make the perfect mate to me…I will
allow their souls to return to Danaan if, and only if, you agree to take their
place in the depths of the crypt. You will be my slave…my love slave. I would
keep you here but you have already begun to get on my nerves. No, I will visit
you when I am in need of your services. In the meantime, you will claim other
souls for me and curse all those who venture into the depths of my realm. You
will do my bidding! If you agree, your mother, husband, and sister will go
free!"_

_"But what of Kardinella?"_

_"There is no hope for her. She is too young to give to Danaan. I must keep
her easily influenced mind here, but she will take the form of another
creature within the crypts that you may visit her as you wish."_

A puff of smoke appeared and the small child lay lifeless at Succumba's feet.
She whisked the girl into her arms and gently stroked her brow. Drops of blood
ran down her cheek onto the child's ragged blouse. In an instance, Kardinella
rose from her aunt's arms and levitated above her. Chadul approached the
child, squeezed the same dark blossom over her and brandished his teeth.
Within moments, the soft skin grew coarse hair; the sweet crystal blue eyes
turned black as night; the hands and feet grew claws; the once precious and
enduring smile became more sneering. There was still a "cuteness" to the
creature. It was as if the power of darkness couldn't consume her whole heart.

_"Well, this form will have to do. Her heart is too pure for a complete
transformation but, in time, she will become as evil as you shall be. I
believe her new name shall be…Kardi."_

Succumba looked at her once precious niece and felt more tears welling up in
her eyes. She glanced back at Chadul...

_"If I agree to be your servant, do I have your word that you will let the
others go to rest with Danaan?"_

_"Aye."_

Succumba took a deep breath and bowed her head…

_"Very well…On your word that my mother, sister, and husband shall be at peace
with Danaan, I am yours."_

A cloud of darkness erupted around Succumba and Kardi as Chadul cackled with
devilish delight.

_"Now, a name for you my sweet…Succubus! Yes, that's it," he muttered under
his breath, "Succubus and Kardi, you are hereby damned to the depths of the
crypt of Mileth. You are a servant of your Dark Lord, Chadul. You shall obey
all that I proclaim and you shall serve me against Danaan for eternity!"_

A shrill wind blew through the lair and the forms disappeared into the
darkness…

_The scripture ended abruptly but this aisling is left to believe that the
souls of Succumba and of her niece Kardinella inhabit the creatures we call
Succubus and Kardi. They are the unfortunate prisoners of the dark lord…the
evil…the chaos of Temuair. Shall we never forget what others sacrifice to give
us a better life under the blessing of Danaan! Thus is the history of the
Succubus and Kardi..._

_So is my word and my belief,_  
_Lady Klaisya Meothair_

`((I named this man Jacobin because of a religiously based political party in
18th century France. The Jacobins were an underground left-wing movement who
refused to succumb to the "evil doings" of King Louis XVI and the conservative
nobility. They were the party responsible for the French period known as "The
Terror" during the French Revolution of the late 1700's. And this story, as a
whole, is based upon the refusal of Succumba to succumb to the evil ways of
Chadul.))`

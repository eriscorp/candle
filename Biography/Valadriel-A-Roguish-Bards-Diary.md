# A Roguish Bard's Diary

_by Valadriel in **Dark Ages**_

## Deoch 14 , 6th Moon , 4th Sun

_I met a young lady named Rhaeg today..._

_She is a priest and probably the most beautiful lady i have ever seen ._

_After a long conversation with her i found out that she has gained much more insight than I have , but as i want to be able to protect such a fair lady I have to do something to catch up with her ._

_I felt comfortablein her presence and i fear my hear is endangered from yon fair priestess..._

## same day , evening

_I decided to enter the dungeon beneath the town of Mileth , for my mentor Seriah told me that facing the horrors who dwell beneath our peaceful city might let me understand things I have never even noticed._

## Deoch 14,6th Moon , 5th Sun

_Not much to write today..._

_Up to now everything is alright , I fought hard today but i don't think i gained much insight ... there must be another way..._

_...yet i am distracted for memories of lady Rhaeg keep me from thinking ..._

## Deoch 14 , 6th Moon , 6th Sun

_I found out that it is possible to venture deeper into the darkness that lies beneath Mileth._

_The walls seem to watch me and the creature are huge and terrifying..._

_The smell of death and decay fills the air and i can hardly breath...yet I think this is right place and though mny people have warned me to venture thus far I had to comehere._

_A huge centipede attacked me today , i found out that i can hardly pierce it's hide with my dagger , so i started to set traps and almost ran into one of them myself..._

_Now i will manage to gain experience and new insight , so I can protect lady Rhaeg... I have met her only once and yet I think i might fall in love with her one day ... or ..maybe I already did_

## Deoch 14, 6th Moon , 7th sun

_When i was fighting a spider today , two of its brethren and a bat came to it's aid ._

_I ran through the dark corridors as fast as i could , stumbling and almost falling i made it around a corner .._

_just to see a centipede , ready to attack. I managed to dodge it's attack , but one of the beasts was a spellcaster and injured me badly._

_I finally got into a small room and shut the door behind me , hoping that the lock would keep the critters at bay for a while. Then i looked aruond in the dark chamber, which was abandoned...or so i thought.._

_The very moment i sat down to rest two bats came flying towards me from the shadows , i drew my dagger to fight them off , but while i hit one of them , the other bit me in my shoulder ._

_Pain slowed my motion and clouded my mind..but then i thought of Lady Rhaeg and turned to fight the second beast ._

_I managed to kill it , taking another blow...but my dagger broke and was useless now._

_After i had written a letter I opened the door a little to send my trained pigeon out , hoping that it would find it's way out of the dungeon and to the lady..._

_I am freezing and bleeding heavily ,the bandages i made out of pieces of cloth i had torn off my shirt are dark red with blood , yet when i visualize the face of yon young priestess the wounds seem to bere mere scratches and it gets warmer ..._

## Deoch 14, 6th Moon , 8th Sun

_I hope the date is correct , I lost track of time being down here in the darkness ´having only a small candle left , now that my torch is gone..._

_My weapon is gone and the fowl beasts will soon manage to break the door , they can smell my blood and want to feast on my corpse, but I am not giving up yet..._

_I keep singing ancient songs to keep myself from becoming insane and it makes me feel better too..._

_The power of the old songs seems to drive away some of the darkness... may the Tuatha be blessed for the ancient bard songs..._

_As i am unarmed i started to set traps ... they are all around me now , but that is okay for i can't go anywhere , wheter there are traps or not ... so i will wait and let the critters have a little surprise..._

_I will continue writing later , but i must stop now for i hear strange noises_

_The lack of food weakens me now that I had to use up my last ration , and the thirst almost lets me drop unconscious_

## Sometime later , i gave up writing dates as long as i am down here...

_Through some strange magical reasons i received a letter from Lady Rhaeg , it seems as if the priestess asked the gods themselves to send it to me ..._

_Anyway i feel invigorated after reading that letter ..she also said something about a reward if i ever get out f here..._

_Now i do have one more reason to get out of here quickly..._

_It is dark now , i hope whoever finds this will be able to read it ..._

_My candle has gone out and it suddenly became pitch black in here._

## The next day ..or so I think for i have slept a while

_I listened to the sounds around me .first it was silent ,but then i heard it , a terrible noise like the mourning of a long forgotten spirit...it was the sound of oaken wood that was about to burst under the pressure of who knows what creatures..._

_I heard the door shatter into pieces and then a disgusting sound of dozens of legs crawling over the dirty floor ._

_the smell was terrible , it was a fowl smell of decay and excrements..._

_They came closer , i knew that for sure but i could not see them , and my heart pounded so loud that i could not even hear them._

_Then i heard it ..the familiar sound of my traps being activated , they ran into them..it had taken me days to set them up but now they would pay the price for imprisoning me in here...._

_After enough of my traps had hit their targets i charged towarsd the door , shouting a fierce battlecry..._

_...and somehow got out of the room unharmed._

_I ran away as fast as i could and made it to a staircase , now I am in a room at the second level of this dungeon , it seems to be rather save and i even found some food here..._

## Deoch 14 , 6th Moon , 16th Sun

_I finally got out of that dark place...the sun was blinding when i stepped outside , but i loved it . The air was fresh and i thanked the gods for saving..and i was also oing to thank the fairest of their priest .. who had prayed for me..._

_After asking an Aisling about the date i realized how long i really had been down there and shivered..._

_I had been down there much longer than i had thought , but the insight i had gained let me understand many things. I even could wear another type of armour , now that i understood how to do so ._

_As i had found some items of value ..probably leftovers of less fortunate adventurers I had some money and used it to buy a present for the fair priestess , yet i still have to find her_

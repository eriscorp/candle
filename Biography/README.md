# Aisling Biographies

Biography. The lives of Aislings, set down by their own hands or by those who knew them. Here are their first days in Temuair, the paths they chose, the friends they kept, and the battles they won and lost.

Most of these works have been edited to conform to the Library's display formats. Where possible, a link to the original work has been included.

_Works with a octagram ![octagram](../images/octagram.webp) displayed next to them represent some of the greatest treasures of Temuair._

_Works with a question mark ![questionmark](../images/questionmark.webp) displayed next to them have been lost to time, and are merely placeholders. If you have these works, by all means, get in touch with us._

Goblin Raid Repelled

Many Aislings are the saviors of Loures this past double-moon. The goblin force, which has been camped near a grassy flatland for more than two Deochs, took Loures castle by storm. The strongest warriors of this particular Goblin tribe were sent on the raid. Luckily, Aislings from all over Temuair came to replace the missing Loures army and repelled the sudden attack.
Early reports told that the goblins' mission was to capture the King of Loures. Yet, their goal seemed an impossibility when Aislings charged in to battle the attackers. Sadly, many Aislings gave their lives in the defense, but the goblins have retreated for the time being.

Agus Araile Poetry Contest
The motley, Agus Araile, is seeking pre-registration for it's poetry contest. The event will be held at the Tulsi Brew tavern, in Piet, where those who registered to recite will be given their time to do so. 
Any type of poetry is welcome, yet the judges will vastly prefer short poems. Winners will receive a new mark upon their spark's legend.  Please contact any Agus Araile member if ye wish to recite in the event. (Landon, Deksar, Trinka, Drind, Pashura... among others)
The time and date for this event is yet to be announced.

Leanabh de Eachdraich
written by: Erenthar

I am pleased to announce to you the founding of a new guild:
Based on a common idea from Leshere, Raevyn, Mismom and your servitor,
Leanabh de Eachdraich ~ Children of History, saw the sun this 11th Sun of the 7th Moon of Deoch 29.
Together we wish to retrieve all the lost parts of our History and to write them in order to give to every Aisling the possibility to know what happened since the first Aisling appeared on Temuair. To achieve this goal we need your help. If you are an historian in heart, if you wish to walk back on the path of our ancestors, if you are ready to discover lost secrets, ...
Then contact Raevyn and she will lead you to the door of Leanabh de Eachdraich.

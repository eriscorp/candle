Temuairian Hide and Seek                                                                            Hosted by: Sealladh


In celebration of the guild Sealladh's founding, we are hosting a Temuairian-wide Hide &amp; Seek event. The rules are simple and the prizes are valuable.

All those who wish to participate in the event must be at the Tulsi Brew (Piet tavern) prior to the start time. ((Which is Saturday, Oct. 5th 2002, at 12 noon PST))

The rules are very simple. Before the start of the event, 6 members of Sealladh will have hidden somewhere in Temuair with their prize and their names will be announced at the start of the event. Find one of them and say "I see you!" within the time frame of the event ((which is 1 RL hour)) to claim their prize. Once you've won once you can't win again.

To narrow down the search and make the search fair for all Aislings who participate, the Aislings in hiding will NOT hide in hunting areas past the first two floors or areas. They will not hide in areas that an insight 99 cannot enter nor in an area an insight 30 cannot enter. They will not be invisible with hide spells, and they will be hiding in any town, nook or cranny. So search everywhere!

((Remember, the fun begins Saturday, Oct. 5 at 12 noon PST and will end at 1pm PST))

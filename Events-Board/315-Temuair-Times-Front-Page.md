Drunkard discovered to have drown in Undine.
     On the 25th Sun of the fourth moon of Deoch 38, Mundane Damara of Undine was shocked motionless when she noticed a dead body floating lifeless in the bay as she left her bakery to take her morning stroll. She was said to immediately have informed one of the patrolling Loures guards. The guard, whose name I was ordered not to expose, was able to swim out and retrieve the body. Upon his return, Chloe, the barkeeper, was able to identify the man as a nameless drunkard who had his fair share of brandy the evening before.
     "The man was out of his mind, speaking about cherry blossoms and bloody skies. I actually felt sorry for him," were Chloe's only comments.
     The man was described to have strawberry red hair and wore rather worn clothing. Kiril, the rebellious, self-proclaimed Magus Philosopher of Undine, was unable to determine whether this man was a Mundane or an Aisling. An Aisling-led funeral service was held half a sun after the man's recovery. 
 Repairman stuck in Rucesion Inn's so-called "Trick Wall".
     We have all known of the Rucesion Inn's quality of construction for Deochs. Even after its initial patchwork, numerous errors have been exposed. One of these is the famous corner of the inn. Maria, the innkeeper, was tired of waiting for Rucesion's Mundane Burgess, Eduardo to approve the coin necessary and hired a private band of repairmen with her own coin. During the repairs, one of the workers was himself stuck inside the mischievous corner. Despite the greatest and most brave efforts of the repairmen and even several unknown Aislings, the man was unable to be freed until Berg, famed Suomi Master Warrior, was summoned to the scene. After several hours of hard work and extreme use of what was in his skull, Berg was able to bring the man out. Unfortunately, the repairman is still in shock and is being treated by Maria herself. Mundane Burgess Eduardo, fearful for another similar mishap, has disallowed any further work to be made on the inn. He would not comment on the matter.

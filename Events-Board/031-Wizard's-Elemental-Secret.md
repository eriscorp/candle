# Wizard's Elemental Secret

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 3rd Moon, 13th Sun · Post #31_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Dar, Kiril, and Hadrian confer over an ancient elemental secret. They attempt to decypher an ancient text on the art of increasing the concentration of a natural element in a creature. 

By translated fragments, the shadowy philosophers believe that this secret allows each element's effect to intensify, increasing vulnerability and also increasing invulnerability. 

The mundane wizards act cautiously, knowing how dangerous it would be if goblin shamans learned this secret. 

Kiril announces he will begin teaching Fas Nadur by Glioca-week, Cail-day's morning ((by 04/10, Noon PDT)). He is working on translating other variations of the secret, beag fas nadur and mor fas nadur, which he expects will take a little over a double-moon.

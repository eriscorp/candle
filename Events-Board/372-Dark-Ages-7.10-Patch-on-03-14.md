# ((Dark Ages 7.10 Patch on 03/14))

_Posted by Eduardo — 4th Moon, 8th Sun · Post #372_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
((On March 14th, Dark Ages will be updating to version 7.10. The following features will be added.
- Improved security on client-to-server communication.
- A new armor slot will be added to allow players to wear non-combat armors over their combat armors (making non-combat armor visible but still have the attributes  of the combat armor). Examples of non-combat armor are political armors and formal armors.
- The ability points for monsters in Medenia will be increased.
- Additional floors and new monsters will be added to the Cursed Home in Hwarone.
- 3 new quests in Hwarone.
  The Cursed Home -- 30,000 ability point reward, repeatable every 2 weeks.
  Dune Patrol -- 1,500 ability point reward, repeatable every 24 hours.
  Trade Merchant -- 7,000 ability point reward, repeatable once every week.
Coming Soon:
Item Shop - Shortly after the patch will be the launch of the Dark Ages Item Shop.  Items sold in the shop will be for looks, i.e. formal armors, holiday armors, pets, effect potions, etc. Items can be purchase using the Kruna currency. Kruna can be purchased, but registered accounts will receive a regular allowance of Kruna. More information will be given about the item shop when the launch date is finalized.))

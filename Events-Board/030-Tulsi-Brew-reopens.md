# Tulsi Brew reopens

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 1st Moon, 1st Sun · Post #30_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Lia, barmaid of the Tulsi Brew in Piet writes: 

Many aislings have visited with me and shared with me fond memories of the Anaman Pact's events at the Tulsi Brew. I have enjoyed listening to all of you speak of your memories. You have told me how any aisling was able to come to the Brew in the evening and tell a story of their choosing or how you created your own "living stories" as a group. I only wished for the day that I could too enjoy listening to these talented young story tellers come together and tell their stories as all of you and Bella enjoyed them. 

I have conveyed your requests to the council of Anaman Pact and they have decided to start these events again due to popular demand. So, on behalf of the Anaman Pact, I would like to invite all aislings of Temuair to the Tulsi Brew in Piet. Anaman Pact will be reopening The Tulsi Brew for public events starting on ((March 31, 2000)). These events will be held twice a week at the Tulsi Brew. The evening will include one of a large range of activities, including some of the favorites: Story telling, social night, political debate, History discussion/debate, and the favorite "throwing/sumo" contest. The schedule will be as follows: 

((Tuesdays: 4:00 PM PST, 6:00 PM CST, 7:00 PM EST))
((Fridays: 8:00 PM PST, 10:00 PM CST, 11:00 PM EST)) 

Something different will be planned for each night and requests will be taken. 

I'm looking forward to seeing all of you and meeting many new aislings that I have not had the opportunity to meet as of yet. 

Sincerely, 

Lia 

((For more information http://www.crosswinds.net/~anamanpact or contact a member of Anaman Pact.))

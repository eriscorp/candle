Rat infestation
Over a double-moon ago, massive amounts of rats flooded above ground to infest both Mileth and Rucesion. It is widely known that rats are carriers of several dark plagues and with hundreds of the little creatures running above ground, the Mundanes were understandably worried about the health of both Mundanes and Aislings. Thus, Aislings were called to arms as exterminators to eliminate the threat, and respond they did, destroying the threat.
The invasion seems to be tamed for the time being, but some scholars believe there is a higher motive in the scheme of things.

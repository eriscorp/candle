Bertil, Fae Priestess of Suomi, speaks of odd travelers:
A handsome, well-mannered man visited me the moon before last. He spoke in fascinating detail concerning a religious sect that devotes it's time and energy to the worship of the white lady, Danaan.  
 He carried several books, one of which he called the 'Caidh Leabhar'.  I only caught a glimpse of it's writings as he thumbed through the pages, but I could see his eyes glisten when he looked down upon the dusty book.  
Near the back cover, several pages remained untouched by an inked quill.  I questioned him about the book's abrupt end, and he told me the blank slates were yet to be written... that the events of the future would decide the book's final outcome.
Before he departed, the long-haired Aisling invited me to a gathering of worshippers.  The evening of  the 9th moon's 11th day on this Deoch, the thirty-third, was the date he gave to me.  Taking place just outside the Tagorian Chapel, of all places.  
 
A worship of Light near the doorstep of Darkness?
Bertil
Fae Priestess of Suomi

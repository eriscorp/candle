# Meallaire Prayer Fair

_Posted by Stilgar — 4th Moon, 22nd Sun · Post #350_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Religion, Spirituality, and Aisling Unity are wonderful qualities found throughout Temuair. For one day, our wish is to bring all the religious fellowships together on a common ground for the enlightenment of all. Join us in a day of celebration of the faith, where the community will have the chance of meeting other pious fellows. During the fair, followers of Temuairian dieties will be present to share their holy experiences and to assist fellows in giving their thanks to the Gods. Sermons will be given during the opening and closing of the fair. In addition to prayer services, there will be a trivia session where aislings can put their knowledge of the Gods and Theology to a test. Cash prizes will be given out to award those who get the questions right.

((The Fair will be held on the 17th of July, 7-9pm EST in North Lynith Beach 1. Visitors should bring their prayer necklaces along to enjoy the assistance of prayers.))

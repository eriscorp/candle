None of Your Bee's Wax! 

East Woodlands - Recently, the wasps of East Woodlands stopped producing wax. This has caused great concern among low insight Aislings and their mentors, especially in the paths of monk, priest and wizards that specialize in Sal magic. 

"My students can't learn to kick!" exclaims one monk "How are they expected to advance?" Beginning priests need raw wax to learn Beag Ioc, the most basic healing spell, and wizards need raw wax for Beag Sal, the novice water spell. 

Devlin, fae priestess of Mileth, sends many Aislings questing for raw wax. She said, "There is an imbalance in the cosmos." When asked to elaborate, she just stared vacantly into space and blew bubbles from her mouth. 

Cian, the Mileth alchemist, was consulted. "Everything boils down to cause and effect." he replied, "Flowers bloom. Wasps collect nectar from flowers. Wasps make wax. I believe that the wasps' life cycle has been disrupted by the numerous herbalists picking out the supplies of hydele in east woodlands. Ironically, the missing wax limits the number of new Aislings taking the paths that can become herbalists. The natural order will always balance itself."

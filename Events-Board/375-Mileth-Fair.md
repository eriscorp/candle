# Mileth Fair

_Posted by Phorbin — 4th Moon, 18th Sun · Post #375_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Nearly 30 Deochs since Mileth last had a fair, we have decided it was time yet again! I have sat down with some of Mileth's finest officials, and a master ranger, and we have settled on some times for these events. ((Mileth will be holding a fair from September 2nd Thru the 8th)). The events will last two full moons, and will be filled with events and activities that all aislings will enjoy. Some events will include...
Aisling Merchants ~ ALL aislings will be given the opportunity to purchase seals that will give them access to booths in which they may utilize to sell goods and/or services.
Aisling Events ~ ALL aislings will be given the opportunity to purchase seals that will give them access to event booths, which they may utilize to hold an activity or sell goods and/or services.
Theater ~ Plays will be held through-out the fair.
Directed by Omner and Rowan.
Amphitheater ~ Contests and games will be held through-out the fair.
Special events ~ Aislings will be given the opportunity to participate in special events, more information will be released soon.
If you or your guild are interested in holding a special event, please contact Wastedd or emrysMorgane for more information. We will be selecting event hosts according to their history. (not all aislings will be selected to hold a special event.) A schedule of the events will be posted closer to the fair.
Aislings will be expected to be on their best behavior during the fair, or be subject to banishment and or exile according to Mileth Laws.

~Aliran, Mileth Mundane Burgess

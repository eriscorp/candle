Dungeon of the double moon - Pravat Caves
Featured Monster - Draco Hatchling       
written by Ramanayan Caesar

Pravat Caves: 
The Pravat caves have a long and rich history. The caves were originally inhabited by two species: the Grimlok and the Draco. However a great war between the two forced the Draco into the neighboring Kasmanium Mines. However the Draco still lay some of their eggs in the deepest regions of Pravat which explains the presence of Draco Hatchlings. Aislings first came to know of Pravat during the entreaty epic event  in Deoch 5 when the Goblins invaded the Pravat Caves to steal the conix stones. The fellowships of Temuair acted in defense of the Grimlok, except Ceannladir which sided with the Goblins, helping them recover many stones and in doing this were given the ability to summon their god's creatures. 
The creatures that live in Pravat can be quite interesting and dangerous. The Grimlok mainly consist of three distinct types- Proles, who mine the conix stones and other gems, and Soldiers and Warriors who protect the miners and attack the Goblins or outsiders. The Goblin invasion force consists of just mainly warriors and guards who are excellent fighters. All creatures here drop gold, some gems, and/or other kinds of magical items when killed. Most notably is the Curved Dagger dropped by Grimlok Warriors which is highly prized by rogues. Some others arms can also be used by aislings. Each uses normally minor spells and sometimes poison. However if you speak to the young boy outside or to the leaders of the Grimlok and Goblins aislings can become allied to the Grimlok and Goblins and act as mercenaries for them.  Pravat can be very dangerous unless you know how to handle yourself. 
Draco Hatchling:
The Draco Hatchlings are Dracos that have not yet reached full maturity. Don't let this fool you; they are quite deadly. The Draco Hatchlings live in the deepest section of Pravat, right before the Chaos Realms entrance. They are much larger then an aisling and can hover quite well above the ground. In their defense is powerful fire breath and deadly claws. The hatchlings are also fond of using mor srad, crahd, and poison spells. Luckily not many are ever in the same area at once. Draco Hatchlings when killed will drop some gold and normally some kind of a magical item. Rarely they may also their jaws and even rarer their eyes which have no use as of now but make nice collectors items. If you think these kids are tough wait until you meet their parents!

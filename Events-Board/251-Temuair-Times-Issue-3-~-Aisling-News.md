The Dagoba

A 'Festival of the gods' the Dagoba is an event where all the religions of Temuair come together and share a little bit of themselves with all who are in attendance. The next is to take place in less than two double-moons. Contact Aislings Duplico or Tormod for more details
The following is the legend of the Dagoba, as told by Aisling Duplico:

It was long ago that the existence of this event was divined. Thaddeus Teffid, a Minister of Glioca, and seven members of the other fellowships' clergies discovered a volume describing the event.
When eight stars light the daytime sky, the Dagoba will be known. When the full moon rises after that, the Dagoba has begun.
The Dagoba, the Temple of Eight, is a time of union between the eight Faiths of Temuair. When the Temple rises, a great celebration would begin. It is a temple, yes, but a building, no. The Temple of Eight is simply a nexus in time when the eight Faiths of Temuair somehow grow together as one.
On the first day of Deoch 27, eight stars blazed to life in the daytime sky. Twenty-five Priests observed this event and watched as the stars danced around, then streaked down to the surface, leaving in their wake a briefly glowing octogram.
They discovered that the stars had indeed struck the ground - it had etched out an octogram in the ground in Suomi, near its fountain, that would not be erased. Beside it, in a small stone slab, were written the words that announced the Dagoba. It would begin, and these twenty-five Priests knew what they had to do.
They created a massive religious festival, that would occur on the day of the Dagoba - to celebrate the union of the Fellowships. And that evening, under the full moon, they stood silently in vigil, together, musing over the great achievement they had put aside their differences to create.
That day, the Dagoba, can never be truly repeated, but we can celebrate its anniversaries.

The Aisling Mastermind Trivia 
The wizard of Suomi, Hadrian, is on a quest to discover the quickest and most intelligent minds in Temuair.  Heavy with a feeling of loneliness due to his vast intellect and his constant solitary research, Hadrian is announcing a challenging Trivia Contest between aislings:

"Clever aislings!  I present to you an opportunity to challenge yourselves in a game of wits with others.  The five smartest aislings will receive legend marks of great respect, and the one aisling who passes my test of knowledge will receive a 20,000,000 gold stipend for his research."  
Because Hadrian is an elderly mundane who finds it difficult to leave his laboratory, he has requested that Fiel Capilla, devotees of the Spark, carry out his mission.  Aislings who wish to be a part of the event must seek out the ingenious wizard where he bides his time ((on February 3, 3:00 p.m. PST, 5:00 p.m. CST, 6:00 p.m. EST.))

Details: Kelberoth Strike
Kelberoth Strike is a fearsome assault fueled by the life energy of the
martial artist. It is a punch delivered with such force that it hurts both
the monk and his target in an explosion of raw power.
Kelberoth Strike bends many rules of physical attack, leading researchers to
believe that it is more of a mystical attack than a physical one. Whether
you attack from the side, back, or front, Kelberoth Strike does exactly the
same damage. Changes in the monk's strength or constitution has no effect on
the attacks power, nor do defensive spells on the target such as armachd or
dion. The skill level seems to have no effect either. However, due to the
difficulty in training this skill, the researcher could not observe a very
wide range of skill levels. (A change from skill level 3 to 4 yeilded no
change in the damage.)
Aite does reduce the damage of Kelberoth strike by 40%. Elemental
interactions (how the attacker's attack element effects the target's defense
element) have a minor effect on this attack.
The power of Kelberoth Strike seems to be based directly on the current
health of the martial artist, modified slightly by element. When using a
strong attack element, Kelberoth strike deals 30% of the monk's current
health in damage to the target.
The major drawback of Kelberoth Strike is that it always drains the attacker
of 60% of her health. 
Kelberoth Strike is gained when a monk learns Kelberoth Form. Martial arts
forms are taught in the Suomi warrior trainer. To learn any form, a monk
must have 5 in each stat and a teacher who know the form, or must have 10 in
each stat to learn alone.

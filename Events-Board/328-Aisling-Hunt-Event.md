_Posted by Eduardo — 9th Moon, 29th Sun · Post #328_

Do you know the Lands?
Do you love riddles and mysteries?
Then the Aisling Hunt is the event for you!
 Puzzles, adventure and prizes, good for any insights.
 The hunt starts promptly at 2pm PST, Saturday, January 3 between Mileth Fountain and the Mileth Crypt.
 First place will win a master item set of his/her choice, 2nd through 4th win Light Belts, and 5th through 10th will get Jade Rings.
 ((The hunt is set up as a modified scavenger hunt.  Several members of the Valkyries motley will be scattered throughout the lands.  The locations where they are will be hidden in poems.  At 2pm PST, Kivrin will start reciting the first poem in Mileth, if you follow that poem you'll find the next Valkyrie who will have a new poem for you.  Winning requires you to visit all the hidden Valkyries, in the order of the poems, first.))

# Mileth Fair: King & Queen

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 5th Moon, 24th Sun · Post #24_

Hollie, chairperson for the Mileth Fair Committee writes: 

The Mileth Fair committee will be taking nominations for the Mileth Fair King and Queen from now until further notice. All entries must be in essay form and must include the aisling's history as well as state reason why he/she would best represent Mileth. Participants will run as a team so essays must include both candidates. Participants may nominate themselves and submit an essay. For more information regarding the rules of this contest, please visit the Mileth Fair Information Center. All entries may be sent to milethfair@crosswinds.net You may also contact Solinox or Dominique with questions regarding this event. 

The Mileth Fair committee has opened an information center to provide all the information needed for the upcoming fair. Although a date has not yet been set for the fair, the progress of planning the fair is well underway and will be outlined in the information center. You will see projects that are being worked on, names of the committee members to contact, information about the Fair King and Queen contest and much more. You can visit the information center at http://www.crosswinds.net/~milethfair

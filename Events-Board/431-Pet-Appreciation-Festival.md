The Suomi Troupe is thrilled to host the second Pet Appreciation Festival! Below, you will find the full schedule and description of each event. To celebrate the occasion, Areini, Pet Master of Tagor, will reward the best competitors with rare pets.
Remember that you must bring a pet to participate in most activities. We hope to see you there!
((This is a Suomi Troupe event that will take place from April 11 to 13.))

-~*~- -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-  -~*~-

Pet Tamer's Lore
****************
Learn about the inspiration behind the festival! This kickoff event will feature a lecture and class competition. More details will be provided in class.
Date: (( April 11th at 4pm PST / 7pm EST ))
Location: Lore Classroom at the Mileth College

Pet Fashion Show
****************

Do you and your pet have what it takes to steal the spotlight? Take to the stage together at Garamonde Theatre and showcase your coordinated style, whether in complementary or contrasting aesthetics. Judges will be looking for the most striking pair.
Date: (( April 12th at 4pm PST / 7pm EST ))
Location: Garamonde Theatre (Suomi Village)

Pet Race
********

Be the first to guide your pet through a meandering maze, avoiding collisions. More details about the tournament will be provided at the event.
Date: (( April 13th at 4pm PST / 7pm EST ))
Location: Loures Soccer Field

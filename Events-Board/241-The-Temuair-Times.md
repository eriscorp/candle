Dear citizens of Temuair,
The Temuair Times newspaper has returned! It will be issued every 2 double-moons, and we are looking for more Aislings to write the Temuair Times with us. If you're interested, contact me (Landon) via pigeon mail 
or whisper. Please remember that only articles that are tasteful will be published. ((Articles must always be IC and do NOT have to have actually happened. If the article is made up, please do not drastically change Temuair, a Mundane or another player.)) Aside from news articles, the Temuair Times will include several columns. If you're interested in starting your own column in the Temuair Times, please contact me. 
Also, announce your weddings, anniversaries, birthdays and other special
occasions through the Temuair Times column "Weddings And Anniversaries." You
may even publicly greet a friend, relative or spouse through the Temuair
Times column "Aisling To Aisling." Just write me with what you'd like to
announce, or what you'd like to say and to whom.
Keep your eyes sharp for the first new issue of The Temuair Times!
 
Landon  
Temuair Times Editor

Loures Court Invites Scholars of Oren

The capitol of Loures has taken steps to welcome the recently discovered island of Oren to the known lands of Temuair by holding an feast in honor of the Oren knights that have fallen to the invading pirates.
King Bruce, himself, presided over the occasion. The many scholars from Oren were treated to a meal along side the nobles of Loures, and afterwards were given a tour of the castle and it's facilities. A good time was had by all, and the whole event went on without a hitch.
Densin, an Oren scholar of history, noted that he was overjoyed that Oren was once again united with Temuair, and that he was excited to begin learning of Temuair's recent past events.

Soumi Fields Protected from Winter

As many know, the cherry and grape harvests do not falter a bit when the bitter winter season hits Temuair. Aisling and Mundanes alike have always passed it off as a supernatural phenomena, yet it was finally a priest that unveiled the secret of the warm fields of Soumi.
The anonymous mundane priest attested in his writings:
"The unnatural warm temperatures that protect Soumi's fields are nothing less than the work of flame lord Deoch.  Deoch's temple, being right on the outskirts of Soumi, is where Deoch's influence is the most powerful. He has taken it upon himself to protect those fields from frost and cold so that priests are able to collect the fruit for their deums.  Perhaps he feels obligated to protect that which saves Aislings from the realm where he was spawned?"
The cherry and grape farmers, however, still insist that it is their special gardening techniques that allow their fruits to grow year around.

Commander Comments on Conflict

Loures army commander of the Goblin conflict, Dealos,  has reported that the battle against the Goblins is going better than expected.
"I must say... Even though they would never stand against the might of Loures's army... the Aislings are quite the fighters. I am glad to report that the Goblins were taken by surprise and their numbers are starting to thin. It is good to know that Aislings will give themselves for the glory of Loures when our armies are away on other tasks," attested Dealos.
Mundanes from surrounding areas paint a much different picture on the success rate of the war. Many say that the Goblins are much higher in number than once realized and also that they may overrun Aisling lines any moon, now

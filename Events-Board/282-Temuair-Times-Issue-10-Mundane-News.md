Precious Metal
During the fifth moon of this Deoch, miners in the mountains of Soumi discovered a new metal under the mountain's base.  The miners pulled only a few shards from their digging site, and have since been unable to locate any further deposits.  The metal was studied by scholars who believe that it may be some sort of remains from the drowning of Hy-brasyl many aeons ago.  

The few fragments that exist are said to be under extensive study by the wizards of Loures, not to mention heavy guard by the Loures army.

Travelers Beware

Masked assailants on the trail between Piet and Loures have committed gruesome murders this past Deoch.  Reports indicate that more than twenty Mundanes have been killed and the unknown attackers also injured several more.  Everyone seems to be baffled by these occurrences, however, a wandering necromancer suggests that the spirits of the dead have come back to take their pain out on their living brethren.  
"Many people have died on that road in the past...from winter storms, murder, or just old age.  There may be something in that area that allows spirits to linger there.  Perhaps now, something is giving those lost spirits the power to kill.  I theorize that it may be because of the upcoming year of Sgrios that they have gained control of death," speculated the hooded necromancer.
Whatever the case, guards have been dispatched to the area and, for the time being, are suggesting that Aislings take a different path Loures.

Temuair Times Change

For more than four Deochs, the new Temuair Times has been a companion to Aislings with its quad-moonly publication schedule.  During this time, the Temuair Times has broken all records for a paper of it's kind and continues to blaze new trails in the area of journalism.  

In order to continue offering its readership the full writing value that they have come to expect, the Temuair Times is changing its format to a yearly publication.  This change is necessary to provide you, the reader, the best newspaper experience we are able to provide.  We thank you for your understanding in this matter.
To everyone the best,

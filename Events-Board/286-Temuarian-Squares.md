 Temuairian Squares

At the first annual Temuiarian Fair we will be hosting a game known as Temuiarian Squares. 

Rules:
This game will work very simply, Nine Celebrity Aislings will stand in a 3 by 3 square. The contestant will choose one of the celebrities and host will ask the Celebrity a question, the celebrity will answer and the Contestant has a choice to agree with the answer or disagree with the answer, if the contestant answers correctly, the Celebrity will drop a gold or silver coin depending on the Contestants color. If they answer incorrectly, the Celebrity will drop the opposite, the idea of the game is to get 3 in a row of your coin.

Each Square will be worth a certain amount of gold in each round, so no one will walk away with nothing.

The Winner will go on to face other contestants until only one stand

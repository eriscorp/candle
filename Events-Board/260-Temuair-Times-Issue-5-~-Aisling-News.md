Mileth's Crackdown

Unveiled by Burgess Ramanayan almost a double-moon ago. Mileth's new heresy law struck Temuair like a dagger. Controversy has since arisen about the stricter punishments that surround a heresy crime.
While taking to several citizens, concerns arose about its effect on new Aislings. Aisling Cazia commented, "I think it is a bit harsh." A small number of private citizens also said the same, also adding that such a quick and harsh change in the law is bound to backfire.
However, the majority spoke in favor of the revised laws. Guard Captain Tenebres stated, "It's for everyone's own good." And the people seem to agree that a stricter enforcement on heresy will bring back the spirit of the old days. Aisling Trinka summed up many Aisling's views, "It's about time something was done to clean up the lands."

Lynith Beach 

Recently, a rogue ship carried Aislings to the newly discovered 'Lynith Beach'. However, mundanes had the ports heavily guarded to prevent outsiders from interfering in their investigation. A mundane spokesman said that no Aislings can enter until they have conducted a full-scale survey of the beach.
Aislings are rich with anticipation. Rumors are abound, but the only confirmed attraction on the island is it's beach... that was said to contain a town's worth of lost artifacts that are lost in the sands. If you're lucky, you might be able to uncover treasure from the beach's grasp.
Lynith beach is located on an island off the shores of Oren. Look for the mundanes to announce it's shipping routes soon!

Bend Space Bagged

It came as a shock to the entire Gramail congregation. Gramail's most infamous supplication, Bend Space, was removed from the power of his worshippers.
"It did not surprise me. There were too many Aislings that were using Bend Space to break the laws that Gramail works so hard to create and uphold," attested a Gramail follower.
Overall, the law god's decision has been met with relief from the Gramail clergy. "It only caused trouble... now maybe we can become the law-abiding fellowship that we were meant to be."

Dye Makers Fix Dyes

Mileth dye makers finally discovered the problem that had plagued rogue hairstylists and their customers for many Deoch. 
In a recent overview of their mixture ingredients, they found that a plant used to make Lavender hair dye would slowly turn hair into a mustard green color when the Aisling would fall asleep.
The Mileth company immediately adjusted their mixtures to fix the oversight. Aislings are safe with Lavender hair, once again.

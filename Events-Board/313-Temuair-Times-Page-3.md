Krytos' Quibbles
Politics
----------------
	Why? Why do we have politics? Is it for the good of the people; is it because some people have a substantial, undeniable right to rule; or is it for more cynical reasons: to further self-esteem, to increase personal self-gratification. 
 	To those who tend towards the cynical side of things, the latter seems much more realistic and appropriate. Of course, some politicians have lofty ideas, a beginning belief that the laws they're passing and the peasants they're banishing improve the world for other players, but these views quickly vanish among the pomp and power afforded to officials, or the endless drudgery associated with filling mundane report.  
              	Temuair isn't possessed with a spirit of charity; there is no philanthropist who uses the bounty of his wealth to aid the masses. Partially, this is due to the fact that anyone and everyone can rise to the top of the economic ladder, and that charity won't have a profound and noticeable difference in the greater scheme of things. But, despite these constraints, charity can make a difference. The gift of money to those who have recently died, who have a need for an item makes a difference on a personal level- something much  more profound and rewarding to a public monument.
	  Temuair is a greedy place.
		  People do things for themselves. If politicians only cared about helping, there wouldn't be a clawing to the highest position, to achieve the extra clout and sponsorship needed to boss people around.   Politics serves as an example of the disease that plays each person who gains their spark--(( because charity isn't fun, people don't pay $10 a month to help others.))
	   And I'm just like everyone else.
  ~krytos

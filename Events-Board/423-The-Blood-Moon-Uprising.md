Aislings,
Your courageous efforts this past moon have eased the threat of the thinning veil separating the land of the living from the dead. Special recognition is owed to Aisling ZeroZone; his sacrificial ritual at Garamonde Theatre--reuniting with his fallen succubus-turned-human partner in eternal embrace--reinforced the boundary between the convergent realms. As well, artist Mimi's brilliant work inspired countless Aislings to answer the call for help.
Many casualties were suffered in our attempts to claim the relics of the Blood Moon cultists. Two valiant Aislings--KaoriWinter and Tython--captured the cultists' most coveted spell-imbued artifact: Spion Torradh Gar.
After concluding his investigation of the relics, Hadrian, Wizard of Suomi, shares his final thoughts:
 "In but a few more suns, the Red Moon shall wax to its fullness. The 
  relics reveal that these bands of liches have been plotting to amass an 
  undead army. With the veil separating the living and dead strengthened, 
  the cultists can command far fewer spectral minions. But will that be 
  enough to prevent the Great Hollowing foretold by the Aisling 
  soothsayers just two moons prior? The fate of Temuair rests with the 
  Aislings."
Prepare yourselves for the final confrontation with the Blood Moon cultists. Arrive fully armed and in groups, ready to face the worst. Further updates and instructions will be provided on the night we band together.

Dungeon of the double-moon- Piet Sewers
written by: Ramanayan Caesar

The Piet Sewers lie near the outskirts of Piet village, just bellow the horse pen in it. Much garbage was thrown into the sewers by Loures over the years that eventually it became over clogged and threatened to spill out into the area. Courtney, the jester of King Bruce, can explain more about this. 
Oddly enough many monster species have made the sewers their home. It consists of about 17 occupied floors with monsters. The monsters on the early levels range from simple Frogs, Crabs, Turtles and some other monsters. As one travels deeper more exotic monsters can be found such as the legendary Kraken, Gruesomefly, the hideous Gog and Gremlins. These monsters range in strength and spell casting ability from floor to floor but most love casting poison spells so ao poison is a must here. In the deepest sections the monsters enjoy casting ard sal and mor crahd spells. The difficultly and experience in the sewers is about the same as the 20th level of the Eastern Woodlands. 
While adventuring through the sewers the monsters may drop some interesting items. Every creature leaves from 1-2 kinds of body parts, some can be common and others very rare and prized. Most well known is the Gruesomefly's antennae which is needed for a monk skill. Necromancers can find Gog's Maws, and Gremlin's Ears also which are used for insight 75 and 97 summons. Also found can be an assortment of magical items, necklaces, and sometimes a few pieces of talgonite. 
Featured Monster- Gremlin  
Gremlin: The Gremlins are the masters of the Piet Sewers. Their species lives in the deepest sections with the Gog's and Rock Cobblers. They are foes to be feared for they attack with deadly jaws and poisonous bites. In addition to good physical strength they also pack a punch with ard level elemental spells and mor crahd. Not many groups who hunt here can dare face one down with ease. 
The Gremlin also will rarely drop its ear which is highly prized among Necromancers. This is used to summon the most powerful necromancer summons: The Death Knights. Although the Dark Wizard of Undine can create fake and often unstable Gremlin Ears the real things are greatly prized and easily worth several million coins. 
Rumor also has it that on the last floor of Piet Sewers which is not occupied by any monsters once lived the Gremlin King, and perhaps still does.

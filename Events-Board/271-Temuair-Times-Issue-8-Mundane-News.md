Star Theory

There has been much speculation on the origins of the lights that hang high above Temuair when the evening cloaks the light, and recently a few more theories have been added into the mix. 
Mundane researcher, Lillium Patrick, has published a speculative paper on the reasons for the mysterious lights. Lillium suggests that the stars represent sparks that are searching for the right people to make Aislings. She also suggests that each time a star disappears mysteriously, an Aisling is born.
Lillium's beliefs have brought mixed reactions from philosophers. Yet, many are intrigued by this new theory and have started to preach Lillium's theory.

Death Sickness

This past double-moon, mundane researchers have identified a rare disease that may be responsible for as many as four deaths, this Deoch alone. Apparently discovered when several Aislings and Mundanes began to complain of a progressive sickness, where the symptoms would disappear for a short time and then come back even worse.
Currently, it is unknown how the sickness is contracted, however it is speculated that such a rare disease could only be found on the lands to the far north. Regardless, it does not appear to be contagious from normal, everyday contact. For now, researchers suggest that close or intimate contact, with Aislings exhibiting symptoms of illness, should be avoided.

Floating Ice
Abel residents were stunned when a huge floating chunk of ice crashed into the harbor ports of Abel. Sailors at the pub that day described the event, "I was jus' sittin in the pub, drinkin my ale...and then suddenly the whole dang building started a shakin!"
The sailing ice destroyed three docks and the better part of a ported merchant ship. Abel's harbormaster remained optimistic, claiming that the incident will not effect any shipping routes.

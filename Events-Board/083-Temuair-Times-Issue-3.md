Foundling in Pravat 

Pravat Cave - Investigating rumors of a strange unknown creature, an exploration party searched deep into Pravat Cave. A moon ago, rumors of a ghost white humanoid creature dwelling in the cave peeked the interest of Cytra, a mundane wizardess, researcher, and devoted follower of Luathas. 

The expedition spent two days underground, returning near the surface only to renew the alliance with the Grimlok. At the end of the second day, Cytra's party returned with news. 

Cytra's group reported discovering a "wild boy." The young albino appears as a human boy of 8 to 10 Deochs age. "Light hurts his very sensitive eyes." Noted Cytra, "So we keep him enshrouded in this cloak." Cytra intends to keep the boy at her laboratory to study him and acclimate him to the world above the ground. 

The boy speaks a heavily accented Grimlok dialect of the common tongue. Cytra has pieced together some of his story. He was raised by a Grimlok worker after being discovered abandoned in a Grimlok mine shaft. No one has any speculation who the unfortunate child's parents are. Cytra says "It is sad, parents of unusual children abandon them rather than face the stigma, even in these enlightened Dark Ages"

The New Temuair Times, issue 1
Necromancy Arrives

After much anticipation, speculation and rumor, Necromancy has finally been
revealed to Aisling wizards. But was this spell truly worth the wait? 
"It doesn't have too much use in a battle", said Aisling wizard JhunJoe. "It
just uses too rare of items, but it definitely has good potential", speaks
another wizard, Heather. 
Spion Torradh uses Gargoyle Skulls, Gremlin Ears, and Gog's Maws among other
rare items to summon the undead for one's aid in battle. But the creature
summoned is often too weak to have much use in a heated battle.
I'm sure most wizards agree with Heather as well as JhunJoe when they said
they hope more is discovered about Necromancy soon. Hopefully the full
capabilities of this dark magic have not been revealed.
Mundane Burgess: Sick?
Mileth Mundane Burgess, Cedric, was rumored to be extremely ill with the
Mundane Influenza (reported in Issue 5 of the original Times) virus. Many
didn't believe this to be true; others have. 
After speaking with Cedric himself, I can assure you this was no more than a
rumor. He is "as healthy as eating a shiny red apple on a warm summer's
day," as he put it. When asked as to how the rumor could have started,
Cedric did no more than laugh and say, "Aislings will be Aislings."
Red Fern Memorial Opened
Since Deoch 6, Undine has been viciously raided over and over again by
Kobold and Goblin Shaman warriors. The Shamans would abduct the children of
Undine to be sacrificed to Arpina, their god, in a religious ceremony known
as Caroun. 
Hundreds of Aislings have gathered together and attacked the Shamans at
their home in Astrid. Many children of Undine and Aislings with courageous
hearts have perished at the hands of the Shamans. 
After over 8 Deochs of torment and chaos, with the raids of Undine still
going on, heartfelt Aislings and Mundanes have come together to create a
memorial burial ground for all of those who have died at a Shaman's hands.
"This memorial burial ground is in memory of all slain children, men and
women of Undine, as well as the brave souls who died trying to fight off the
Shamans," spoke Undine Town Leader, Pauline. 
A large red Fern has been planted on the outskirts of Undine with an
inscribed stone tablet inserted in the ground next to it to create the
memorial. The inscription reads "This Red Fern is in loving memory of all
slain by the Shamans. May their spirits be forever at peace. A ray of hope
will always shine as long as Aislings exist."

Corrupt Mileth?
written by:Lynke Raydge

This is Lynke Raydge reporting to you, representing the newly inspired guild, Sealladh. As many of you know, the Rucesion citizen Kralle was sgathed last night by Mileth officials. Was it right that this murderer be punished for what he had done?

I'd say yes, it was the right thing to do. But does this mean everybody else in Temuair should be punished? NO! Cazia, Stargazar, Beardog.. just a few of the names to mention of the Mileth officials who abused their power during the Sgathing. I, for one, was illegally banished from Mileth. How long are we going to sit back and let these officials run our town the wrong way? They say they're going to change many things, be fair, help out young aislings and such, but once they get elected, what happens?

NOTHING

That's exactly what.

I guess it's okay now for officials to make their own laws, even if they aren't legal or even written on the law board. Is there anything we can do to stop Mileth officials from what they've already began to do? Can we even fix this, or will we always be screwed over for Deoch's to come? Hopefully next time someone asks you to cast your vote for them, you'll think twice about who you're voting for. 

Many people, including the entire guild of Sealladh, are very upset and are talking to the mundanes about this very issue. Hopefully you'll help support us and get some better and more legitimate office holders on duty. Thanks for taking your time to ready this, and remember what I said:

" Think twice about who you're voting for "

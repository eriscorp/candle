Age Old Formula

Dar, the well-known master mundane wizard of Mileth, accidentally stumbled across a formula while he was cleaning his many tomes of ancient books. The formula that he discovered is a long forgotten concoction that was used during the many wars that followed the formation of the 'Pact of Anaman'.
Dar explains: "After the Pact of Anaman was completed... the dubhaimd, that had appeared eight hundred years earlier, began to grow much more powerful. It came to the point where even a touch from a dark creature could rot the flesh of an attacker. The formula that I just recently uncovered was mixed and then poured on to the skin of a warrior that was about to do battle against the darkness. The coating effect that the potion had... blocked the rotting touches of the dubhaimd! Without this amazing mixture... Temuair would have fallen long ago."
Even though the potion is useless now that the dubhaimd are without their old powers, Dar is contented to keep the formula under tight wraps.

Discovery By Sea: Follow up

As reported in issue 3 of the Temuair Times... mundane navigator, Kessi, set sail to find new lands to explore almost a Deoch ago. She believed that the Mundanes' fear of Kadath kept Temuair from discovering new and exotic lands.
The ship with Kessi and her crew returned to Rucesion this past moon. With it, the ship brought only questions. The mundane Rucesion port manager recalls his inspection of the returned vessel."It was eerie. There were no signs of the crew, the food rations were rotted, and all documentation of the trip seemed to be missing... she was literally a ghost ship!"
Loures chancellor Jean didn't seem surprised. "We had told her that Kadath was a place of no return. Why she did not heed our warning about sailing north... I will never know."
Rucesion authorities conducted a lengthy investigation, and they concluded that it was indeed supernatural means that had brought the demise of the expedition.

Mountain Expedition

The Soumi Mountains are a dangerous place of falling rockslides, deadly animal life, and bitter cold. All that, however, does not stop Jaquise from returning to the mountain range every Fall. 
Jaquise, a humble tavern owner, had made his Fall trek to the mountains every year for the past fifteen Deoch. "There is not much to be seen, just the animals, the rock, and the nearly endless climb," attested Jaquise.
Jaquise will be making his trip into the Soumi mountain range soon. If your lucky you will see him before he begins his annual accent.

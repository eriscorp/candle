# Plagarists caught

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 7th Moon, 31st Sun · Post #166_

To All Aislings,

There has always been those few who have attempted to submit other's works as their own and attempt to win the various contests within our lands.  In the past, the mundanes have attempted their best to monitor those troubles and correct them when found.  However, a recent Aisling award winner was just found out to be a plagarist.  There is a final confirmation on this case but all evidence points to the fact that this Aisling stole an existing piece of Art and attempted to pass it off as his own.  His punishment has been the erradication of any legends he may have created during his life as an Aisling.

Any attempt a plagarism can result in such a punishment or complete banishment from Temuair.

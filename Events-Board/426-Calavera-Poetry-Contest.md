</span><span style="color:#b4b2b4">((A calavera poem, is a type of satirical Mexican poetry traditionally written around Dia de Muertos (Day of the Dead))). These short, humorous verses personify death in playful ways, often imagining the subject's demise or poking fun at their personality, habits, or quirks. Despite their dark theme, calaveras are meant to be lighthearted and are written to honor friends, family members, public figures, or even fictional characters.

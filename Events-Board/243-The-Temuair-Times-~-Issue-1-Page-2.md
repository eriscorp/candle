Mehadi Swamp Flowers Bloom
The Jivanta Mukul (Flower Keeper) has made it known that a record number of sacred flowers, that are grown in a secluded place inside the Mehadi swamp, have bloomed this growing season. Speculation has arisen about that unusual blooming and signs are pointing to the upcoming Adoration of Danaan.
"The Adoration has been delayed for sacred reasons, which may be causing the flowers to receive an abundant source of the Light element," said the Jivanta.
The Tulsi, Sevti, and Nila blossoms have all been privileged to the phenomena. Aislings can be assured that all three of the flowers will be in plentiful amounts.

King Oren Still Missing
Even with the Master classes of Aislings beating down the pirate threat, the King of Oren remains in hiding. The king's royal guard has announced that the king will unveil himself once they consider the threat to be eliminated. He urges Aislings to continue slaying the vile raiders so that peace can return to the lands of Oren. 
Only the strongest and the most skillful are advised to aid the town of Oren, as the pirates are unusually powerful.

All aboard! Lynith Beach Opened.

This past moon, passenger ships finally set sail to the newly discovered Lynith Beach! The beach, itself, consists of several beautiful areas, and a fresh new look at the Oren ocean by the seashore. 
The sands of the beach are rich with lost treasures... perhaps from a long lost Aisling settlement. Many Aislings have been reporting discoveries such as... several types of nice clothing, strange weapons, and odd items of many kinds.
However... also found on the beach is the pirate ship which raided the kingdom of Oren. Aislings have begun to infiltrate the pirates' flag ship and have brought back news of a secret hidden treasure!

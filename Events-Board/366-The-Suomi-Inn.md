Greetings, Aislings. My name is Eeva, and I was recently approached by the Suomi Troupe to host the new Suomi Inn. I am excited and look forward to meeting all of you who come to visit me in my humble inn... Once it is complete.
Several calamities have struck our workers, and the preparations are falling behind schedule! Vidar, a kind man who has offered to build the inn needs strong Aislings to help him with some jobs. I also require energetic Aislings to visit the inns of Mileth, Rucesion and Tagor to examine their plans and help create a new design. Finally, Bertil and Hadrian have prepared holy blessing and magical protection rituals for Aislings to memorize, to bless the land the inn shall be built on.
Thank you for your help, Aislings! I look forward to seeing you in my cozy inn, once it is completed.
Eeva, Suomi Innkeeper

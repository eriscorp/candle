"Priestess, Undercover. Mission: Peasant"     

       As many others have remarked, the cobbled streets of Mileth are a dangerous place for those who value their sanity. Not only are the crowds daunting, but it tends to be a place for younger Aislings to gather. Many younger Aislings are articulate, kind beings, but a few still have the accent that I (uncompassionately) refer to as 'Mileth gutter-rat.' "plz give me gld" they'll ask, and one's ears strain to understand the poor beings. So I thought the best way to see Temuair from their point of view would be to hide amongst them and learn their ways, learn how to teach them best to earn a standing in society or, failing that, perhaps get to make a little "gld."      
   
      Donning a peasant dress, blue boots (which I think most of us still remember as quite ill-fitting) and arming myself with a stick found on the ground, I approached the Mileth Altar, where the peasants seem to gather. My ears were immediately assaulted with heresy. I rallied, I must say, magnificently and joined a cluster of similarly garbed citizens. After a bit, I began to understand their strange tongue. "Mny plz" seems to translate best as "I am new to my Spark, and have yet to learn to make my way in this land. Can you patiently tell me how to earn my living?" The odd phrase "will u be gf?"  is apparently a type of mating call to other Aislings, and it is tragic that they are unaware that this remark tends to repulse the mate in question. The curious, oft-heard "u suck" seems to be the equivalent of gaining rank in the herd, as I noted when two young males began hitting each other with sticks while repeating the phrase again and again.

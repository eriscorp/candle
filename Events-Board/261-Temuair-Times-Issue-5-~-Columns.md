Horoscopes
written by: Xenek

The horoscopes are based upon Jean's Theology of the Octave in Years, and determined by the Deoch one became an aisling.  Each year is represented by a creature in the Aisling Zodiac, based once again upon the Octave and the Gods...


The Gog ~ Deochs 1, 9, 17, 25 ~ Year of Deoch

     Word has it that the mundanes are preparing for another aisling contest.  Use Deoch's help and put that spark to work, this could be your chance to take the prize.
The Faerie ~ Deochs 2, 10, 18, 26 ~ Year of Glioca
     With the fall season here, take this chance to realize and appreciate things the way they are.  Remember, things change and won't always be the way you want them to.
The Shrieker ~ Deochs 3, 11, 19, 27 ~ Year of Cail
     Winter is coming soon.  Allow yourself to shift with the wind into the new season.  Don't take those grudges and bad moods with you.
The Polyp ~ Deochs 4, 12, 20, 28 ~ Year of Luathas
     Your Deoch may have passed, but that doesn't mean things have to get any worse for you.  Keep up with your tasks and don't lag behind with things you need to get done.
The Rock Cobbler ~ Deochs 5, 13, 21, *29* ~ Year of Gramail
     Your Deoch is drawing nearer to its send, so take advantage of the time that you have left.  Tie up any loose ends you may have and try to make those ends meet.
The Marauder ~ Deochs 6, 14, 22 ~ Year of Fiosachd
       Like the ants before the blizzard, you might want to get yourself prepared for anything that may go wrong with the coming winter.  Even if nothing does happen, better safe than sorry.
The Orc ~ Deochs 7, 15, 23 ~ Year of Ceannlaidir
     Even though things may seem to be slowing down everywhere, don't go into hiding.  Try working on some skills or even spells.  It couldn't hurt to keep them warmed up.
The Succubus ~ Deochs 8, 16, 24 ~ Year of Sgrios
     Don't leave yourself open to everyone and everything.  Even if you don't notice, something may cause you harm, even if you think you're impenetrable.

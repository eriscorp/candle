# A Night at the Theatre

_Posted by Eduardo — 6th Moon, 21st Sun · Post #348_

Come one, come all, come great, come small, come young, come old, come shy, and bold, to the Garamonde Theatre, of northern Suomi!
Let it be know in all the land that the Temuair Theatre Group has prepared yet another delightful presentation for your viewing pleasure. This next play tells of the adventures of a daring rogue named Vall, and his dealings with both royalty and an ogre.
The curtain will rise in but little over one doublemoon. ((March 11th, 5 PM PST 8 EST, 1 AM GMT)) Additional preformances by request and availability.
Please remember to bring your manners, as any troublemakers will quickly find themselves wishing they were not.
Also, any aisling interested in a possible understudy role is welcome to contact their local theatre director.
Omner Garamonde
Theatre Director

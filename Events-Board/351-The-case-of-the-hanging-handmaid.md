Case of the Hanging Handmaid
The facts:  One of the handmaids who worked in Loures Castle was found murdered shortly after breakfast.  Her body was hanging by her neck just above the bed she slept in.

The case: Your goal is to figure out who commited the crime, how and why.
Method: Right now there are 15 members of the Valkyries guild scattered inside Loures Castle.  Each is playing a part based on what happened in the murder.  You need to talk to them about the case.  Some of what they know is important, some is designed to mislead or confuse.  But the answer is out there.

Winning: to win you must walk to the fountain at the entrance of the castle and whisper to Kivrin your solution to the case.  Winners will be announced once all detectives have either given their report, or decided to stop investigating.
Rules: whispers will only be answered if you are in the same room as the Valkyrie you are whispering.  No whispering from afar
Also, all the participating Valkyries will remain inside Loures Castle for the event.
Kivrin will be standing by the fountain at the entrance and will answer questions.
Note: only 2 aislings know who is the murderer at the time this is posted,  Kivrin, and the aisling who is playing the murderer.  Please don't randomly ask who it is.  The idea is to have fun investigating the case.

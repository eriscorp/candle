HoroScopes
by: Xenek

The horoscopes are based upon Jean's Theology of the Octave in Years, and determined by the Deoch one became an aisling.  Each year is represented by a creature in the Aisling Zodiac, based once again upon the Octave and the Gods...


The Gog ~ Deochs 1, 9, 17, 25 ~ Year of Deoch

     Now is a good time to try new things.  Take advantage of this time and go all out.  Try writing some poetry, a story, or maybe even some art.  You may be surprised by what you can accomplish.
The Faerie ~ Deochs 2, 10, 18, 26 ~ Year of Glioca
     If things seem to be catching up to you and giving you stress, take a load off and relax a bit.  Taking a walk through a scenic path might not be such a bad idea to help you take a break from the hustles and bustles of Temuair.
The Shrieker ~ Deochs 3, 11, 19, 27 ~ Year of Cail
     With the season now changed into winter, it wouldn't be so bad for you to have a change of your own.  Maybe a change of clothing wouldn't be so bad, or even a new hairstyle if you're feeling bold.  Try attending the mass of another God, or hunting in a different place just to try something unusual.
The Polyp ~ Deochs 4, 12, 20, 28 ~ Year of Luathas
     This Deoch is your time to shine.  Try working on a new project or goal before the Deoch is over, you might gain something new and satisfying.  Yet, don't try to hard, or you'll find yourself overwhelmed.
The Rock Cobbler ~ Deochs 5, 13, 21 ~ Year of Gramail
     Try not to become too involved in too many matters.  Make sure you take time to worry about others and their needs for a while.  However, don't get so involved with people that you end up forgetting about everything else.  Find a balance between them.
The Marauder ~ Deochs 6, 14, 22 ~ Year of Fiosachd
       If things don't seem to be going your way, find some time to appreciate what you already have, even if it isn't much.  Don't give up on your goals and inner needs, you'll get to them someway or another.
The Orc ~ Deochs 7, 15, 23 ~ Year of Ceannlaidir
     When things seem to get on your nerves, try looking at them from more than one point of view.  It is important that you understand the full aspects of a situation before you make your judgment.  It'll all be clear soon enough.
The Succubus ~ Deochs 8, 16, 24 ~ Year of Sgrios
     Winter is the season for you to take advantage of.  Don't let everyone else tell you what to do.  Stand up for yourself and what you believe.  You know what you need, so don't let anyone try to tell you any different.

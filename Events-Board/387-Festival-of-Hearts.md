 Greetings Aislings!

 Our lady Glioca has gained high favor among aislings, and the Suomi
 Troupe is hosting a Festival of Hearts in her honor. Come join us over
 the course of two eight-sun cycles (( two days )) for games and
 contests in the spirit of celebrating love.

 Wear your hearts on your sleeves during the Dating Game, take a chance
 on a regal frog or two, compete against others with your silver tongue
 in our Love Ballad Contest, compete with others in the battle of wits
 during the trivia contest, and fight for the hand of another during the
 Love is a Battlefield event.

 We hope that everyone can make it out to the games and to celebrate the
 goddess of love this upcoming season. If any aislings are feeling
 especially festive, please contact me to become a volunteer as part of
 the Dating Game event.

 Thank you,
 - Topic

# Case of the Bobbing Boy

_Posted by Kivrin — 9th Moon, 11th Sun · Post #374_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">

Horror of horror!  The body of a young boy was pulled from Loures Harbor this morning.  Although people rushed to his aid the local healer declared him dead shortly after being awaken from her sleep.

The people of the village swear that the boy had a huge lump on the back of his head like he'd been struck and thrown into the water.  Its well known that he would often taunt inhabitants of the harbor with threats to inform the authorities about questionable activities he supposedly witnessed.  The King is livid that one so young might have been murdered to conceal another crime and is demanding an investigation.  The people of Loures Harbor have been ordered to stand by and make themselves available for an investigation.

Can you solve the "Case of the Bobbing Boy?"   are you the detective who can find out who murdered the poor lad?

If you think you are come and be an investigator and test your detective skills.

Interested aislings will be able to obtain a list of suspects from Kivrin, who will be at the entrance to the harbor, at the start of the event along with more background about the case.  Interview the suspects and then whisper Kivrin with your theory as to who commited the crime, how and why.  Winners will receive a legend mark for solving the case.))

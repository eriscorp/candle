Come one, come all to Loures Harbor for the first ever Temaurian fishing tournament, sponsored by the Valkyries guild.
Date: ((July/31/06 at 5:10pm pst)).
Prizes
First : 2 million
Second:1 million
Third:500k
Fourth:250k
Rules:
First one to bring a specific list of fish to DuenanKnute at the Loures Harbor pier win. However, there is a minimum time limit and DuenanKnute and Shadowkitten will be around to peek contestants to confirm they don't have fish stockpiled before they begin. Fish will not be accepted before the time limit. Attempting to turn fish in early, or to bring fish in that were not caught at Loures Harbor during the contest will result in disqualification. This gives everyone a chance, and prevents use of arcane magiks and/or explosives.

Jean, the leader of the Loures High Council, writes:
Some four deochs ago a mysterious object was reported to have been recovered from a mine outside of Tagor (see the events of Temuair board). Few knew what it was or what it meant.
Investigations have shown the mysterious object is but the latest piece in a mystery spanning back to Deoch 33, starting with the murder of then Loures Councilor Marcus Aurilius Antonius. Through six different Novus Imperia coordinated events over the deochs aislings have tried to and unravel a mystery that has become ever more complicated with each passing event.
As revealed in "Mysteries of the Dubhaim" something has returned to Temuair - something long forgotten. It has been lying dormant for nearly two decades since the breaking of the Seal of Temuair and gathering strength. It seems now that its time to awaken is near.
Several sinister organizations are aiding in this. The necromancer order seems to have been actively pursuing whatever this thing is for over 10 deochs now. Even remnants of the Pact of Anaman seem to have a stake in it.
What will happen when it awakens? No one knows.

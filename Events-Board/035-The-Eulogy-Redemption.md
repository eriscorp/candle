The prophecy of The Eulogy and The Eulogy: Invocation, three summers ago, dances in the imagination of the Grimlok, Kobolds, and Mukul. 
On Deoch-week, Cail-day's evening, Hesperos, the evening star aligns with the smile of Danaan, the sun. The devotees begin their redemption ritual. In Pravat the Grimlok worship Adranuch, their Lady of Gold. In Astrid the Kobolds worship Arpina, their All-Mother. In Mehadi the Mukul worship Matrika, their Goddess of the Sun-heart. 
Perhaps a gentle Aisling may worship alongside at any of these, provided she does not slay one of their race and is devote, so has not worshipped at one of the other two sites. 
The priests of these races an artifact bestowed at the first Eulogy. In the three artifacts' combination by three Aislings, with proper ritual, the essence of the Great Deity may be reawakened. 
The priests implore Aislings to repel any dubhaimid or Anaman interference.

** Etiquette Class **
((This is an introductory class on how to roleplay to help teach people about roleplaying in Dark Ages))
** Solstice Ball **
The Suomi Troupe requests your presence at the Solstice Ball held in honor of King Bruce of Loures. Please join the Lords and Ladies of the Court in the Oren Ballroom for an evening of festivities and fashion.
** Heraldic Costume Contest **
You may represent any city of your choice. The contest is open to your interpretation of your city's heraldry but might include common dyes or themes found in that area. 

There will be two categories: Local Fashion only ((Items found in-game, no Kruna items with the exception of permanent alterations such as skin color and hair dye))

High Fashion: ((Kruna shop items and accessories are permitted in addition to regular in-game items))

For questions or assistance dyeing armors contact: Theowin, SirNair, Snooze, or Claiss.

** Charades **
Gather at the Garamonde Theatre in Suomi where the Troupe will act out various Mundanes for the audience to guess. Prizes, laughs, and fun all around.

** History of Loures - Undine Tension **
Learn about the long, dark, and bloody history of tension between Loures and Undine. Aisling chatter has reached a fever pitch.

** Last Decree of Lord Undine **
Nearly 500 years ago, the last Lord of Undine signed a treaty annexing Undine to Loures - or did he? Unearth the Last Decree of Lord Undine.

** Town Recruitment Faire **
Have you found yourself wondering what it was that drew you to your nation in the first place? Come to the Faire for information and a Q&amp;A on what each town has to offer.

** Erbie Egg Rescue **
Erbies have found their way into the Suomi Tailors dyes and now their eggs are fabulous colors and are being left behind by confused mothers! Please help recover their eggs. I fear some eggs have been taken by some dangerous creatures!

((There will be a timed first portion followed by a secret second part that will require forming groups. Bring warranty bags to combat challenges together))

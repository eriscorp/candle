Jean, who provided lore of Mehadi Flowers during The Pact, writes about the appearance of a something that bears resemblance to a historical sketch of a member of the Pact of Anaman: 

It becomes increasingly difficult to maintain official composure, due to the onslaught of dubhaimid terror. 

As the priest of each Holy place reached the height of their Second Rite, a horned shadow-clad form, lined with fine crimsom robes and daunting plates of dark metal mail appeared. 

His appearance drew all breath out of the worshippers. His eyes projected fear. His foul voice summoned dubhaimid. His arcane ritual of otherwordly origin caused all gathered to tremble and see visages of the Other Gods. 
Gods never meant to be seen. Gruesome and incomprehensible sigils and glyphs of Aosdic or older origin covered Aisling worshippers and brought them. 

The form spoke haunting hints at truths this mundane would rather never imagine. 

Jean
Councilor of Loures

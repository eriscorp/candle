Hadrian, Dark Wizard of Suomi, shares grim speculation on the Red Moon's impending return:

 "Countless Deochs have passed since the Red Moon first bled into the 
  still night, and many more since the death of the demon sorcerer who 
  wrought the curse. Yet that which dies does not truly die; it remains, 
  decays, and returns in horror. Whispers speak of an emergent cult 
  plotting to revive the sorcerer's fallen order.

  Seduced by the Red Moon's ominous power, the Children of the Blood Moon 
  meddle in forbidden magic to carry on the sorcerer's legacy. The 
  cultists conspire in shadow, leaving prophecy as the only means to 
  pierce their veil."

Taking heed of Hadrian's warnings, Suomi calls upon Aislings to join in efforts to foil the cultists' schemes.

Two-sentence Horror Story
*************************

Our first task is an unusual one. By invoking an ancient soothsayer tradition, we can conjure glimpses of terror that will soon unfold in Temuair. These fragments of vision are imperfect, yet perhaps they will unveil horrors that lie in wait.

Two-sentence horror tells a story in just two lines. The first line sets the scene; the second twists it into terror with additional context. The prophecy is found in what is left unsaid:

 "The Red Moon rose, painting the fields crimson as I walked alone. Only 
  when the reflection followed me into the river did I realize it wasn't 
  the sky that bled."

 "The Mileth tailor measured me with a smile, muttering he needed 'more 
  material.' When he cut the thread, I realized it wasn't cloth tightening 
  around my throat."

Wolves of the Blood Moon
************************

 "We had no business wandering the woods after dusk; we'd all heard 
  Torbjorn's tale, yet Tomas was somehow none the wiser. There we found 
  Tomas, hunched just beyond the treeline by the stream. I remember it all 
  so vividly--Tomas' scream as his jaw snapped forward, flesh splitting at 
  the seams to make way for a snout of bone and teeth. I was fortunate to 
  escape when his attention turned to my companions, though their dying 
  cries still haunt me..." 

Reports from the villagers suggest the cultists may have already begun channeling the nascent Red Moon, now a visible waxing crescent. Who among us carries the affliction? Use your detective skills to identify the werewolves in disguise. Or perhaps you will pounce on your victims in the dead of night.

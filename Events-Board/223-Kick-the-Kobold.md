The first side to do this five times will be the winner of the match.  A side will have to win 3 matches total.  The first matchs will be staggered into the following time periods:

((Wednesday, 7pm PST))
((Thursday, 2pm PST))
((Sunday, 10am PST))

The final time will hold the matches until a victor is chosen.  Note, that seating is limited so if you cannot make it to one match, you will need to try for another.

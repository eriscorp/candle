# Black Market - Braz, Smuggler of Rucesion

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 8th Moon, 6th Sun · Post #10_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Braz sets up a black market in an abandoned shop in Rucesion. Only rogues with 11 or more insights may enter. 
He scrawls: 
I buy several wares not bought elsewhere. I have wares in stock that are often not in stock elsewhere. My prices are higher, because of the risky business. If there's something you're willing to pay big money for, post it and if I can get a hold of it, I'll sell it. My goods and demand are subject to change. My prices are subject to go up or down at any time, with no notice. 
- Braz

Emerald Ring
Spinel Ring
Wine

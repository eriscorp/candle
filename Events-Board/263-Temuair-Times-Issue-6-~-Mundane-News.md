Loures Occupation Continues

Well over twenty Deochs ago, the Loures army seized control of the Undine region of  the Soumi province. Today, the domination by the Loures army continues, denying Undine it's freedom. The 'Liberation Organization of Undine' has constantly clashed with Loures peacekeepers for those twenty Deochs. 
Even though it seems like the violence is never-ending, the leader of the 'LOU' remains optimistic... "The time of Undine's liberation is near. The recent goblin raid on Loures castle has shown that the Loures kingdom has lost it's ally in Astrid, and they will have no further reason to exploit Undine the way they have for so long," 
Currently, there is no official word by Loures army generals. However, with the new threat that has risen around Loures.. the bulk of the Loures army has set sail away from Undine.

# Ranger Twila

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 1st Moon, 20th Sun · Post #148_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Twila has been appointed as the first Ranger of Temuair.  Her new duties will include being able to remove Sleep Fighters ((Auto Hunters)) and place them into the Loures Dungeon as well as arrest troublesome exiled Aislings anywhere in the Kingdom.  

Note, a Ranger's jurisdiction is over those who have been banished or exiled from other towns.  So plese, go through those political channels first.  A Ranger is not meant to be a replacement of the existing political powers but rather an aid to effect law where the normal judicial branches cannot reach.

Ramanayan Caesar, guild leader of Novus Imperia, writes:
Novus Imperia will be, for the final time, coordinating efforts to finally bring a conclusion to this deoch spanning mystery as recently written by High Councilor Jean. Over the deochs hundreds of aislings have come forth to join us in epic events and adventures. This is the final chapter my friends and we encourage all to come out and try to seek the truth.
((Saga of the Darkness will be held on Friday September 30th at 5:00 PM PST, 6:00 MST, 7:00 CST, 8:00 EST. The event will begin in the Loures Castle Throne Room. Aislings will form groups of 3 to 5 for this event. All groups will either be composed of all masters (or higher) or all non masters (above insight 10). No mixing is allowed. The event is all but the same for both types of groups however several sections are tweaked for masters or non-masters.
The groups will then venture to different locales across Temuair where they will engage in puzzle solving, adventure, questing, task completion, and some light combat if needed. The event will feature a rich and unique plot filled with twists and turns, references to Temurian history and culture, and much more.
Unlike previous Novus Imperia events the Saga of the Darkness is more non-linear in scope and is planned to provide a fresh challenge to aislings both young and old. Also for the very first time in our events all groups, not just the winners, will be able to fully complete the event within an allotted time frame. The event endings for each group may be completely different based on that group's choices during the event.
There will be three winning groups each of whom shall receive one of three unique legend marks created for this event. Groups finishing after these three will likely still receive some kind of a reward.
For more information the history behind Saga of the Darkness and the previous events leading up to it visit its official website here at http://www.s92617579.onlinehome.us/events/saga/saga.htm ))

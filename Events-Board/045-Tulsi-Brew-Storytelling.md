# Tulsi Brew Storytelling

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 12th Moon, 18th Sun · Post #45_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
"Story Telling night" -- where all Aislings are welcome to participate in songs and stories and poems, and entertain the patrons of the tavern. 
Those that participate, will be taken to the school at 10 p.m. PDT and awarded for their telling of tales. 

The Tulsi Brew is nestled in the city of Piet and was formerly known as "Bella's Fine liqueurs from the Fields of Piet." 
The Tulsi Brew was recently purchased from the former owner, Bella, for an undisclosed amount of money by a few nobles of Loures that wished to remain nameless. 

Strangely enough, the nobles of Loures wasted no time in turning the owernship of the establishment to -- the "Pact of Anaman" or, Anaman Pact, as they go by. The Anaman Pact is a motley of Aislings, who are on the more "shadowed" side of things. 

It is my understanding that the tavern will be open at all times. In the absence of one of the motley members of Anaman Pact, Lia has agreed to manage the pub after Bella's tragic disappearance. 

The tavern will be open to the public, and anyone is welcome to come and enjoy the festives whenever they wish. Despite the fact that there will be times when no Amanan Pact member will be there to host the tavern. 

Jontus Loures Reporter 



(( Story Telling Night: Fridays. 9 - 10 p.m PDT ))

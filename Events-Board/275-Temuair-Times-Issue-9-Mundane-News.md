Rain Drops

This moon not only had rain for the lands of Temuair... but it also packed bitter cold temperatures and fierce winds, which combined into one of the most dangerous storms in the past five Deochs.
According to several sources, the areas of Mileth down through Loures were inflicted with several destroyed homes and fallen elder trees. The Isle of Dawn had also had it's fair share of damage, as several ships were forced to run aground on to the mainland. Many merchants are expected to be a double-moon behind, when everything is said and done.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Wine Shortage

It was much as the grape farmers of Soumi had predicted, because of the early winter and the less than impressive crop last Deoch...there is a slight shortage in the flask at many Temuairian Taverns.
"Spring is the merry season, and also when we have to most customers," one barmaid spoke, "with the shortage of wine, I would best expect many angry customers when the bottles are all empty."
Sadly, the troubles don't stop there, as farmers are predicting and even worse growing season this Deoch.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Speaking out of Turn

Shocking news made it's way around Loures this double-moon. It appears that an aid to King Bruce, of Loures, had his head removed when he interrupted a speech being given by the king, himself. 
The king had been addressing several rumors about dark experiments being conducted in the castle sub-cellars, when, suddenly, the aid stood and openly questioned the validity of the king's explanation.
Quickly, the aid was drug, supposedly into the sub-cellars, and beheaded for his arrogance.

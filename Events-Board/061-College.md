Classes are held at Mileth College, which is just south of Glioca's Shrine, outside of Mileth.  All schedules are subject to change faster than can be relayed by mundanes.  Check the classroom tablet and the tablet by the Mileth Library. (( All times are OOC ))

Classes may be given by a recognized talent in that subject.  Classes offer an alternative method of earning experience.  Classes require over half of your time available.  If you have crafted, enchanted, or otherwise labored much in the day you will not have time to reap experience of the class.

More importantly, classes offer knowledge.  The class transmits wisdom and knowledge.  The instructor of the class determines who has passed the class and therefore gains experience.  Pay attention and a level of respect expected at any class and you will gain experience and wisdom.  

Most instructors require you to be on time.  For details and updates, go to Mileth College and write the teacher.

Should a date, time, or name be added, removed, or changed? Send updates to the mundane chronicler (( www.darkages.com/community/news , Click "Submit an Event" )).

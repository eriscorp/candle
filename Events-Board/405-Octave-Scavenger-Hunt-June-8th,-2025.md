Aislings,
The Octave calls! Will you follow its path? Join the Suomi Troupe in the coming moons for the Octave Aisling Scavenger Hunt! There will be two circuits: 

 o Gear of the Gods - for newly sparked Aislings (( new players ))

 o Pilgrimage of the Octave - for veterans 

Newly sparked Aislings are welcome to challenge themselves in the Pilgrimage if they wish.

A special Octave Mass will follow the Pilgrimage. 

Please see below for full event details and times. We hope to see you there!


Gear of the Gods
****************
FOR NEWLY SPARKED

The Temuair deities are bestowing divine armaments and blessings upon the newly sparked who complete this scavenger circuit. No preparation is needed, though you will want to group with others in your insight range.

Location: Oren Dock (Oren entrance) 
Date: (( June 8 at 3:30pm PST / 5:30 CT / 6:30pm EST ))


Pilgrimage of the Octave
************************
FOR VETERAN AISLINGS

In this sacred geas of the Octave, you will decipher clues to uncover the locations of clergy members. The first three Aislings to reach the final destination will be rewarded with best-in-class gear. The first-place winner will also have a unique mark engraved upon their legend.

There will also be a special finale! Following the hunt, Aislings of all faiths are invited to take part in an Octave mass, during which a clergy member of each faith will perform mass.

Location: visit the Shrine of Danaan (Blackstar Village), where the full rules and details will be shared by the main host, Solei.

Date: (( June 8 at 4:30pm PST / 6:30 CT / 7:30pm EST ))

# Great Temuirian Sage trials!

_Posted by Eduardo — 4th Moon, 26th Sun · Post #294_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Welcome to the Great Temuirian Sage trials! Aislings have gathered here to test their knowledge about anything and everything and to be recognized as wise aislings. 
   Contestants will compete in teams of two or for those confidant aislings who dare think they know everything, their own solitary team of one aisling. 
   Through each round each team will be asked 5 random questions from a pool of questions amassed by the sages of Temuair. If a team fails to answer a question correctly it will be open for whoever shouts first. If a team answers a question correctly then they will be given up a followup question to the first one whereby the may possibly double their points. 
   At the end of each round the team having answered the least amount of correct answers will be given a pat on the back for having participated as well as a small prize. If two teams are tied to be eliminated then an athar round shall take place, in which the two tied teams must answer questions as fast as the namesake element moves. Whoever is first to answer a question correctly will move on and the other team will step down. 
   Another athar round will occur during the final round when only two teams remain. In order to become recognized as an aisling of great sage wisdom, one team must shout out more correct answers before another team before the time limit is up and the questions cease. 
   Good luck to you all and may Luathas grant you a small part of the Gnosis to aide ye!
 *5 teams will compete in this game
*Each round, five questions will be asked.  The team with the least correct answers will be eliminated
*During the athar round it will be a version of "buzzing in" though we lack a buzzer, a perfectly spelled, perfectly right answer will be what gets you a point. Spelling will not matter until these rounds where you must shout it out before the other team. This is to avoid "the answer is udnen lkear" and then when they have a chance to think they about it they may come up with the right answer.
*The prize for the sage contest itself will be a unique legend mark that is in itself more precious than any material item
*Consolation prizes will be given out.

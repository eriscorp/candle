Dear Aislings, 
It was my great pleasure to be a part of this Mileth Fair. During that full phase of a moon, each aspect of your beauty shown through. Contest, event, and game hosts entertained us and gave Aislings the opportunity to shine, in ways that mundanes can greyly dream of but cannot actualize. Merchants provided many things. They are paid in kind, but let them also be paid in kindness for exchanging their treasures with us. Contest entrants hefted Valor. To attempt, each has begun the road to nobility. Guards and Demagogues wisely empowered Aislings and protected the precious gifts. And the Fair Committee wove each thread together. 
Fairgoers, for you we offered this. By participating, each of you sweetens the day's air for moons to come.

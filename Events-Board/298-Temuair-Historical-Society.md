Temuair Historical Society 

In these dark times accurate information and knowledge of history is 
sometimes hard to come by. We must preserve and understand the past for a 
better future. I have thus prepared to open membership to the new Temuair 
Historical Society. The Temuair Historical Society is a group of aislings 
devoted and interested in the history of Temuair. Some of the things planned 
for the society are:

-Mailing List for information on History Classes
-Society News Letter
-Meetings, Lectures, and Discussions 
-Society Hosted events to help aislings learn more about history -Reenactments of battles and other historical events 
-and more!

If ye are interested in joining the society please send me a pigeon mail with 
your name, insight, path, and your current experience and knowledge in 
history. There are no requirements to join, save NO HERETICS are permitted. 
Once I have your name you will be on the mailing list for information on 
meetings, upcoming classes and the like. 

If ye have any questions please feel free to give me a whisper or send a 
mailing my way. 

-Lord Ramanayan Caesar
Historian, Temuair Historical Society Founder

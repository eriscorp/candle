Tools of the Trade
   Strength in arena relies on a number of different factors; skills and class being the main ones.
 	  Having a high ammount of health ((and having your statistics at their maximum)) is an important step on becoming a good arena fighter, but without the skills and the knowledge of how to use them, your health ((and statistics)) becomes un-important.
	How do you become a good arena fighter?  The first step is to choose a class or path (take for example a warrior), then train hard to reach your 99th level of insight. After reaching that point, one must make a decision that will determine your future both in Temuair and in the arena as well. When you reach the 99th insight you get the choice to choose from mastering as pure path or to sub-path to one of the other classes.
   	If you choose to become a pure pathed master of your class, you will get certain benefits, such as better weaponry and equipment and a few skills only available to pure path masters. On the other hand, if you choose to sub-path, you will have to start back from the begining, but the rewards are great; for example, you will be able to use skills and spells from both your initial path and your new one.  Sub-pathing has lead to a few class combinations that are extremely powerful, such as monk-warriors, and wizard-priest, these being the most popular classes for arena fighting. With the new added skills, pure path warriors are not to be taken lightly, being able to deflect back both skills and spells. Another class which is suprisingly good in the arena are priest-monks, being able to heal, prahm, and unfreeze themselves, and still have the firepower monks posses.
 	 On the next issue of Halls of Combat we will go in depth into the strongest class in arena, wizard-priest, also I will be interviewing Krytos; one of the most re-knowned Aislings in Temuair and also one of the most skilled arena fighters i have encountered.

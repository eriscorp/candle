# Warrior's Honor Fields Open...

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 8th Moon, 2nd Sun · Post #120_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Since the Coliseum had opened its doors, the warriors of Temuair have felt that something was missing. That the true test of combat was never felt there. That there was no fear of death and, therefore, no real thrill of victory. 
Then, a Stranger happened upon an isolated coast, and enlisting the help of the lonly shopkeeper, Ase, had begun to put together secret Pit fights. These fights are for warriors only, and are to test the skill of one on one combat. The fight is not over until one of them are dead. 
So, if you are of warrior blood and wish to make combat within the pit, for the ultimate challenge against another, find an opponent of close insight to you ((3 levels)), make both of your ways to Ase's shop and ask to be put upon the Honor Field of your insight ((say "Honor Field [Insight level]")).

((Thursday, Friday and Saturday, March 16-18)) and ((Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, March 23-25)) ((The open hours on all scheduled days are from 3:00 - 4:30 pm PST.))

All gold collected will go towards the prizes of this event. If you wish to participate in this event, this is the time to register. If we have not chosen 40 finalists by the time of this event, we will allow late entries at a substantially higher fee.

# Murder Mystery of Loures

_Posted by Eduardo — 4th Moon, 25th Sun · Post #292_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Shocking news has just been heard from Loures Castle, member of the Loures 
High Council Marcus Aurelius Antoninus was found stabbed to death in his 
study just outside of Loures Castle. Loures investigators believe the body 
was moved from another unknown location to the study.
Many look to comparisons of the murder of former High Council member Cyril in Deoch 6, just a little over 25 Deochs ago. Investigators also report that there are many clues to follow that might lead to the killer. However these leads are many and with the Goblin army still close to Loures territory man power is short to investigate. 
The government of Loures is requesting the assistance of aislings to help 
find out what exactly happened to Consular Marcus. Aislings will need to 
discover who committed the crime, where did it take place, and using what 
type of weapon. Loures says all of these facts are required for a complete 
investigation and closer of such. 
The aisling that succeeds in finding out these three things shall have the 
event of solving the murder recorded onto their legend and enjoy the personal thanks of Loures. 
Lord Ramanayan Caesar and his entourage will coordinate the aisling search 
this coming forth moon.
((Murder Mystery of Loures will begin on Friday January 24th at 6:00 PM PST in Loures Castle, throne room. Participants will be required to attempt to discover who killed the consular, where it took place, and using what weapon. 

Clues will be given out, and then the aislings must seek out different 
aislings and mundanes for more information. Once an aisling thinks they have all three answers they may whisper Ramanayan. If they are not correct they will be told that, but not told what answers are incorrect. They may try again. 
The first aisling to answer correctly all three will be the solver and winner of the Murder Mystery. Their name will be submitted to the mundanes and they will be given the unique legend mark "Solved Consular Marcus Murder - Deoch 33, Summer". 
Other information will be given out when the event begins. Please be on time.))

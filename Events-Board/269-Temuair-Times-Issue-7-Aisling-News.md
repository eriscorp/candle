Oren Invitational Ball

At all levels, King Oren's ball was a great success. One hundred invitations were given out, which meant that around two hundred people were crowded into the throne room of Oren Island. Before the ball, the skilled tailors of Oren fit Aislings for new styles of formal clothing.
Among the honored guests, there were the Mundane Burgesses of both Mileth and Rucesion, and also Oren's mundane representative 'Law' made his appearance to greet the attending Aislings. After a huge smorgasbord of taste tempting delights were served along side the best wine Temuair has to offer, a joke contest was held to celebrate the laughter that we all need so much in these dark times.
Finally, dancing filled the night air as an orchestra played it's music for everyone to enjoy. As a small tidbit, an announcement, to the gathering, by an Aisling priest told of Landon's engagement to Flyss, congratulations to the both of them.
Two more balls are planned for the future, we hope to see you all there.

Excommunication Adjustment

As many have already noticed, the gods of Temuair have made a judgment regarding priests' ability to excommunicate members of their religion. As it stands right now, only the higher clergy (Clerics, Ministers, and High Priests) are endowed with the ability to cast out unruly members. This drastic change is attributed to the many priests that have been excommunicating innocent and faithful people

Wedding Clothing

It had been known that the mundane tailor/merchant of Piet, Jilt, was working on something special for all Aislings to clothe themselves in. Finally, Jilt announced his masterpiece to the world. New wedding clothing!
As first seen at the union of Landon and Flyss, these pieces of clothing are made of only the finest garments and silks... which becomes rather obvious when anyone looks at the price tag of five million coins each.
Jilt comments that he created the new clothing for those Aislings that are looking to 'put their money where their mouth is.'
If you wish to purchase the clothing, please go see the mundane merchant, Jilt, in Piet.

More for the Lovers

Construction workers from all over Temuair came together to finish one long-standing project and to quickly partake and complete another. Chapels in both Tagor and Rucesion have been opened to Aislings that are looking for a different place to announce their eternal bondings. 
Both the Tagor and the Rucesion buildings are unique and have their own attributes of beauty, added to the currently standing chapel of Mileth, there are sure to be more than enough places for weddings at any given time.

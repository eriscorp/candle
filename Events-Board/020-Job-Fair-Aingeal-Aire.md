# Job Fair - Aingeal Aire

_Posted by Mundane Gossip — 3rd Moon, 13th Sun · Post #20_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
The members of the motley Aingeal Aire proudly present: 

A Job Fair - Opening on ((Saturday, February 26, 2000 from 8PM PST to 11PM PST)) 

This Job Fair presents the first insider look at the paths of Temuair. Do you have the beef to be a warrior, how about the smarts to be a priest? What about fashion? Skills? Come visit the fair and find a plethora of information for the taking. There is no admission fee, and all Aislings of all ages are welcome. There are five major events going on, so feel free to stop in at any or all of them! 

Event 1: Senior Patrons - Five 'elders' from each class will talk about what it was like growing up as 'class name' to an open forum. See Imajica or Trinity if you'd like to share or need more information. Senior Patrons is open from 8-9PM PST in the Mileth College lore classroom. 

Event 2: Clothing Wares - On site display and bartering of clothing from each of the five paths. See Diamanda or Dinea if you'd like to donate or need more information. Clothing Wares is open from 9-10PM PST in the Mileth College art classroom. 

Event 3: Sumo Tourney - Always a popular game in Temuair, to give those a break from learning to get in a bit of exercise. See Allita or Pasha for rules, entry forms or more information. Sumo Tourney is open from 8-11PM PST in the Mileth College plaza. 

Event 4: Skills &amp; Spells - With demonstration areas to show off all important skills and spells and what they do. See Sarc to display your skills or for more information. Skills &amp; Spells is open from 9-10PM PST in the Eastern Woodlands Wastelands. 

Event 5: Job Shadowing - Open forum where aislings can talk about the mannerisms of each class with each other. Also featured is a job shadow, where one can follow another path around. See Rhianna to speak at the forum or for more information. Job Shadows is open from 10-11PM PST at the Mileth College Library. 

Information Booth: Located in the Mileth College plaza, will be open all day to provide information to those who need finding a location of an event, schedules, and entry forms. Information Booth is open from 8-11PM PST at the Mileth College plaza. 

We hope to see you all present. Live in the light! 

Regards, 

Paladine Utan Meatha
Aingeal Aire, Founder and Council

Trick or Treat 
**************
As the veil separating the living and dead continues to thin, the malevolent spirits must not be allowed to roam freely among the living. To deceive these restless souls, Aislings are decorating abandoned studies of House Macabre to mimic the realm of the dead so that the dead pass us by. But this alone will not suffice--your aid is needed. 
To keep the malevolent spirits at bay, we invite Aislings to visit the studies in the third and fourth yards of Macabre disguised as spirits--whether spectral, dreadful, or fantastical. So don your best costumes and greet each host with "trick or treat" for a surprise! Many hosts will also offer games and activities bring joy amidst the gloom. 

This event will occur at the same time as Monster Mash. Aislings are encouraged to participate in both events to maximize their assistance.
Location: Road to House Macabre (House Macabre entrance)
Date: (( October 11 at 5pm PST / 7pm CST / 8pm EST ))

Costume Contest
***************
As the veil continues to thin and the lost souls wander, it will take more than warding the studies with decorations--we must become the illusion. We call upon all Aislings to take part in a costume contest where disguise is both art and protection. The Suomi Troupe plans to reward the most convincing disguise that may fool the spirits entirely!
Location: Garamonde Theatre in Suomi 
Date: (( October 12 at 5pm PST / 7pm CST / 8pm EST ))

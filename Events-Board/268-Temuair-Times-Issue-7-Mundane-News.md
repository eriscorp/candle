Mundane Plague

Reports of the rare 'Mundane Plague' have come up around the areas on the Isle of Dawn. This odd infection, which has nearly no affect on Aislings, is deadly to Mundanes, if they are not properly treated. Mundane explorers and scholars that have recently come to the Isle seem to be the most susceptible. Doctors suggest that residents of Rucesion have grown an immunity from minimal exposure to the plague.
Warning signs on the plague consist of pale skin, shorted breath, forming skin boils, and intense muscle weakness.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Unsafe Shipping?

Out at sea, a startling trend has started to occur. Shipping lines between Rucesion and Loures have been shocked with the disappearance of more than six cargo vessels. There has been no explanation as to the mystery behind the disappearances, most likely due to the total lack of survivors from the missing ships. 
Also, just as mysterious, no debris from any of the ships has floated ashore. An experienced mundane captain commented it, "If ye are askin me, I say that them there pirates are behind it all. Those scurvy dogs..."

Scholars, however, point to something else entirely. "Ye see... the currents in these waters have changed dramatically starting this Deoch. We suggest that these missing ships have mistakenly followed the wrong current and found themselves sailing toward Kadath."

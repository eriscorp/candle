Catch of a Lifetime
A Mundane fisherman pulled a very unusual treasure from the waters off of Abel's port this week. Jaus, a forty-year-old sailor, threw out his pole and string while his ship was restocking.
"I couldn't believe it!" Exclaimed Jaus when he recalled the event "I thought that I had landed a whopper!"
It turned out that at the end of the string hung an old, rusted statue. Jaus, after finishing his fishing, took the statue to the bank in Abel. "I knew that it was something special the second that I laid eyes on it," recalled Lamont, the Abel banker.
The statue was later announced to be an artifact, dating back to the tenth Aeon. To be more precise, it was a handcrafted metal figure made to represent King Ainmeal of Loures. After little consideration, Jaus choose to keep his catch.

Summertime Fun

Summer has reached Temuair, which means that Mundanes and Aislings alike have been hitting the beaches! Every summer, around this time, attendance at Abel beach increases substantially.
One Mundane explains, "This is the best place in Temuair during the summer days. The cool sea air comes off of the ocean, and everyone is having a great time! It's a great way to keep your mind off of the heat."
Everyone is suggested to go enjoy the beautiful summer weather while it lasts.

Sounds of the Night

The Castle of Dubhaim has always been a place of great mystery and unusual sounds, yet it has been a place frequented by a few brave souls. However, this place has been drawing more attention by priests, scholars, and adventurers in recent moons.
What has drawn the attention of these high members of society? Howls. Ghastly howls that started to echo all throughout the Isle of Dawn, keeping Rucesion citizens awake at night.
Many blame the Dubhaimid for the night shattering sounds. "It could be some type of dark ritual," speculated one scholar. Even as more researchers flock to the old castle, so far, nothing is for sure. 
However, one warrior summed it up in a few simple words... "Whatever it is... it's nothing this blade can't stop."

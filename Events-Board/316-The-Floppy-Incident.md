# The Floppy Incident

_Posted by Eduardo — 5th Moon, 12th Sun · Post #316_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">
Areini, Proprietor of Tagor Pet Store, writes,

"I am afraid I have made a terrible error.  One that may affect all the lands."
     "You see, captive animals are very reluctant to breed.  This is part of why I cannot furnish more than one pet per Aislng.  Floppies proved very popular in the store, but nearly impossible to mate.  This seemed odd to me, for rumors say that floppies breed incessantly in the wilds."
    "I thought, perhaps, if I furnished a flower from the wilds, perhaps that might stimulate the cuddly bundles to love."
    "Alas, the simple purple flower proved too potent a medicine.  Within hours my shoppe was overrun with floppies!  I fear I have let loose a plague upon the lands.  For this flower blooms commonly in the wild lands, and floppies' hunger cannot be sated.  Their numbers will be unimaginable.  I can only offer my deepest of apologies, and pray the bright spark of the Aislings might overcome this dilemma."

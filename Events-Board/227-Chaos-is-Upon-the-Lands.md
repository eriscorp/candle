Chadul has lost the Light and Darkness contest but he is far from a gracious loser. He has, with the help of his disciples, created a rift within the realm of Temuair. 
Soon, as you awaken in Temuair, if your Spark feels cold, then the land is one of violence. That Aisling may attack Aisling wherever they please. The only sanctuary are the buildings in town. Not even the town squares are safe from bloodshed. 
However, the beggars, being thankful for all that has been given to them in the past, now show their appreciation and they will bring back to life anyone who dies during this chaotic time.

Deice Interview

Kaeltegoeth: Now then. Lord Deice, what are the duties of Burgessery?
Deice: Well, in no particular order ...
       Manage the Demagougery.
       Review and create/revise/remove laws as necessary, just like
       a Demagouge but with a little more finesse. :)
       Chair and run the Demagougery meetings, in which both
       officials and citizens can express their views on laws in a
       non-disruptive/combative way.
       Exile severe lawbreakers.
       Bar corrupt officials from political office.
       And a great many minor obligations that are too numerous to
       list here. :)
Kaeltegoeth: I see. *grins*
             So, did you have to take any part in the recent
             Threads/Leogar controversy, as a Burgess?
Deice: As a Burgess, I only moderate the discussion of it that is
       carried on upon the Political Discourse board outside, making
       sure it remains within the lawful obligations of use of that
       board.
       As a politician with an opinion though ...
       My personal feelings are that Threads is not capable of seeing
       himself as someone that can do wrong ... therefore, his
       recent punishment is something he contests based on that
       principle.
       You may have that on or off the record, whichever you
       like. 0:)
Kaeltegoeth: It's good to see you maintain a perspective on
             the issue, sir.
             As a Burgess, I'd imagine you don't have much spare time -
Deice: *nod*
Kaeltegoeth: Do you ever have a chance to go to any masses or social
             events of the sort?
Deice: Actually, I do manage to find the time, thanks to keeping
       my schedule fairly well-organized but at the same time, not
       locking myself down to things for every moment of every sun.:)
       Balancing it all is tricky, but it is possible to have a
       political career and do the other things you want to. :)
Kaeltegoeth: Now, if you don't mind my asking, what sorts of things
             -do- you do in your spare time?
Deice: Well, I have my marriage to my wife which I put a lot of my
       spare time into enjoying, along with other pursuits such as
       hunting, attending classes, attending mass and the like. :)
       I'm a fairly normal Aisling outside of my political office. :)
Kaeltegoeth: Well, it certainly sounds that way ^_^
             Is there anything you'd like to add, lord Deice?
Deice: Mmm ... perhaps the only thing I would like to say on my own
       is that regardless of some of the accomplishments I have under
       my belt, such as being a Burgess or a Kingdom Lore Keeper or
       what-not, at heart, I am no different from any other Aisling
       in Temuair that aims to make the world a better place than it
       is. :)
Kaeltegoeth: Inspiring words, m'lord. *grins*

Dungeon of the double-moon: Astrid
by: Ramanayan Caesar

Astrid is the home of the Goblins and the site of several previous events. In it are roads carved thousands of years ago and a mysterious pentagram in the center. 
Those that dare travel into Astrid should be advised that this is one of the trickiest dungeons in Temuair. The Kobolds will lay minor traps as well as blind and poison traps. Most dangerous are probably the Shaman who will cast ard spells in their defense in the deeper portions. Even in the early sections the Kobolds will cast beag level spells. Most frightening of all is that the creatures that live in Astrid can and will summon dubhaim monsters to attack intruders. These range from Fiend Pupa, to Ghasts, and Gargoyles. Aislings are not advised to enter Astrid unless they are well prepared to meet the full force of the Goblin tribes. 

It is also rumored that those who understand the secrets of Goblin society can make use of some of the powers hidden in Astrid. Kalliope in Undine tavern can speak to you about learning these secrets. 

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Featured Monster of Astrid- Goblin Shamans
by: Ramanayan Caesar
Goblin Shaman: The Goblin Shaman is truly the most powerful and evil monster of Astrid. The Shaman will start casting normal then to ard level spells often more then one at once! In the center-deep portions of Astrid they can be seen calling forth Dubhaim monsters often yelling out "aghast, aghast!" However they are not strong in direct combat and will often retreat if you get to close to them. So if faced with fighting one just charging madly at it just might work. Shamans sometimes drop some magical items including the coveted "Fine Light Dagger."

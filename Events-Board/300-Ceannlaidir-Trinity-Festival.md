# Ceannlaidir Trinity Festival

_Posted by Eduardo — 4th Moon, 13th Sun · Post #300_

<pre style="background:#12100c; color:#e8e0cc; padding:1em; border-radius:6px; white-space:pre-wrap; font-family:monospace;">

The Ceannlaidir temple is hosting a festival to honour the three Gods of the Trinity: Fiosachd, Ceannlaidir and Sgrios. There will be games with prizes, and Arena battles to honour these Gods. 
The Festival will be held in Castle Dubhaim Courtyard. Aislings of all faiths are invited to attend, but must abide by the laws of Rucesion while they are there. 
Fiosachd's Treasure Hunt: Be the first to collect the most items of a list of 25 bizarre objects in Temuair, and win a surprise. It could be jewelry, useful armour, a brightly coloured puppet, or a Mystery Grand Prize. (( The rules will be posted on the Dubhaim adventure board. Contest runs from 5 pm PST - 7 pm PST. Whoever wishes to participate must contact Ellora during this time period. )) 
The Hemloch Shells of Sgrios: Enter at your own risk! Three bottles of rum will be placed on the ground. One of them has Hemloch Deum underneath it. Choose this bottle of rum, and you must drink the hemloch first and then the rum, and die. Whoever dies the most wins a prize. (( The rules will be posted on the Dubhaim adventure board. Contest begins at 5 pm PST and ends when we run out of hemloch! Enter at your own risk. No one will be forced to kill their character, but if you want a prize, you might want to deposit your valuables in the bank! )) 
Ceannlaidir's Ascension Killoff: One very strong aisling will put on an ascension mask and run around the grounds of Dubhaim. Will you be the one who lands the final blow? If you do, you will win a prize. (( The rules will be posted on the Dubhaim adventure board. This game will begin at 5 pm PST and last several rounds until the Ascender becomes tired. )) 

((cont.))

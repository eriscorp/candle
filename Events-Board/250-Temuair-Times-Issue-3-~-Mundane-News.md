Discovery By Sea
A mundane navigator, Kessi, has announced her intention to seek out new lands by way of sea travel. Setting sail from the port town of Rucesion, Kessi and her crew will set their sights north in search of new and exciting lands. 
"For as long as history tells us," she speaks, "there has been only one known large land mass that exists... and I plan to rewrite that part of history."
Several mundane scholars have denounced Kessi's journey as a 'foolish endeavor' Also claiming that she will undoubtedly come within the influence of Kadath before long.  "Her and her crew will undoubtedly be driven to madness, if they have not already been done so," quipped one scholar.

Tagorian Chapel Construction

The chapel in Tagor has remained a disaster area since the discovery of the town some eight Deoch ago. There are boards blocking the doorway,  windows of broken glass, and gaping holes in the outside wood. However, recently a mundane group of carpenters, woodworkers, and designers have started repairs on the old ailing building. 
The Mileth Construction Group, same company that built the Rucesion Inn, has taken control of the 'Tagorian Chapel Reconstruction Plan'. A spokesman has attested that the damage to the chapel is extensive and it may take up to two Deoch before they complete their renovations. 
"I know that Aislings will appreciate finally being able to enter the chapel... I hope that our work will be as welcomed as the Rucesion inn project was," said the spokesman.

Piet Sewer Cleaners Needed

"I just don't know how much more of this sewage backup we can keep out of main floor Loures Castle," panicked, the former sewer cleaner turned jester, Jay.
For those unaware, the Piet sewers has been needing a good cleaning ever since the hostile creatures took up a home there. "Occasionally, an Aisling or two will take up the job, but it's not enough!" Jay said, "we need more people that are willing to get down and dirty so that Loures castle remains above the rising stench."
If you are willing to become a clog cleaner for Loures Castle, please talk to Jay the jester, in the Loures court.

Fas Nadur Changes

Mileth College - As many have noted, Fas Nadur has reversed effects. Before
this change, Fas Nadur amplified the defensive element of creatures. Now Fas
Nadur amplifies offensive elements. 
To compensate for this change, a wizard must cast the spell on the opponents
to defend his party members and cast fas on his party members to increase
the effectiveness of thier attacks.
Mundane scholars gathered in Mileth College to discuss this mysterious
change in the nature of magic. They have come to two opposing conclusions.
Cian, alchemist of Mileth, proposes that the gods have made some cataclysmic
error and that the very fabric of reality has been altered. This idea seems
very popular among aislings. 
Many were offended by this idea, including Devlin, the Fae Priestess of
Mileth. "The old coot is always blaming the gods for his troubles. He sounds
like an aisling!" She said.
The other majorly accepted opinion was best stated by Dar, wizard of mileth.
"Aisling will," he said, "has the power to mold reality. Of this there is no
doubt. The nature of aislings in general has been steadily shifting to a
more offensive stance, hence their abilities are becoming more offensive to
match this outlook."
Both groups have agreed that Fas Nadur will likely return to it's original
function sometime in the near future. In Dar's words, "Be watchful of the
effects of your spells and adjust your strategies according to any changes
you observe."

# Wizard's Elemental Secret

Kiril announces he will begin teaching Beag Fas Nadur and Mor Fas Nadur Cail-week, Cail-day's morning ((04/18, Noon PDT)). He has made progress in translations and in better understanding the base Fas Nadur's power. 

Dar, Kiril, and Hadrian confer over an ancient elemental secret. They decyphered an ancient text on the art of increasing the concentration of a natural element in a creature. 

By translated fragments, the shadowy philosophers believe that this secret allows each element's effect to intensify, increasing vulnerability and also increasing invulnerability. 

The mundane wizards act cautiously, knowing how dangerous it would be if goblin shamans learned this secret.

Trouble in Pravat 

These days, Grimlocks in Pravat Caves have had frequent strange behavior. Some aislings allied with them reported to us that some have attacked them without any reason, sneaking around them and backstabbing when there was an opportunity to do it. 

We tried to find out what the reason of this, and according to local informants, it seems that there is a black market for the Grimlock's weapons. When we questioned the Grimlock Queen, Phailan, we were told "Grimlock give up weapon only when die. Grimlock survive!" So, as it seems, the Grimlock are angered with all the assaults on their civilization by other Aislings, who want nothing more than money. It is rumored that curved daggers sell for near 1 and a half million coins! 

Special correspondent, Aeda Vales 


The Perfect Fit: A New Matchmaking Column (Part 1)

The Perfect Fit is a Matchmaking endeavor open to all aislings of all ages, if interested in this service contact Iris. 

Tips for writing a sucessful personal ad:
A good personal ad is honest and gives those who read it a sense of your true personality. So be candid. 

Include a physical description of yourself that's accurate but not too detailed. 

Be sure to include a profile of the type of person you're seeking--physically, intellectually and spiritually. If you're only interested in Deochian men, say so. If you have an age range in mind, mention it. 

When you finish writing your ad, run it by a friend. Ask them if you have represented yourself accurately. Take their advice into account, do a revision if necessary, then submit the ad. 

Show up with realistic expectations and your intuition fine-tuned. Be prepared to skip dessert and move on to candidate number two. It may take several tries before a potential soul mate turns up.

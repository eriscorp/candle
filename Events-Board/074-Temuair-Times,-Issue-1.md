Mundane Sleeps 2 Double-Moons Straight 

Piesin, Mundane son of Cedric, the Mileth Mundane Burgess, made a record last moon for sleeping longer than any other Mundane or Aisling in Temuairan history. Piesin slept for 2 Double-moons straight, with an occasional wake for a piece of bread and glass of water. 

When questioned as to how he made himself so tired, Piesin simply laughed and said, "Before I began sleeping, I caused a shortage of wine in Abel." The Mundane is proud of his record, as is his father who stated "That's my son for ye!"

Suomi Theater
written by: Muirgael Sonneveldt
A new building has been revealed in the faraway land of Suomi.  Thulin is proud to present his brand-new Theatre.  In cooperation with Eduardo,he gratefully offers the building to the leaders (directors) of the Temuair Theatre Group. Lorre,Hotaru and Shiroto swore an oath to maintain the building and amuse the people of Temuair with various plays.  

The opening play by Shiroto, "Aisling McDanger and the Necklace of Light was said to be "Magnificent" and  "a splendorous example of good writing and acting".  Though the future of the theatre is a bit cloudy, Lorre has foreseen a massive total of 3 plays in the very near future. Until then, we hope to know more of the coming productions.

Bad Dreams
Nightmares seem to be a normal way of life for the Mundanes of Temuair these days.  However, there is a peculiar boy at the Abel inn who tells of his unusual dream. 
On one side stands Glioca, Cail, Ceannlaidir, and Luathas... on the other stands Gramail, Deoch, Fiosachd, and Sgrios.  They are all wearing some kind of cap and they seem to be arguing with one another.  They turn to the boy and seem to speak with him, yet he claims that he could not understand what they were saying.
What does it all mean?  Aislings of the second and a half circle and up are encouraged to help the boy decipher his mysterious dream.

Mundane Disputes
Many rumors are starting to spread concerning the Mundane burgesses of Mileth and Rucesion as well as the representative from Oren island, Law.  Earlier this Deoch they were seen together at the Oren Tavern discussing various affairs of concern.  Witnesses attest that Cedric, Mundane burgess of Mileth, stormed from the building and departed from the island angrily.  Some attest that Eduardo did the same a few hours later.  
It is a little known fact that Law, representative of Oren, views Aisling-kind as a nuance that needs to be oppressed.  His philosophy seems to have been adopted from the Loures court council.  Aisling-kind, be weary when traveling to and from Oren.

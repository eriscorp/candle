Places To Go, Rituals To Do
written by: Xenek D'Suomi

Aislings from all over Temuair had the chance to perform the sacred Adoration of Danaan not too long ago.  They were seen traveling from places near and far to perform this ritual with other Aislings.  The Adoration is the third part of a series of three rituals:  The Eulogy, The Redemption, and The Adoration.  In order to perform these, an Aisling must travel to one of three locations for the Eulogy and Redemption:  Astrid, the Mehadi Swamp, or the Pravat Caves.  During the Adoration, an Aisling must meet up with two Aislings from the two other locations to complete it correctly.
But to which one do you go?  Does it matter?  I asked Aislings all around Temuair to find out where they went in their most recent of the rituals, and why.
Of several individuals polled...
-20% represented Astrid
-45% represented Mehadi
-35% represented Pravat
The reasons for these choices varied greatly as I asked more and more Aislings.  They ranged simply from things such as location, to more in depth reasons that go back to an Aisling's family or history.  Many made their decision this time based on their experiences from the last.
"...I've been around for a while, and I know from the past times that the Astrid role was more difficult to find," said Stilgar, Master.
However it seems that the number of Aislings in each place varies through each time, but usually there seems to be a pattern, I'm told.  Many Aislings, however, chose simply based on location.  Some found it easier to go to a place like Pravat, which is closer to home, rather than off to Astrid or Mehadi, which requires some walking and a bit more danger.  It also depends on what they favor.  One Aisling told me she chose Mehadi because she enjoyed the garden flowers and thought the Mukul were wonderful creatures.  Some Aislings, such as Kaeldra, were trying to complete the Adoration for each.
"I'd already worshipped at Mehadi and Astrid in the past, so Pravat was the last to complete the trilogy."  Kaeldra, Insight 99
Maybe next time the Eulogy comes around, it might be favorable to do some research and make a good decision before you get caught up in it all over again.  Either way, whatever you choose does not really affect your outcome in the end.  It just may be harder to find certain roles.

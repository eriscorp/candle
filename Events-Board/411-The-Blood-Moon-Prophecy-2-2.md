Complex Concoction Conundrum (( Potion Brewing Contest ))

Oh no! While gathering rare and exotic ingredients for a potion to counteract the effects of the Red Moon, Witch Ulli was beguiled by its influence! While transformed, her warped body and decayed mind prevent her from researching the right mixture. She also failed to gather one final ingredient, but she cannot remember what it is! Can you come to her aid and deduce the correct concoction?

The panel of judges will rate contestants based on the creative use of ingredients and their improv abilities. ))

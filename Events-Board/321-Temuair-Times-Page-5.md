The Approximate Science of "Loures Aite"       

            Alright, I will start off my column with an interesting topic: "Loures Aite."  As all of you know, Loures Aite is the special sanctuary spell cast in the Loures dungeon by two people simultaneously.  One of the two people is Marlin himself who casts io dia naomh aite ionad on you, this being the most powerful form you can receive as of yet.  The second spell is cast by the Aisling who assisted him in finding the whereabouts of Bella. This spell hits all in your party with a medium strength "naohm aite" spell.  The third spell is also cast by the Aisling. This spell strikes everyone in the area with a pathetically weak naomh aite, often inciting rage from those around you.     
          We are here to discover the meaning behind that rage and its practical uses.  I have a theory about what incites this peculiar behavior in people; I will present it to you shortly.  I have stumbled upon many an Aisling standing there in the dungeon asking me to group them before I cast.  I do not group them or even warn them I am casting, seeing as how they know that I am casting aite because of my presence in the chamber. Any aisling can assist Marlin and cast their own naomh aite spell.  Some would call this laziness; I call it co-dependency. Co-dependant people are people who insist on being dependant on others for day-to-day needs even when it is not necessary. Loures Aite is DEFINITELY a 'day to day' need. I now quote a conversation I had not too long ago with an Aisling:  Aisling: grp me befor u cast please Me: *casts* Aisling: dam noobs ~whispers start~ Me: hmm noob? well i'm not the one in there begging for aite :) Aisling: whoaaaaaaa anit begging, asking noob Me: *shrugs* begging and asking are synonomous to me. Aisling: becuz ur a noob :S Me: whoa astounding come back! you have dazzled me with your wit!      
         This is all planned. King Bruce relies on Co-Dependancy as a fuel source.  The anger from aislings helps to heat King Bruce's castle and the excess is sold to Rucesion to fuel their lamps.     
         So next time you see someone standing in the dungeon, cast on them; rest assured, you are helping to fuel the economy.

Uprising in Undine
Undine citizens once again clashed with Loures guards earlier this moon. Several were arrested, yet there were no casualties during the violence.
The incident is the latest in a continuing trend of defiance by the oppressed people of Cail's enchanted ground. The 'Liberation Organization of Undine' claimed responsibility for the uprising. The hostilities began with simple taunting and escalated to the use of steel. The rioters, outnumbered, quickly threw down their weapons and sat upon the ground, in a silent protest, as they were dragged from the scene.
The 'LOU' was founded after suspicions arose about the nature of the Loures occupation in regard to the Goblin raids many Deochs ago. They believe that the court of Loures is using mercenary goblins to justify their presence in Undine. King Bruce has denied the allegations.

Goblins Amass Near Loures
Many other Mundanes have reported sightings of the Goblin army that was first discovered by Mundane Decasco. Aislings seem be nervous with anticipation while they sharpen swords and train to meet the imminent threat. Jean, counselor of Loures, has yet to comment on the action , if any, the Loures army will take on the increasing threat. The only words that have come from the counselor's mouth on the matter are "We continue to assess the situation."

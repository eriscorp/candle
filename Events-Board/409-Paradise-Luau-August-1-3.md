Paradise Cooking Contest
************************

In this competition, chefs must prepare their finest dishes only using ingredients found in Temuair and Medenia. Points will be awarded for creative use of ingredients and stage performance.

There are three rounds. First, submit your entry dish based on the application instructions on the Events of Temuair board (( click 'w' )). If you are selected, you will move onto the competition round at Garamonde Theatre in Suomi, where competitors must work with the ingredients on stage. The top 3 competitors on stage will proceed to the final round. 

Please consult the application instructions for how to submit your entry dish.

Paradise Beach Bash
*******************

Don't miss out on our main event: the Paradise Beach Bash! Come down to the shores of Lynith Beach for a sun of games and beverages, with plenty of chances to win prizes. The Beach Bash will feature the three competitions below.

Beach Fashion Contest: in this contest, our judges will be prowling the 
beach to search for the hottest (and coolest) in beach bash fashion! Anyone on the beach will be open to having their outfits judged by our panel of judges, and the top three winners will be announced at the end of the beach party. Be sure to keep your outfits on for the entire party!

Rock Paper Scissors: step into the ring for rock paper scissors--Paradise Luau edition! Defeat challenger after challenger in a king of the hill format: win, and you stay; lose, and you're out! Who will rule the reef? There will be two stations available. 

Summer Trivia: Aislings will be tested on their knowledge of summer lore. More details about the format and rules will be provided at the event. 

Paradise Combat Tournament
**************************

Join us in our Paradise 2v2 Combat Tournament! Aislings may form any class combination in pairs. Rules are as follows: no star arrow, hail of feathers, fas, gar spells, prayer spells, trap spam, group heals, or shields. Please contact Moogle, Palantir, Set, or KimmieFace to sign up in advance.

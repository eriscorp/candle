Mundanes and Aislings Enjoy the Tulsi Brew 

A moon and a half ago, Nuadh Toiseach hosted the grand reopening of the Tulsi Brew in Piet. There were many Aislings and Mundanes alike in attendance, and all had fun. They drank wine and brandy, listened to stories and some poetry and talked some. 

Perhaps the most memorable part of the night was Shanira's fictional stories of herself and her friend Celestria. All in attendance enjoyed them both, and I believe I speak for all who were there when I say we hope she is there again next double-moon. 

Remember, all Aislings and Mundanes are welcome to attend. Just come on in, the drinks are on Nuadh Toiseach! 

((The Tulsi Brew is held at Piet Tavern, every Sunday at 8pm Eastern, 5pm Pacific.)) 


Repeated Cleanings of the Deoch Board 

For a fourth time, the second time this Deoch, the Deoch Fellowship board has been wiped clean of all posts. The first deletion of the posts was in mid Deoch 12, and then the end of Deoch 12. The third and the fourth deletions were done in the past 2 double-moons. Deoch priestess Layla Juniper is calling together a meeting between Deoch priests ((for this Saturday at 1pm eastern)) to discuss this problem. When asked who she feels keeps doing this, or even if it is done by the same Aisling, Layla states "I do not want to point the finger until I know for sure who has done it. When I find out who, I will confer with other Deoch priests on to how to handle the Aisling." 

I also questioned well known and very much respected Deoch priest Paladine, for his feelings about this matter. He had not much to say, except a sighed "Why ruin Deoch's light?" Priestess Morrigan also had few words. When asked if she felt Deoch would ever forgive the guilty Aisling, she responded "Deoch is not as forgiving as Glioca." 

To whom ever is deleting the posts from the board, how can you call yourself a priest of Deoch when you repeatedly desecrate the words of his followers?

# Board Usage Guidelines

The boards of Rucesion are available for use by all Aislings, with the understanding that posting is a privilege, not a legal right.

## 1) Guidelines

The following guidelines apply to all boards found in Rucesion, and her territories. The official(s) administering a board may remove any post that is deemed abusive. This includes any post is verbally abusive or inflammatory; any written work which constitutes a Malicious Act; Heresy (refer to Standards of Practice); or Slate pollution ((spamming)) by those with an over active quill; Personal issues; Libel (unproven statements which could harm an Aisling's reputation).

## 2) Monitoring and Removal

All officials are mandated to regularly monitor the boards under their jurisdiction for possible abuse. Final judgement and interpretation on the content of any post in question will be left to the proper monitor(s) of each board. Abuse of posting privileges is regulated by the Malicious Acts Law. Officials removing posts are to keep memories of illegal posts removed for at least one Deoch. Failure to do so will be considered an infraction as per the Integrity Assurance Act.

## 3) Boards

As a temporary measure in times of need, a Judge or Burgess may reallocate the usage and/or purpose of a board by giving notice therof on appropriate boards.

### 3.1) Merchant's Board (located inside bank) [Regulated by Legislators]

Reserved for content relating to the local economy, buying, selling, and trading. ((Advertising the trade of registration codes, kruna codes, or characters is not permitted))

### 3.2) Community Board (located near the chapel) [Regulated by Legislators]

General discussion, guild recruitment, and civilized debate may occur here. Public Service announcements may be posted by Rucesion Officials, Knights, and Rangers. Posts which are more appropriate for other boards in Rucesion will be moved at a monitor's discretion.

### 3.3) Justice Cases Board (left in court) [Regulated by Captains, Judges]

Warnings, banishments, warrants, unbanishments, actions taken lists, evidence, meeting notices, and other related guard issues are to be placed here.

### 3.4) Adventure Board (in Dubhaim Castle) [Regulated by Legislators]

Reserved for posts regarding adventures, hunting, etc.

### 3.5) Demagogues Board (in town hall) [Regulated by Burgesses]

Proposals for legal amendments, applications list, details of inappropriate name investigations, voting thereon, and other related Legislature issues are posted here.

### 3.6) Judgments Board (court, right side) [Regulated by Judges]

Reserved for posts regarding judicial actions such as trials, exiles and related matters, and applications in terms of the Commonwealth Preservation Act.

### 3.7) Political Discourse Board (in civics area) [Regulated by Burgesses]

Reserved for posts regarding official and political events, opinions, and other such uses which specifically pertain to Rucesion Politics.

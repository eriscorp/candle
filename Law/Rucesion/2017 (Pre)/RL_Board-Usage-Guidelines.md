# Board Usage Guidelines

The boards of Rucesion are available for use by all Aislings, with the understanding that posting is a privilege, not a legal right.

## 1) Guidelines

The following guidelines apply to all boards found in Rucesion, and her territories. The officials administrating a board may remove any post that is deemed abusive. This includes any post that is verbally abusive or inflammatory; any written work which constitutes a Malicious Act; Slate pollution; Personal Issues; or Libel.

## 2) Monitoring and Removal

All officials are mandated to regularly monitor the boards under their jurisdiction for possible abuse. Final judgement and interpretation on the content of any post in question will be left to the proper monitor(s) of each board. Abuse of posting privileges is regulated by the Malicious Acts Law. Memories of illegal posts removed by Officials removing posts are to be kept for one Deoch. Failure to do so will be considered an infraction as per the Integrity Assurance Act.

## 3) Boards

As a temporary measure in times of need, a Judge or Burgess may reallocate the usage or purpose of a board by giving notice on appropriate boards.

### 3.1) Merchant's Board (located inside bank) [Regulated by Legislators]

Reserved for content relating to the local economy, buying, selling, and trading. Prohibited trade is not allowed.

### 3.2) Community Board (located near the chapel) [Regulated by Legislators]

General discussion, guild recruitment, and civilised debate may occur here. Public Service announcements may be posted by Rucesion Officials, Knights and Rangers. Posts which are more appropriate for other boards in Rucesion will be moved, or removed, at a monitor's discretion.

### 3.3) Adventure Board (in Dubhaim Castle) [Regulated by Legislators]

Reserved for posts regarding adventures, hunting, etc. Any posts related to the Sgrios Temple are to be removed, and the Aisling notified of the purpose of the Adventure Board, then redirected to the Sgrios Temple board.

### 3.4) Justice Cases Board (left in court) [Regulated by Captains, Judges]

Warnings, banishments, warrants, unbanishments, actions taken lists, evidence, meeting notices, and other related guard issues are to be placed here. Notice of removal, sponsor dismissal or demotion of an official must be posted, including the reason, assistant(s) and copies of reports of action taken against the citizen.

### 3.5) Judgments Board (court, right side) [Regulated by Judges]

Judicial actions such as exiles, pardons, prohibited trade crimes, and sgath warrants are placed here.

### 3.6) Political Discourse Board (in civics area) [Regulated by Burgesses]

Reserved for posts regarding official and political events, opinions, and other such uses which specifically pertain to Rucesion Politics, new citizen admissions and notification of extended leave for personal issues during office.

### 3.7) Demagoguery Board (Hall) [Regulated by Burgesses]

Reserved for law proposals, voting, meetings and minutes, and maintaining a list of ineligible citizens.

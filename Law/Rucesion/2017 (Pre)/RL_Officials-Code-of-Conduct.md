# Official's Code of Conduct

The Commonwealth of Rucesion hereby empowers its government to protect and serve the Commonwealth, through the powers and regulations granted through this ordinance. Contravention of this Act by any official will result in action under the Integrity Assurance Act.

## 1) Requirements for Capturing Evidence

All evidence submitted to the Judicial Administrators and/or Legislators must contain the following:

- A) Identity of the Aisling's City Shield
- B) Aisling's Legend for criminal marks.[for Eligibility requirements]
- C) Name of Aisling above his head ((mouse-over effect)) [for other crimes]
- D) When having a conversation, ensure that the text is visible at the top of the screen or in the chat box.

Protocols for collecting evidence regarding specific crimes should be found on the Justice Cases board.

### 1.1) Evidence Forwarding

All administrative officials are to include their pigeon addresses ((email)) on the Mandatory Evidence Forwarding list on the Justice Cases board. Failure to do so within a moon of taking office must be dealt with as a failure to notify. All evidence pertaining to misdemeanors must be forwarded to all Guard Captains and Judges. All evidence pertaining to felonies and capital crimes (or any other issue which may result in an exile warrant, Sgath and/or barment) must be forwarded to all Guard Captains, Judges and Burgesses (or the Acting Burgess).

### 1.2) Notification

Rucesion Officials are required to make a report of the use of any official action. (See Board Usage Guidelines)

## 2) Protocol for the Judicial Branch

This Section relates to the responsibilities of Guards, Captains and Judges. Reporting protocols are governed by Section 1 of this act.

### 2.1) Formal Warning

A formal warning should be posted to the Justice Cases Board and a copy thereof sent to the offender.

### 2.2) Protocol for Banishments

Any act of Banishment removes an offender from Rucesion soil for eight days ((24 hours)) and should be posted to the Justice Cases Board and a copy thereof sent to the offender. Guards and Guard Captains are also required to send evidence of their cases within one Temuairan day to all Judicial Administrators.

#### 2.2.1) Protocol for Banishment Warrants

A banishment warrant is an instruction to all judicial officials to banish an Aisling on sight. Banishment warrants, in the form of a full report, are placed on the Justice Cases Board when a law enforcement official is unable to execute banishment at that moment.

#### 2.2.2) Protocol for forgiveness of banishment

If evidence and circumstances warrant it, a judicial official may forgive the banishment of an Aisling. Forgiveness of banishment can only take place in the light of evidence of improper banishment and can be warranted only by a Guard Captain, or a Judge.

#### 2.2.3) Protocol for filling Banishment Warrants

After fulfilling a banishment warrant, or banishment request from a board monitoring official, the banishing official is to post a banishment report to the Justice Cases Board and send a copy of the report to the banished Aisling. The report must include a copy of the warrant or banishment request. In the case where a repeated banishment occurs due to an official not posting that they had fulfilled a warrant, the official failing to notify is to receive a demerit as per the IAA (2.7) and also improper banishment, as per the IAA. An Aisling who has been warranted for exile may be banished indefinitely until their exile is enacted.

### 2.3) Protocol for Exile Requests and Warrants

The Court of Rucesion may warrant exiles at its discretion, including in response to exile requests for felonies, if warranted, and capital crimes, or as a judgment from a trial. Such warrants are to indicate the length of the exile. In the absence of a Judge, the responsibility for ordering exile warrants falls to the Burgess or Acting Judge. In the absence of both, the responsibility is relegated to the Deputy Captain with the assistance of the Acting Burgess.

The Legislative Branch is free to execute warranted exiles at will. If a Demagogue cannot be located to fill an exile warrant against an Aisling in Rucesion territory, the offender is to be banished. If an exile was fulfilled before fulfilling an outstanding Banishment Warrant, the banishment warrant will be nulled. Memories are unnecessary in this case.

### 2.4) Protocol for Mandatory Guard Meeting

Guard captains must hold at least one mandatory guard meeting once per term; multiple Guard Captains may fulfil this requirement by collectively organising a meeting between them. No more than one mandatory meeting may be held per double moon. No demerits may be issued to Guard Captains unable to hold mandatory meetings due to there already being more than enough for the duration of their term. Any Guard Captain must give notice on the Justice Case Board of their meeting a moon before the scheduled date. A mail about the meeting is to be sent to all Guard Captains and Guards in office. Deputy Captains may also schedule meetings to organise the guards; however, these meetings cannot be mandatory.

### 2.5) Protocol for Prohibited Trade Case

Prohibited Trade refers to any form of advertisement on Character Sales or Real Money Exchange.

#### 2.5.1) Protocol for Advertisement on Merchant's Board

Officials are to capture the title of the entry in the content page, along with its content. All evidence must be submitted to the Judiciary Administrators.

#### 2.5.2) Protocol for Advertisement using Group Banner

Officials are to capture the the advertisement in the banner and the name of the Aisling ((using mouse-over)), if the Aisling is found in Rucesion Grounds.

#### 2.5.3) Protocol for Advertisement using Local Shout

Officials are to capture the local shout and patrol the vicinity for the victim and capture evidence to prove and/or state the Aisling's present location. ((the command ! used in whispers))

### 2.6) Protocol for Harassment Case

Evidence taken must show the activity of the criminal performing the actions described in MAL 1.5 on an Aisling. Evidence must also include the victim declaring the contact with the perpetrator to be unwanted (and/or asking them to stop communicating with them) as well as adding them on their ignore list ((F9)), along with proof that harassment has continued by communication through mail, world shouts, using siblings to bypass the ignore list, or continuing the use of skills or spells on the victim. If the victim continues contact with an alleged perpetrator of harassment while the perpetrator is on their ignore list, this constitutes harassment of its own and nullifies their initial case. If the crime took place outside the Isles of Dawn, the criminal or the victim must be a Rucesion Citizen.

### 2.7) Protocol for Theft Case (refer to Malicious Acts Law for definition of Theft Case)

A written report is allowed in the event where a theft occurred through advertisement on the Merchant's Board. In the event an item is stolen from below an Aisling, memories should be taken ((with the white text above)), to prove that it is active. The name of the thief is to be shown clearly.

### 2.8) Protocol for Murder Case (refer to Malicious Acts Law for definition of Murder Case)

In the event where the life of a Rucesion Citizen, or any Aisling within the territory of Rucesion, is threatened, evidence should be taken of the Aisling being on the verge of death ((skulling)), along with the murderer. A written document must be submitted to the highest ranking Guard Captain and/or Judge as well as the senior Burgess, along with the evidence, if any. In the event of evidence not being present, an investigation may proceed with the Mundanes with the assistance of a Knight.

## 3) Protocol for the Legislative Branch

This Section refers to the responsibilities of Demagogues and Burgesses. Reporting protocol is governed by the Board Usage Guidelines.

### 3.1) Standard Exiles

A standard exile may last no more than one Deoch per charge.

#### 3.1.1) Exile Procedures

The Demagoguery executes exiles warranted by the Court. After the end of the exile period, a pardon may be executed by the Demagoguery on request of the exile, or at their discretion. A Demagogue who exiles an Aisling without a warrant will face corrective action as per the Integrity Assurance Act.

### 3.2) Pardon Procedures

An official who pardons an exile or lifts a political bar before due date is to receive a demerit as per the Integrity Assurance Act, as well as the official or citizen who assisted the pardon. A Burgess or Judge may issue a new warrant for the remaining days of the original exile, and may issue additional time, up to one Deoch, at their discretion.

### 3.3) Mandatory Legislative Meetings

A Burgess is to organise the Demagoguery and must give notice of the meeting a moon before the scheduled date. The Acting Burgess may also schedule a meeting. Proposals may be discussed during the meeting, but they may also be posted for further commenting and amended accordingly. Other issues and organisational or training matters of the Demagoguery may be discussed as well. The whole of the legislature is asked to attend; a demerit will be given if justification for absence is not received beforehand, when the meeting is deemed mandatory. Only a Burgess may hold mandatory meetings, although there is no requirement for such meetings to be held where there are no law proposals to be discussed. An Acting Burgess may not hold mandatory meetings, but may hold regular meetings.

#### 3.3.1) Law Proposal

Only the Burgess may propose any amendments to the Law, or new legislation. If the office of Burgess is vacant, the Acting Burgess may propose legislation. Legislation must always be presented at a Legislative Meeting, and feedback on it must be received from the entirety of the Legislature before a law is opened for voting.

#### 3.3.2) Voting

To revise, instate, or repeal a law, two-thirds of the votes cast must show support for the proposal. If there are less than three Legislators currently in office, then the Senior Judges or the Acting Judge may also cast a vote. In the instance of multiple proposals, votes must be cast separately for each. Judges are able to cast their votes by proxy (since they cannot post on the Demagoguery Board) or post on the Political Discourse board. No law may be proposed while there are less than three eligible voters (including substitutions by a Judge).

All Legislators and substitutes must cast their vote(s) for the proposal(s) within one moon of the opening. Each official may cast a positive, negative or abstinent/neutral vote. Once a moon has passed, the senior member of the Legislature is to close the vote, count, and post the results to the Demagoguery Board. Dereliction of Duty may be applied to any Rucesion official who was allowed to vote but did not.

#### 3.3.3) Law Posting

Law revisions may pass immediately if the majority of voters have cast their votes, or at least three officials have voted such that the remaining voters would not have altered the outcome of the vote either way. No vote is required to remove a law replaced by a revision. A Burgess or Senior Demagogue is to post the new law, including the date the law was passed and all officials who voted on it.

#### 3.3.4) Amendments to the Charter

Any attempt to amend the Charter of Rucesion follows section 3.3.1 of this law, except as follows: The voting period is extended to a double moon; all Administrative Officials must vote or place notice of abstention on the Political Discourse Board; the proposal must attain 80% approval to be passed. Any Amendment to the Rucesion Charter is to be posted separately from the Charter. The Rucesion Charter may only be removed by 100% approval of all Administrative Officials.

## 4) Protocol for Ranger and Knight Interaction

All officials of Rucesion are required to assist any Knight or Ranger in any way possible, except in the event that the safety and/or integrity of the Commonwealth, its practices, or standards are placed in danger. In such an event, all officials of Rucesion are expected to contact another Ranger or Knight to investigate the situation, and clarify the level of cooperation needed and expected.

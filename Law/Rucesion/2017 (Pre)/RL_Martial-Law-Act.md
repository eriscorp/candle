# Martial Law Act

During times of crisis, Martial Law may be declared by the most senior Burgess in office. In the absence of a Burgess, the Judge or Acting Judge with the assistance of the Acting Burgess is to enact Martial Law. In the absence of an Acting Judge, a vote is to be declared between all higher officials of Rucesion. All eligible officials who are awake must vote. Abstaining is not an option. All must be given the opportunity to vote and their names and votes will be record on the notice of Martial Law. Once approved, Martial Law is immediately in effect. These regulations make the enactment of Martial Law as swift as possible, but also as lawful as is attainable within reasonable boundaries.

## 1) Times of Crisis

- 1.1) Siege of Rucesion's Citizens
- 1.2) Mass summonings of hostile creatures
- 1.3) Riots
- 1.4) Wiping of the Rucesion Law Board
- 1.5) Undefined Times of Crisis

## 2) Declaration of Martial Law and Informing

**2.1)** It is required of Legislators to inform all other Officials, who are not present, that Martial Law has been declared. Legislators must post a notice on the Rucesion Law board that Martial Law is in effect, and remove the notice when Martial Law is no longer in effect.

**2.2)** It is required of all Guard Captains to inform their Guards that Martial Law has been declared and that Guards are to enforce and regulate the city of Rucesion as accorded.

**2.3)** It is required of Guard Captains to organize placement of Guards in defenceless areas.

**2.4)** For the first eight days ((24 hours)) of Martial Law, it is required of the guards placed at the docks to announce that Martial Law is in effect, and that Aislings entering Rucesion must refer to the law. Guard Captains may ask any other official to make this announcement if there is a shortage of Guards.

**2.5)** All Aislings will be held accountable for their actions and can be dealt with according to the terms of Martial Law, whether they state that they knew Martial Law was in effect or not.

## 3) Creation of Law

Demagogues can create and make official laws, which relate to the crisis, as long as they have a majority vote of the Demagogues who are currently in office. This means that a law may pass if there is more than 50% approval. A Burgess can create emergency edicts without a vote. In the absence of a Burgess, this post is taken up by the Judge, but not by an Acting Judge. A law may pass without the one moon grace period. All laws passed are temporary and will be removed at the end of the period of martial law. No law may ever be removed without the two-thirds approval and a one moon waiting period. Special changes may be made to laws, but these are martial law notices and end when martial law ends.

## 4) Drafting of Manpower

If deemed necessary, Rucesion Officials are required to dedicate their labour in order to aid Guards in banishing. In the absence of sufficient officials, Rucesion Citizens may also be drafted.

## 5) Removal of Martial Law

A two-thirds majority vote of Rucesion Administrative Officials is required for the removal of Martial Law earlier than its expiry date of one double-moon. Martial Law requires a vote every double moon for which it is to be extended, and must achieve a two-third-majority vote to remain in place.

_Passed into law this 4th Sun of the 12th Moon of the 113rd Deoch, by Burgess Suspiria and Demagogues Pyprus, Rainwind and OwenQuinn_

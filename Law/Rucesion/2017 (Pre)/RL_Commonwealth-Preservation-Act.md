# Commonwealth Preservation Act

The Commonwealth of Rucesion recognizes Citizenship and Political Office as privileges, and will apply the standards set forth in this Act for the admission of new citizens and current citizens who wish to assume office.

## 1) Eligibility

Any Aisling, with or without jail marks, may be admitted as a citizen of Rucesion. The admitting official must post on the political discourse board (located outside the Rucesion Hall) any new citizens they have admitted. No Aisling may become a citizen if they bear more than three criminal marks as defined by the Standards of Practice. Their last criminal mark must be at least one Deoch old.

### 1.1) Eligibility for Office

All Aislings bearing any criminal marks must apply for Higher Office by contacting a Legislative official, before taking office. No Aisling may bear more than two criminal marks, as defined by the Standards of Practice in order to apply for office. Their last criminal mark must be at least four Deoches old. The office of Respected Citizen is exempt from these restrictions.

#### 1.1.1) Higher Office Applications

An application for higher office must be opened by a legislator. The application is to be posted on the Demagoguery Board as well as the Judgment Board. Voters are to vote on whichever of these two boards they are able to post on. All administrative officials must vote on the application or make a note of abstention; failure to do so will be considered a Dereliction of Duty (as per the IAA). The application must be closed after a moon has passed by the official initiating it, or by any other legislative official if the initiating official is not in office. Failure to close an application before the applicant takes office will be considered a Failure to Notify (as per the IAA).

## 2) Authority

As the Legislature is responsible for maintaining the integrity of the citizenry, Legislators are hereby authorized by this Act to issue banishment and exile warrants for contraventions of this act with a maximum of one Deoch. Judges have discretion on lowering exile periods as seen fit in cases where the illegal citizen/official willingly renounces their citizenship and/or office, and no malicious intent can be established.

## 3) Violations

The following circumstances are considered violations of this act:

### 3.1) Marks prior to Citizenship

Any citizen found to have obtained more than three criminal marks as defined by the Standards of Practice, prior to citizenship on their legend must renounce citizenship immediately, or be exiled for a maximum of one Deoch.

### 3.2) Marks during Citizenship

Any citizen who has obtained more than three criminal marks as defined by the Standards of Practice on their legend must renounce citizenship or be exiled for a maximum of one Deoch.

### 3.3) Marks during or after Office

If an official acquires a criminal mark, as defined by the Standards of Practice, while in office, they are to be removed immediately, and re-apply for any other higher office, if they wish to take office again, conditional to them still being eligible as defined by Section 1.1. Failure to present themselves for removal while they are in office will result in an exile for one Deoch, as well as the other corrective actions that usually accompany removal as per the IAA 3.

## 4) Retroactivity

This law applies to citizens admitted before its last revision, or officials who held office before its latest revision.

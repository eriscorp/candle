# Standards of Practice

The Standards of Practice define many of the terms often encountered within the framework of Rucesion Law, both its political and civil codes. Definitions of terms which are determined to be contested should be added here for future reference as decided by the Legislature.

## Rucesion Legal Lexicon

The Commonwealth of Rucesion recognizes a standardization of legal terminology.

### 1) Rucesion Office Terminology

- Higher Office: Any office other than Respected Citizen.
- Legislature: The body of Demagogues and Burgesses currently in office.
- Legislator: A Demagogue or Burgess currently in office.
- Judiciary: The body of Guards, Guard Captains and Judges currently in office.
- Judicial Official: A Guard, Guard Captain or Judge currently in office.
- Administrative Official: Any official of Rucesion, except a Respected Citizen or Guard.
- Commonwealth of Rucesion: The territory of Rucesion, including the city of Rucesion, and Dubhaim Castle location on the Isle of Dawn.
- Criminal Mark: A mark of exile, jailing (including auto-hunting arrests), or Sgath on an Aisling's legend. Hwarone Arrests are exempt from the scope of this term.
- Demerit: A notification of improper Official Conduct.
- Review Panel: A committee organised by a Burgess, or Acting Burgess, to deliberate on the punishment or review of punishment meted out to officials, or their rehabilitation, as outlined by the Integrity Assurance Act.
- Office Substitution: The event where an otherwise inferior-ranking official assumes the practical function of an office one rank superior to them due to the absence of officials in that office. Therefore, an Acting Burgess is the most senior Demagogue in the absence of a Burgess, an Acting Judge is the most senior Guard Captain in the absence of a Judge, a Deputy Captain is the most senior Guard in the absence of a Guard Captain, or as specified by a Guard Captain in office.

### 2) Political Sponsorship

Sponsorship is a form of agreement between two Aislings necessary for acquiring or maintaining office. A higher official may only sponsor his subordinate, peer or superior within the same branch.

- Respected Citizen: May seek a Guard or Demagogue for sponsorship. It takes 1 sponsorship point to sponsor a Respected Citizen.
- Guard: Takes 5 sponsorship points to sponsor another Guard, and 10 sponsorship points to sponsor a Guard Captain.
- Guard Captain: Takes 1 sponsorship point to sponsor a Guard, 5 sponsorship points to sponsor another Guard Captain, 10 sponsorship points to sponsor a Judge.
- Judge: Takes 1 sponsorship point to sponsor a Guard Captain, 5 sponsorship points to sponsor a Judge.
- Demagogue: Takes 5 sponsorship points to sponsor a Demagogue, and 10 sponsorship points to sponsor a Burgess.
- Burgess: Takes 1 sponsorship point to sponsor a Demagogue, and 5 sponsorship points to sponsor a Burgess.

### 3) Others

- High Clergy: A person who holds the rank of Cleric, Minister, or High Priest in one of Temuair's temples.
- Knights and Rangers: Aislings who serve as a mediator between peers and the Mundanes, enforcing law and strict discipline in all regions of Temuair and Medenia.

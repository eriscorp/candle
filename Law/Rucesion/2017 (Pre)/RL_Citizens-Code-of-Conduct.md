# Citizen's Code of Conduct

Accepting the privilege of citizenship in the Commonwealth of Rucesion is an agreement to abide by the practices and standards of ethics presented not only herein, but also in the entirety of Rucesion Law. The Citizen's Code of Conduct is the basic law governing the inalienable rights of the citizenry, along with the Charter of Rucesion.

## 1) Knowledge of Rucesion

All citizens of Rucesion are obligated to keep themselves informed on the details of Rucesion Law, Officials in office and proper procedural activity. Ignorance of these details or of the proper procedures taken for various activities is not an acceptable excuse for misconduct. In addition, office holders are required to maintain an up-to-date knowledge of all Political boards containing discourse and reports.

## 2) Board Usage

Posting privileges are governed by the Board Usage Guidelines.

## 3) Citizenship or Office

Eligibility for citizenship or office is governed by the Commonwealth Preservation Act. In summary, obtaining four jail marks or more will result in the permanent loss of Rucesion Citizenship. This requirement supersedes any exemption or special circumstance found in the entirety of Rucesion Law. In the event of a contradiction, this clause takes precedence over any and all other circumstances.

## 4) Malicious Acts

Citizens and guests in Rucesion are to respect the rights and freedoms of others or face the consequences in terms of the Malicious Acts Law.

## 5) Mundane Mandate

Punishment for contraventions of the mundane mandate is carried out not only by Rucesion officials in terms of the Malicious Acts Law but by Rangers who may arrest or imprison offenders. Any Aisling receiving six arrests and/or jailing marks may be expelled from Temuair ((account blocked)).

## 6) Charter Amendments

In addition to the rights and privileges granted by the City and Commonwealth Charter of Rucesion, the following unalienable rights are guaranteed to citizens of Rucesion.

### 6.1) Equality

All Aislings will be treated as equals regardless of their chosen Religion, and shall be judged solely on actions deemed to be illegal as outlined by Rucesion Law. No Aisling can or shall be persecuted based on Religious beliefs alone.

### 6.2) Rights of Summoning

All Aislings may summon in the territories of Rucesion, but will be held responsible for the actions of the creature summoned. Any crime committed by a summoned creature will be treated as if the summoner committed the crime (as outlined in the Malicious Acts Law.)

### 6.3) Rights of Summoned Creatures

Summoned creatures have no rights in Rucesion, and may be killed at whim. By this accord, citizens of Rucesion are responsible for their actions in any location, as per the Mileth and Rucesion Jurisdiction Accords.

### 6.4) Rights of Ascension

The city of Rucesion recognizes the laws of Loures. Any Aisling who executes a vulnerable summoner, or an ascender, is released of all liability for damages incurred therein. Death resulting from the attack of a vulnerable Warrior utilising Asgall Faileas will not be prosecuted under Rucesion Law.

### 6.5) Separation Clause

The Commonwealth of Rucesion recognises the sovereignty of the religious temples, and will only intervene in situations at the request of the Higher Clergy of a Temple, or at the request of Clergy in good standing with the Higher Clergy, in the absence of High Clergy. The use of supplications to cause harm or discomfort to Aislings, while in office, will result in a demerit, as per the Integrity Assurance Act.

## 7) Victim

Any citizen of Rucesion who is an alleged victim or offender of a situation through whisper, chat, guild chat and mail is to be dealt with the consequences in terms of the Malicious Acts Law.

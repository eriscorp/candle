# Citizen's Code of Conduct

Accepting the privilege of citizenship in the Commonwealth of Rucesion is an agreement to abide by the practices and standards of ethics presented herein.

## 1) Knowledge of Rucesion

All citizens of Rucesion are obligated to keep themselves informed on the details of Rucesion Law, Officials in office and proper procedural activity. Ignorance of these details or of the proper procedures taken for various activities is not an acceptable excuse for misconduct. In addition, office holders are required to maintain an up-to-date knowledge of all Political boards containing discourse and reports.

## 2) Board Usage

Posting privileges are governed by the Board Usage Guidelines.

## 3) Citizenship, Office, and Aisling Names

Eligibility for citizenship or office, or Aisling Names are governed by the Commonwealth Preservation Act. In summary, those any arrest, jailing, exile and/or sgath marks must apply for both citizenship and office. Taking citizenship or office with any criminal marks and failing to submit an application and receive approval is grounds for immediate exile and/or removal from office. Former Mileth and Hwarone Officials must also apply for office other than Respected Citizen. Obtaining four or more "criminal marks" may result in the permanent loss of Rucesion Citizenship. This requirement supersedes any exemption or special circumstance found in the entirey of Rucesion Law. In the event of a contradiction, this clause takes precedence over any and all other circumstances.

## 4) Heresy Prevention

Speaking heretically is discouraged, but not illegal. Citizens, guests, and visitors to Rucesion are asked to respect those who do not wish to hear heretical speech by moving the conversation to a different locale, or whispering ((or group or guild chat)). Failure to respect the rights of others in Rucesion's heresy free environment will result in harassment prosecution, as per the Malicious Acts Law.

## 6) Malicious Acts

Citizens and guests in Rucesion are to respect the rights and freedoms of others or face the consequences in terms of the Malicious Acts Law.

## 7) Mundane Mandate

Punishment for contraventions of the mundane mandate is carried out not only by Rucesion officials in terms of Section 2.5 of the Malicious Acts Law but by Rangers who may arrest or imprison offenders. Any Aisling receiving six arrest and/or jailing marks may be expelled from Temuair ((account blocked)).

## 8) Charter Amendments

In addition to the rights and privileges granted by the City and Commonwealth Charter of Rucesion, the following unalienable rights are guaranteed to citizens of Rucesion.

### 8.1) Equality

All Aislings will be treated as equals regardless of their chosen Religion, and shall be judged solely on actions deemed to be illegal as outlined by Rucesion Law. No Aisling can or shall be persecuted based on Religious beliefs alone.

### 8.2) Rights of Summoning

All Aislings may summon in the territories of Rucesion, but will be held responsible for the actions of the creature summoned. Any crime committed by a summoned creature will be treated as if the summoner committed the crime (as outlined in the Malicious Acts Law.)

### 8.3) Rights of Summoned Creatures

Summoned creatures have no rights in Rucesion, and may be killed at whim. Any attempt to aid a hostile summoned creature will be treated as outlined in the Malicious Acts Law. By this accord, citizens of Rucesion are responsible for their actions in any location, as per the Mileth and Rucesion Jurisdiction Accords.

### 8.4) Rights of Ascension

The city of Rucesion recognizes the laws of Loures. Any Aisling who executes a vulnerable summoner, or an ascender, is released of all liability for damages incurred therein. Death resulting from the attack of a vulnerable Warrior utilizing Asgall Faileas will not be prosecuted under Rucesion Law.

### 8.5) Separation Clause

The Commonwealth of Rucesion recognizes the sovereignty of the religious temples, and will only intervene in situations at the request of the Higher Clergy of a Temple, or of Clergy in good standing with the Higher Clergy. The use of supplications to cause harm or discomfort to Aislings, while in office, will result in a demerit, as per the Integrity Assurance Act.

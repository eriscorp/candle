# Separation of Powers Act

The Commonwealth of Rucesion recognizes that each office of Rucesion is separate, and is arbitrated by a chain of command. Officials are only permitted to perform the duties of their office granted to them by law. Those who attempt to take more power then their office grants them are to be dealt with in terms of the Integrity Assurance Act.

## 1) Substitution

If an office is vacant or it's inappropriate for the only incumbent to act, the most senior official of the subordinate office may assume some of the responsibilities of that office, except where substitution is precluded by law. For purposes of Demerits, the Official is considered a peer.

### 1.1) Dispute of Substitution

If a substitute must be chosen between two officials of equal terms and they cannot come to an agreement, the official with the least serious disciplinary record will prevail. If the two candidates are equals in this regard, the official whose term began first will be considered the senior officer. Any disputes beyond this will be settled by a Knight or Ranger formerly from Rucesion.

## 2) Subordination

A superior may overrule a judgment made by a subordinate without consultation. Action against a superior may be taken by two subordinates, if the superior has no peer or superior. Decisions by a peer may be overruled by two peers, if they have no superior. Overruling the decision of an unrelated official is only possible when specifically legislated.

## 3) Official Responsibilities and Chain of Command

The government of Rucesion is divided into three categories of Officials. All officials, save Respected Citizen, are split between the Legislative and Judicial branches.

### 3.1) Respected Citizen

Respected Citizens are Aislings who hold positions of integrity among the citizens of the Commonwealth. It is through their influence that new Aislings may become citizens and that tailors can be persuaded to dye garments. As such, an Aisling in this position is expected to act in an appropriate manner, by responsibly performing their sworn civic duties. All other officials also undertake the basic duties of the Respected Citizen. A Respected Citizen is subordinate to all other officials of Rucesion, and may not overrule the decisions of any other officials.

### 3.2) Judicial Branch

#### 3.2.1) Guard

Guards are sworn to serve and protect the citizens of the Commonwealth. With regular patrol, cooperation and communication with their fellow law enforcement officials, the betterment of the Commonwealth is attained. Through intelligent discernment, a Guard's first intention is to settle a dispute, without resorting to punitive action. A Guard is the direct superior of a Respected Citizen and subordinate of a Captain.

#### 3.2.2) Guard Captain

Captains perform investigations into habitual law breakers, maintain the Punishment Reports, and request exiles. They may carry out the duties of a Guard, appoint Deputy Captains and assist Judges. As a team, each Guard Captains must hold one mandatory meeting per term and are expected to attend this meeting just as a guard would. Additional meetings may be called with optional attendance, as seen fit by the Senior Guard Captain. A Captain is the direct superior of a Guard and subordinate of a Judge. When required, the Senior Captain may carry out the duties of a Judge.

#### 3.2.3) Judge

Judges represent the Court of Rucesion and are responsible for the safety of the town and its citizens. Their duties include organizing the Captains, monitoring the Judicial and Judgments boards, holding trials to determine guilt or innocence, reviewing laws, and maintaining the Exile, Pardons due, Probation and Bar List. In the event of the office of Captain being vacant, Judges may appoint Deputy Captains. A Judge is the direct superior of a Captain and may carry out the duties of that office.

### 3.3) Legislative Branch

#### 3.3.1) Demagogue

Demagogues pursue the maintenance of stability in Rucesion through the continual review of legislation. Demagogues must maintain fairness in all their doings and take into consideration the entire population of Rucesion. They must attend Demagogue meetings, assist the Judicial Branch with exiles, maintain the integrity of the citizenry and monitor board usage. A Demagogue is the direct superior of a Respected Citizen and subordinate of a Burgess. If the office of Burgess is vacant, the Senior Demagogue becomes Acting Burgess.

#### 3.3.2) Burgess

The primary duty of the Burgess is to ensure the safety and wellbeing of Rucesion's citizens and officials. Burgesses are responsible for organizing the Demagogues, holding Demagogue meetings, maintaining the integrity of Rucesion Office and acting as an intermediary between Aislings and the Officials. A Burgess is the direct superior of a Demagogue.

### 3.4) Other Officials

#### 3.4.1) Rangers

Rangers, by appointment by the Mundanes, may exercise their authority in the territory of Rucesion without fear of retribution. A Ranger may request an Aisling be banished from Rucesion, so long as proper proof of criminal activity is shown. Rangers are to be considered citizens of the Commonwealth, and may enjoy any privileges a citizen of Rucesion does.

#### 3.4.2) Knights

Knights, by appointment by the Mundanes, may step in and mediate or investigate any situation or incident they feel is necessary. A Knight may request a political action be held for a period of one moon, in the instance that said political action would disrupt or tamper with a Knight's findings in their investigation. Knights may, if they so choose, act as attorneys in the Court of Rucesion, and may also request the banishment of an Aisling, so long as proper proof of criminal activity is shown. Knights are to be considered citizens of the commonwealth, and may enjoy any privileges a citizen of Rucesion does.

# Commonwealth Preservation Act

The Commonwealth of Rucesion recognizes Citizenship and Political Office as privileges, and will apply the standards set forth in this Act for admission of new citizens and current citizens taking office.

## 1) Eligibility

An Aisling bearing a criminal mark may not be admitted to Rucesion without an application and prior approval. Citizenship or higher office may not be taken by any Aisling who bears a criminal mark less than four Deochs old, or bears three or more criminal marks. Any Aisling who takes Rucesion Citizenship while under warrant for, or investigation of, exile or political bar must renounce citizenship and apply per Sections 2 and 3.

Any Aisling taking citizenship or higher office in contravention of this Section is to immediately renounce citizenship, witnessed by an Administrative Official, or face exile. Any official who admits or sponsors such an Aisling is to be dealt with in terms of the Integrity Assurance Act.

## 2) Application

An Aisling meeting the requirements of Section 1 may apply to hold citizenship or higher office. The application is to be lodged with a Legislator, and placed for review within one moon of receipt. See CPA 7.2, Offical Format and Legal Documents

## 3) Voting

The Legislator who receives the application is to ensure that it meets the requirements of this Act before posting it on the Judgments Board, and opening the voting on the application for one moon. Every Administrative Official holding office during that moon is to vote on the application or indicate abstention, or incur a demerit in terms of the Integrity Assurance Act. Applications are which receive two-third majority of the votes, being no less than three votes, are approved. A Legislator is to close the voting, post the result on the Judgments Board, inform the applicant of the outcome and post the approval on the Political Discourse Board.

## 4) Persistence of Preservation

Any citizen who attains more than three criminal marks less than eight Deochs old and who has no record of approval in terms of Sections 2 and 3, or receives an additional criminal mark after receiving approval in terms of Section 3, must submit an application in terms of Section 3. If such application is denied in terms of Section 3, he is immediately to renounce citizenship or face exile.

## 5) Authority

As the Legislature is responsible for maintaining the integrity of the citizenry, Legislators are hereby authorized by this Act to issue banishment warrants for contraventions of this act.

## 6) Inappropriate Names

The Commonwealth of Rucesion maintains the right to preserve its heritage through the regulation of Aisling Names.

### 6.1) Guidelines

An Official must deny admission to an Aisling requesting citizenship if the Aisling's name seems inappropriate. The Aisling may then be offered a vote on his/her name in anticipation of citizenship at a later date.

Inappropriate names include, but are not limited to:

* Names that blatantly refer to a supposed realm beyond this one

((PepsiCola, PlayStation))

* Names deemed offensive for any reason

* Names which form a statement or make a request ((GimmeMuni, MentorMi))

* Names which are unpronounceable in the King's Tongue ((BCdogz, xbobx))

* Names which blatantly refer to places, real or of blatant nature

((Funkytown, StankVille))

* Names comprised of a phrase that is not a valid title

### 6.2) Investigation

A Legislator contacted by a citizen or appointed by the Burgess to carry out an investigation in terms of this Law is responsible for completing it, whether or not his term of office ends, unless another Legislator accepts responsibility for completion and posts notice to that effect on the Demagoguery Board. Failure to comply with this provision will be Dereliction of Duty. In the event that the name blatantly violates the terms listed in section 6.1, a Legislator may post denial of the name, so long as at least three Legislators are currently in office. Any other Legislators may object to this denial within eight Temuairan days. If any objection is posted, the name will be investigated as per section 6.3.

### 6.3) Name Inquiry

Any citizen (with the exception of citizens who have resided in Rucesion for 10 Deochs or more with no criminal record) who is suspected of having a heretical name must be issued a letter which entails a brief explanation of the process, and request that the Aisling give a non-heretical explanation for their name within one moon. The Aisling may also include any information they think is pertinent to proving their worth as a citizen of Rucesion that they feel necessary, and may also obtain letters of reference from other Aislings. The investigating Legislator shall then give notice of having sent the inquiry to the citizen by posting on the Demagoguery Board.

### 6.4) Voting Process

#### 6.4.1) Opening the Vote

Once the application has been received, it is to be posted on the Demagoguery Board, and the voting will be declared open. If the citizen fails to respond to the Legislator that requested the application within one moon, notice to that effect must be placed on the Demagoguery Board, and the voting opened.

#### 6.4.2) Voting

Each Legislator must indicate whether they find the name inappropriate or not (see section 6.1), or indicate abstention. Failure to do so will be considered Dereliction of Duty.

### 6.5) Action

Upon closing the vote, the results of the vote are to be sent to the applicant. The applicant is required to save a memory of the letter, as proof that they have already passed an Inappropriate Name Inquiry. If the applicant was denied, they are to be informed that they have one moon to renounce citizenship in the presence of an Administrative Official, or face banishment.

### 6.6) Appeal

An appeal to the results of an Inappropriate Name Inquiry can be made in writing to any Judge or Knight, after the applicant has renounced Rucesion Citizenship. Upon receipt of an appeal, a Knight or Judge must investigate the application, votes, and results, and determine if alternative actions are necessary. A Judge may void the results of an application. A Knight may suggest to a Judge that an application be voided, but cannot void the application themselves.

### 6.7) Failure to comply

In the event that an applicant fails to renounce citizenship after one moon or retakes citizenship in contravention of this act, a Legislator may warrant the exile of the applicant for one Deoch.

### 6.8) Reporting

It is the joint responsibility of the Legislature to ensure that reports are maintained on the Demagoguery Board indicating every past and current investigation, including details of the investigated name, the findings of the Legislature, the subsequent action, and pertinent dates. This report is to be recreated at least once each quarter, failure to do so constituting Dereliction of Duty.

## 7) Preservation of Office

In order to safe guard the integrity of Rucesion Office, the following procedures are now placed in effect.

### 7.1) Background Check

Legal Documents written by Rucesion Legislators are property of the City of Rucesion, and are the Official Format to be used according to Protocol.

There may be no changes to the Official Formats of either The Legislative Branch nor the Judicial Branch by any single individual. Formats will be created by the Legislators, and voted upon as a proposal of law.

#### 7.1.1) Eligibility

Any Aisling who is currently under warrant for banishment, exile, political bar, or sgath must first satisfy their punishment. If such an Aisling gains a criminal mark due to such action, they must also apply to satisfy the requirements in the Commonwealth Preservation Act.

#### 7.1.2) Application

The application must include a list of offices and terms served in Mileth, the date of the most recent occasion they have held office in Mileth, a short summary of why they feel they should be allowed to assume office in Rucesion, and state which office they seek to obtain. The application may be mailed to any administrative official, and posted on the Judgment Board for review. An administrative official reserves the right to void an application which does not list any corrective actions taken against them.

#### 7.1.3) Voting

Once the Application has been received and posted on the Judgment Board, all administrative officials have one moon in which to vote yes, vote no, or abstain from voting on the application. Any official who fails to vote may be warned for Dereliction of Duty as seen fit by their superior(s) and/or peers.

After one moon, any administrative official may close and tally the vote and place the results on the Political Discourse board. Any application which fails to receive a 2/3rds majority will be denied. Any Aisling who is denied may reapply in four Deochs. The list of Aislings approved or denied by this law will be kept by Demagogues.

#### 7.1.4) Situational Ethics

Due to the vast differences between Mileth Law and Rucesion Law, any Aisling who receives approval to take office must also undergo a test of situational ethics, to be administered by a Judge or Burgess. Failure to pass a test of Situational Ethics will result in denial for office, and the Aisling may reapply in four Deochs. A test of situational ethics must include questions which highlight the core values of Rucesion Office, such as avoidance of personal involvement, cooperation, and teamwork.

### 7.2) Official Format and Legal Documents

Official Formats;

#### Legislative Branch

Citizen Application Format:

This application is used in the occasion an Aisling seeking Rucesion Citizenship bears marks of Jailing or Arrest.

Illegal Citizen Report Format:

This format is used by the Legislators of Rucesion to record and report illegal citizens housing in Rucesion

Inappropriate Name Investigation Format:

This format is used to determine the eligability of an Aislings name for future Citizenship, or to investigate current citizen status.

#### Judicial Branch

Banishment Report Format: This format is used by the Judicial Branch, when administrating banishments in Rucesion.

#### 7.2.1) Violations

Falsifying information on an application, cheating on a situational ethics test, or taking office without the approvals listed in this law will result in instant removal from office and exile. The applicant will have to wait 5 Deochs to reapply.

### 7.3) Recognition of Formality

All Formats, and Applications are Official Documents of The City of Rucesion, the use of these Documents is mandatory. The failure to uphold and maintain the lucrative use of Official Formats and Applications may result in a Demerit for Improper Procedure.

### 7.4) Special Circumstances

Any Aisling who has already attained higher office in Rucesion by the Deoch 50 is exempt from the requirements of this law. In the event of a disputed punitive action in Mileth, an administrative official reserves the right to hold a hearing to determine the appropriateness of the action.

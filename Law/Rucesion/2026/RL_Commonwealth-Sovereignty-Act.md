# Commonwealth Sovereignty Act

> _"Lux in Tenebris, Vires in Lumine"_

The Commonwealth of Rucesion is a sovereign territory, and holds sole authority over the lands it governs. This Act declares the borders of that authority, the obligations it places upon Rucesion's own officials, and the terms upon which Rucesion deals with other territories. Where Rucesion recognizes an authority it did not grant, this Act says so. Where Rucesion holds a power and declines to use it, this Act says that also. Rucesion legislates for Rucesion, and asks the same restraint of others. This Act supersedes the Jurisdiction Accords, which no longer bind the Commonwealth.

## 1) Territories

Rucesion recognizes three classes of territory. Every place in Temuair falls into one of them.

### 1.1) Sovereign Territories

A Sovereign Territory possesses government: a standing body of aisling officials empowered to make, revise and enforce its own law. Rucesion recognizes Mileth and Hwarone as Sovereign Territories, and is itself one. The authority of a Sovereign Territory persists beyond its own borders, as described in Section 2. Citizenship alone does not establish government, and does not qualify a territory for recognition under this section. Recognition of a further Sovereign Territory is extended by treaty under Section 5.

### 1.2) The Borders of Rucesion

The Commonwealth of Rucesion consists of Rucesion Village, Dubhaim Castle, the Mileth Sgath Pit, Gigean's Dwelling, and any territory normally reached only by crossing a Rucesion threshold or by holding Rucesion citizenship. The Shrine of Luathas and the Shrine of Sgrios are excluded, and are Limited Territories under (1.3). Where Rucesion Law refers to the territory, borders or dominion of the Commonwealth, this definition governs.

### 1.3) Limited Territories

A Limited Territory governs its own affairs and may restrict access to the ground it holds. Its authority ends at its own threshold and reaches no aisling beyond it. Rucesion recognizes the Coliseum Arena, the Mileth College, Suomi and the religious temples as Limited Territories. Rucesion officials do not intercede in the affairs of a Limited Territory except at the request of its governing authority, or as Section 2 permits. The recognition of temple sovereignty in the Citizen's Code of Conduct is preserved in full.

### 1.4) Neutral Territory

Neutral Territory is all territory that is neither Sovereign nor Limited. No territory holds authority over Neutral Territory itself. For the purpose of jurisdiction, mail, whispers and worldshouts occur in Neutral Territory. The dojo is Neutral Territory, and is further governed by (3.4).

## 2) Persistence of Authority

The authority of Rucesion follows its citizens and its officials. It does not depend upon the ground they stand on.

### 2.1) Citizens and Guests

A citizen of Rucesion is answerable to Rucesion Law at all times and in every territory. Rucesion may investigate, charge, try and punish either for any act contrary to Rucesion Law, whatever the location of that act. Both carry the protections of Rucesion Law with the same reach. The renunciation of citizenship to escape consequence is governed by the Malicious Acts Law. Where this Act refers to a citizen of Rucesion, it includes a guest, unless the provision states otherwise.

### 2.2) Prior Prosecution

Punishment imposed by another territory or by a Ranger does not bar Rucesion from prosecuting the same act. Rucesion reserves the right to prosecute any violation of Rucesion Law, and the Judiciary weighs each instance on its own merits. Punishment already imposed elsewhere may be weighed in mitigation of sentence.

### 2.3) Office Beyond the Borders

An official of Rucesion holds their office wherever they go, and remains bound by the Official's Code of Conduct and the Official's Protocol Handbook in every territory. An official holds no power to compel any aisling outside the borders of the Commonwealth, and may act beyond them only by investigation, by request, or as a treaty provides. The wearing of uniform is governed by the Official's Protocol Handbook.

## 3) Restraint in Neutral Territory

### 3.1) The Restraint

Rucesion asserts jurisdiction over an aisling in Neutral Territory only where a citizen of Rucesion is an alleged victim or an alleged perpetrator of the act in question. Where a citizen is involved, the jurisdiction of Rucesion extends to every aisling involved in the case, whatever their citizenship.

### 3.2) Citizens of Tagor and the Stateless

This restraint applies without exception to citizens of Tagor and to aislings holding no citizenship. Rucesion prosecutes neither in Neutral Territory where no citizen of Rucesion is involved.

### 3.3) Suspension and Withdrawal

The limitation in this section is self-imposed. The Legislature may suspend or withdraw it, in whole or with respect to any territory, by the ordinary process of legislation. Rucesion keeps it because a power held in reserve serves the peace better than a power spent.

### 3.4) The Dojo

No aisling may be prosecuted by Rucesion for sleephunting within the dojo. This limitation is not within the power of the Legislature to remove. All other acts committed within the dojo remain subject to this Act

## 4) Loures, Tagor, Knights and Rangers

Loures and Tagor are the seats of mundane authority in Temuair. They are neither Sovereign nor Limited Territories, and this Act does not classify them.

### 4.1) Rangers

Rangers hold jurisdiction over every aisling in every territory in the enforcement of the Mundane Mandate. Rucesion officials extend Rangers full cooperation, and place no condition upon the exercise of that authority within the Commonwealth.

### 4.2) Knights

Knights hold such authority within the Commonwealth as the mundanes vest in them, and Rucesion officials extend Knights cooperation as dictated by Rucesion Law.

### 4.3) Existing Provision

The standing of Knights and Rangers within Rucesion government is established by the Separation of Powers Act. The assistance owed to them by Rucesion officials is established by the Official's Protocol Handbook, and their substitution into Rucesion office by the Codes of Practice and the Official's Protocol Handbook.

### 4.4) Rule of Construction

Nothing in this Act limits mundane authority. Where any provision of this Act appears to do so, the mundane authority governs, and the provision has no effect to that extent.

## 5) Treaties

### 5.1) Power to Treat

Rucesion may enter into a treaty with any Sovereign or Limited Territory. A treaty binds the Commonwealth only when passed by the Legislature as per the Official's Protocol Handbook.

### 5.2) Standing of a Treaty

A treaty holds the force of Rucesion Law. Where a treaty conflicts with this Act or with any other law of Rucesion, the law governs and the conflicting term of the treaty holds no effect, unless the Legislature amends that law in the same act by which it passes the treaty.

### 5.3) Subjects of Treaty

A treaty may provide for, among other matters: the execution of warrants that require prior action, such as removal from office; the form and contents of a case file; the retention of evidence and its release upon request; the recognition of a further Sovereign Territory; and terms under which Rucesion forbears from prosecuting an act already punished elsewhere, as reserved by (2.2). Cooperation between Rucesion and another territory is a matter of treaty, and no such cooperation is owed in the absence of one.

### 5.4) Officials of Other Territories in Rucesion

No official of another territory holds authority within the Commonwealth. A request made by such an official is left to the discretion of Rucesion officials, and must accord with Rucesion Law, as provided by the Separation of Powers Act. A treaty may not grant an official of another territory authority over a citizen of Rucesion.

### 5.5) Storage and Display of Treaties

Ratified Treaties must be posted to the Rucesion Law Board with the subject prefix Treaty.
